  `war_current` returned 17 `not_recorded` refusals and four `live_pending`
  responses. Twelve `war_history invalid_tag` errors came from the retired
  elixir-bot test path, not member traffic.
- **Large answers needed priced, tool-specific recovery.** Eleven results
  were truncated: three `battles_query`, two timeline, and one each across
  rankings and analytical reads. The queue did not justify silently dropping
  fields or widening the response cap.

- The meta latency and size arc shipped through the population-table and
  compact answers in 6.12.0, collection slimming in 6.14.0, indexed level-gap
  reads and analytical budgets in 6.19.3, and bounded database reads in

- Timeline paging and repeated-story semantics were replaced by the newsfeed
  model in 7.0.0 and stable story `id` plus `revision` in 9.15.0. The current
  preview surfaces made 867 calls in a day with zero MCP errors.
- War facts were narrowed to what the source can support: weekly aggregates
  replaced unreliable day allocation in 9.0.0 and 9.0.1. The invalid-tag
  consumer was retired on 2026-09-26.
- Result-size findings stayed as contract pressure: compact shapes, pagination,
  response-size notes and acceptance controls were added rather than raising
  the shared cap.

## Ranked next improvements

1. **Keep the analytical tail under observation.** Current meta-card p95 is
   still high even though it completes; require repeated natural failures
   before changing capacity or storage again.
2. **Report natural and adversarial traffic separately.** Preview behavior,
   Gym pressure and acceptance refusals answer different questions and should
   not be blended into one error rate.
3. **Retain the size controls.** A synthetic truncation is useful only while it
   remains a control case with a narrower successful retry.
