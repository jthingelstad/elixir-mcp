## What users and agents told us

- **The Discord previews became a strong natural control.** The three previews
  made 4,512 calls with two errors. Five thumbs-up items filed near the end of
  the week praised the editor's selective posting, channel-context reads and
  one player-summary drill. They are positive regression signals, not requests
  for a new feature.
- **Release validation dominated the measured week.** Acceptance made 17,164
  of 22,430 calls (76.5%) and 848 of 883 errors (96.0%). Those refusals cover a
  week of rapid contract and product-scope changes; they are not the natural
  client failure rate.
- **Right-sizing changed what the usage census means.** Retired ranking,
  Collection, game-wide meta and recommendation tools made 2,884 calls during
  the transition, mostly through release gates before they left the product.
  Historical volume is not evidence to restore them. The retained direction is
  personal and clan recording, notifications, agent access and the web record.
- **Size pressure remained explicit and bounded.** The audit counted 139
  truncated results (0.62%), led by `battles_query` (59),
  `clans_members_timeline` (38), retired `rankings_players` (23) and
  `clans_participation` (19). The response cap and narrower successful reads
  remain the correct behavior.

## Ranked next improvements

1. **Preserve the editor behaviors people praised.** Treat the five positive
   items as regression signals for selective posting, context reads and
   player-specific fact checks; do not turn them into extra routine traffic.
2. **Judge latency after the cutover on current, natural reads.** W40 recorded
   21 `cards_card` query timeouts and a 14.6-second p95, but acceptance dominated
   the sample and the tool's population changed. Require repeated current
   natural evidence before another storage or capacity change.
3. **Keep public copy aligned with the new boundary.** Ordinary member signup
   is public, while collector admission remains governed; integrations no
   longer enroll players or clans for recording. Current docs must say both.
