# Elixir

Elixir keeps a lasting record of your Clash Royale play and the people you
play with. The website, useful emails and optional agent connection make
that record easy to explore and use. Clan leaders get evidence and tools
for their own decisions; people make changes in the game.

Keep the service useful and enjoyable to maintain as a hobby. Record
players because someone wants their history. Preserve uncertainty and
provenance. Avoid autonomous elite populations, game commentary, gameplay
recommendations and replacement player scores.

The [product guide](apps/site/src/docs/about.md) describes the experience;
[DECISIONS](docs/DECISIONS.md) holds the standing boundaries. This page is
the shared direction, not a second behavior specification.

Current release priorities (Jamie, October 3, 2026):

1. Clan leadership ready for Monday: verified access, trustworthy policy
   evidence, atomic human actions and useful editable messages.
2. Ladder working well, with immediate orientation: someone opening Elixir
   understands its purpose and the next step for their own play or clan.
3. Invitation readiness: check the complete first-use journey before
   spreading the word. Access remains the approved gated, free beta.

Design and orientation are part of readiness. Keep Drop and the Collector
working. Database downsizing is optional and waits a couple of weeks for
representative post-cleanup evidence; it is not on the current release path.
