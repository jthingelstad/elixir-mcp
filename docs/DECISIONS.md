# Decisions

What stands, one line each. The code and the public docs
(`apps/site/src/docs/`) describe behaviour; this file records the choices
that constrain it. The reasoning behind a line is in git history
(`git log -S "<words from the line>" -- docs/`). A change is a note in
`docs/notes/` plus an edit here, written as the current rule, never as
"was X, now Y".

Every line ends with who decided it:

- **[Jamie, date]** is Jamie's decision. Do not re-litigate it; a
  surface that disagrees with it is the defect, and a conflict you find
  is a question for Jamie.
- **[engineering, date]** is a default an agent chose. Follow it, and
  propose a change with evidence (a note and an edit, in a pull request)
  when it gets in the way.
- **[Jamie, date; engineering: …]** is Jamie's decision with agent
  detail; the part after "engineering" may be revisited like any
  engineering line.

A line says no more than the decision behind it. A guide, skill, page,
comment or test that needs the rule names the line's heading rather than
restating it in wider words.

## Scope and footprint

- **Elixir is the recorder** — a Clash Royale recorder for a player, their friends and their clan: explored on the website, delivered in record-driven mail, and connected to a person's own agent over MCP as an extension. A feature must serve that circle without an autonomous elite population or game commentary; the top of the ladder and esports are out of scope. [Jamie, 2026-09-23, 2026-10-02]
- **No recommendations from Elixir** — Elixir's own outputs (tools, timeline, mail, Ladder and the site) give no deck, upgrade or matchup advice and carry no recommendation scoring, priors or candidate generation, over the whole game or any smaller population (friends, clans). Agents (a person's own, and the family's Discord agent) may coach and recommend from Elixir's facts; the suggestion is theirs, never presented as Elixir's. [Jamie, 2026-10-02, 2026-10-11]
- **No game-wide statistics** — no corpus card or deck performance, meta tools, global comparisons or population rollups. The card catalog and art, canonical deck identity, archetype labels, the factual event calendar and scoped personal and clan history stay. [Jamie, 2026-10-02]
- **No leaderboard capture or autonomous elite recording** — board endpoints are retired at plan, lease, submit and ingest (`RETIRED_RECORDING_ENDPOINTS`); a recorded player's own profile still supplies their rank. [Jamie, 2026-10-02]
- **No named recording Collections** — followed players are organized as primary, alt, friend or watching; no replacement grouping, curated creator catalog, always-on recording or popularity quota exemption. A player's owned-card collection is a game fact and stays. [Jamie, 2026-10-02]
- **History is kept for what someone tracks, and a year after** — a roster poll of a clan nobody tracks keeps only the tracked players on it (membership, role, roster row, the clan's name) and writes no clan day row; a retained battle keeps every participant. A subject nobody tracks is trimmed one year after its last tracker leaves (its own series: profile days, roster rows, its battle log; a battle with a still-tracked participant stays with every participant's rollup) and a later track starts fresh. The maintainer's own `ops` recording reason is retired: only people's tracking records. [Jamie, 2026-10-06, 2026-10-07, 2026-10-12; open #528]
- **Elixir rates no player** — no branded or invented player score or rating (Pilot Score, "Elixir Lift"), residual, similarity, playstyle or adoption-cost number. Plain derived facts (rates, counts, differences and their direction) are what a recorder serves. [Jamie, 2026-09-19, 2026-10-11]
- **Every aggregate ships its control** — the control sits next to the number, in the service; a note fires on a detected confound, never as boilerplate. [Jamie, 2026-09-18]
- **Elixir names decks, never judges them** — an archetype label is vocabulary, not a verdict; null is the correct answer for a card no public source attests; nothing clan-specific enters the vocabulary. [Jamie, 2026-09-20]
- **Elixir is the system of record for game facts** — only collectors write the game record; what family apps attest is held apart and labelled (Data model). [Jamie, 2026-09-04, 2026-09-25]
- **Orientation is release readiness** — a person opening Elixir understands that it is a recorder and sees a clear next step. [Jamie, 2026-10-03]
- **No blockchain, POAP or ENS; no Supercell marks** — the unofficiality disclaimer is on every user-visible surface, tool metadata included. [engineering, 2026-09-03]

## Recording, collectors and rate budget

- **One global rate budget** — the collector fleet is redundancy, never quota; no pooling across collectors; the ceiling does not move. The tick charges only rows it inserts and plans inside capacity less queued bulk; every live mint takes a token or queues nothing. [Jamie, 2026-09-28; engineering: the tick's charging and the live-token rule]
- **Only collectors call the CR API** — the token lives on allowlisted operator machines, never in CI, a Lambda or a browser. [engineering, 2026-09-03]
- **Zero-trust collectors, open enrollment** — no AWS access; Bearer `config`/`lease`/`submit`; the server chooses all work; provisioning is reveal-and-copy of a one-time token; whoever raises a hand is approved, and the admission checks are the trust (no shadow lane first). [Jamie, 2026-09-06, 2026-10-12; open #540]
- **Running a collector earns the Family tier, and nothing else** — fetch points, call credits and the bonus player and clan slots retire. [Jamie, 2026-10-12; open #539]
- **The collector is Go only** — a Python version still parses so the minimum can refuse it, and the 426 hint points to the Go installer. [Jamie, 2026-09-26]
- **Releases are signed candidates until named** — naming promotes to Latest after the hub verifies url, sha256 and signature against `COLLECTOR_RELEASE_KEYS`; unsigned releases are refused. There is no version pin, even for a canary; rollback is naming the previous release. Raising `min_client_version` is Jamie's call; it never refuses config and fails open on an unparseable version. [Jamie, 2026-09-06, 2026-09-25, 2026-09-26]
- **A stale client is fixed by updating the client** — never by restoring a retired field on the hub. [Jamie, 2026-09-26]
- **The signed badge is self-reported** — `signed`, `dev_build`, `unverified` or `mismatch` against every release ever named; public badge, admin/operator-only hash; nothing gates on it. [Jamie, 2026-09-26]
- **Which collectors run is answered by the hub** — Admin ▸ Collectors, `elixir_collectors` and `/api/public/status`, never repo docs. [Jamie, 2026-09-26]
- **The session clock is the player schedule** — follow up 30 minutes after a productive read, doubling after empty ones, 2-hour ceiling (jitter included); profiles daily and after a session. [Jamie, 2026-09-19]
- **A failed fetch is retried in minutes** — 15, 30, then 60 (`poll_state.retry_at`), planned and charged like any plan; freshness moves only on admission. [engineering, 2026-09-27]
- **A 404 holds a subject for a day** — except `currentriverrace` within six hours of the clan's last admitted race, which keeps its cadence through the season roll. [engineering, 2026-10-05]
- **A race in matchmaking is no race yet** — its receipt is `matchmaking`, neither admitted nor rejected: nothing projected, freshness holds, never charged to a collector, never a missed race; every surface answers it as no race yet, never as an error. [Jamie, 2026-10-08; engineering: its receipt, freshness and charging]
- **Roster `lastSeen` never gates battle logs** — it is a profile-efficiency signal only. [Jamie, 2026-09-13]
- **`live_fetch` only fetches** — Jamie 2026-10-08: "live_fetch should ONLY live fetch and not record data." Its job is fetch-only (`job.record = false`): the payload reaches the caller from `live_fetch_result` (an hour at most) and never the record, receipts or archive; a recording ask turns an open fetch-only job into a recording one, never the reverse. `live: true` on a recorded tool, Verify and the first read on add record. [Jamie, 2026-10-08; engineering: which reads record (live on a recorded tool, Verify, the first read on add)]
- **Never manufacture a request to diagnose** — wait for the natural wave; errors never advance freshness. [engineering, 2026-09-14]
- **Ingest never pauses on catalog integrity** — an unseen card is stubbed and healed. [Jamie, 2026-09-15]
- **The retained record's archive is write-once** — `payloads/` is content-addressed (`If-None-Match: *`) and the rebuild source; every field has a manifest disposition. History leaves only through a reviewed, Jamie-approved manifest that also deletes the selected object versions. [Jamie, 2026-09-11, 2026-09-22, 2026-09-25, 2026-10-02]
- **Backfills and imports** — each gets its own revocable gateway row; a source database is opened read-only; the recorder wins on overlap; an approximated stamp never replaces a real observation. [engineering, 2026-09-15, 2026-09-18]
- **Collector points rewarded `new_facts > 0`, and the economy retires** — `yield_24h` stays as a health figure; points, credits and the Sunday mail's points lines go with the points economy (Family tier is the reward). [Jamie, 2026-09-06, 2026-10-12; open #539]

## Data model and semantics

- **Tags are the only game IDs** — no surrogate keys; `claim` is the only join between accounts and game data; a claim unlocks history, it never migrates it. [Jamie, 2026-09-03]
- **Store UTC** — every connection's session zone is UTC; time zones are display only. [Jamie, 2026-09-03, 2026-09-23]
- **The game day is the 10:00Z grid** — daily series and `days: N` resolve on it; the war clock follows the policy grid for every clan, with the observed offset reported raw. [Jamie, 2026-09-07, 2026-09-19]
- **Mode discipline** — modes are different games: a rate with no mode carries its per-mode-group split and a note, and `mode: "event"` pools events with a note. A clanmate battle is `casual` even when tagged; an untagged `unknown` is `casual`, a tagged one is event content. [Jamie, 2026-09-22, 2026-09-25, 2026-09-28]
- **`event_tag` is the discriminator** — a null tag is a permanent format grouped by `type`; a tagged battle belongs to its event; never pool across them, and never invent groupings for `trail`. [Jamie, 2026-09-22]
- **Absence needs proof** — a missing capture, observation or profile clan is unknown, never zero or "no clan"; quiet days count only where complete reads bracket them. [engineering, 2026-10-08]
- **Battle-stub identities are not the recorded population** — headline and population counts (badge populations included) count recorded players: direct, or members of a comprehensively recorded clan. Every retained battle still keeps factual rollups for all participants, so a later-tracked opponent's history is consistent. [Jamie, 2026-09-23, 2026-09-25, 2026-10-07]
- **A boat defense is not the member's battle** — it leaves every count of a member's own battles; a boat attack stays. [Jamie, 2026-09-24, 2026-09-25]
- **A duel counts as its rounds** — each round is one game with its own tower-less deck and result; `battles_decks` lists round decks apart. [Jamie, 2026-09-26]
- **War facts are weekly aggregates** — no surface splits a member's week by war day; judgments read the race's weekly `decksUsed` against four a war day up to the finish; the day in progress is the game's own `decksUsedToday`; training days are never attendance. [Jamie, 2026-09-24, 2026-09-25]
- **A week's donations are the highest counter seen in its game days** — never pin the reset minute. [Jamie, 2026-09-23]
- **A clan's profile aggregates carry each member's latest profile forward** — `members_profile_carried` says how many. [Jamie, 2026-09-23]
- **Historical clan presence reads historical membership** — at the window's end for summaries, at the moment's own time for moments. [Jamie, 2026-10-02]
- **Duration is a proven bound, never a timing** — both-negative trophy changes are a Path of Legends draw. [engineering, 2026-09-22, 2026-09-23]
- **Labels go beside identifiers** — never instead; card levels on the 1-16 display scale (`displayLevel`); evolution forms never merge (`form: base|evolution|hero`). [Jamie, 2026-09-03, 2026-09-19, 2026-09-25]
- **Archetype grammar in code, vocabulary in data** — the grammar is in `packages/contracts`; the vocabulary is in cr-agent-api-docs, kept by the Clash Royale Analyst; Elixir never merges candidates. Win condition, bait/bridge and `names_deck` roles; CYCLE_MAX is 3.4; a new card triggers review. [Jamie, 2026-09-20; engineering: grammar in contracts, vocabulary in cr-agent-api-docs]
- **Shared facts have one derivation** — the website, mail, MCP, the JSON API and Clan read the same functions over the same recorded facts. [Jamie, 2026-09-19, 2026-09-25, 2026-10-02]
- **Game facts and clan decisions stay distinguishable** — the recorder reports facts (`member_left` stays raw); policy evaluations and human decisions keep their provenance and visibility. [Jamie, 2026-09-13, 2026-10-02]
- **Attested facts** — family apps record what a person did in a clan, and an app's own computed results (`award_standing`, for the season running only: a closed season's result is `award_granted`), apart from the game record, labelled with the app, attesting verified player, role and time. Visibility is the type's rule at read time: clan facts to verified members (and their agents); an away only to that clan's verified leaders; a player fact to whoever has the player on their timeline. [Jamie, 2026-09-25; engineering: award_standing for the running season only, 2026-10-11]
- **A kick is "was removed", never "left"** — the roster's departure says the member departed (it cannot tell a leave from a kick); a leader's classification says removed or left on their own; the same words wherever the timeline is told: MCP, console, mail, Discord (2026-10-10, Jamie). [Jamie, 2026-10-10]
- **Timeline shape** — a notification feed per subject: battle sessions, never individual battles; the clock is the agent's (`game_clock`), not the feed's. [Jamie, 2026-09-13]
- **The timeline is a newsfeed** — newest first everywhere (`elixir_timeline`, console, mail); past the cap keep the newest and count the rest; `next_cursor` and `mark_read` go to the window's end. [Jamie, 2026-09-23]
- **The timeline is the spine of delivery** — everything Elixir tells a person (console, mail, Discord, an agent) is a timeline item or a digest of items, so one derivation feeds every surface. [Jamie, 2026-10-12; open #536]
- **An empty poll is nearly free, by opt-in** — `elixir_timeline` `skip_empty` answers a window with no item from one check over every item source, never the default; the check may say yes wrongly but never no (a missed source is a lost post), so a new item kind joins `NEWS_SOURCES` with its seed in the sweep test. [Jamie, 2026-10-10; engineering: opt-in, never the default, and the sweep test]
- **A timeline item is publication-grade** — its `text` is a time-free sentence about its subjects in their in-game names (time is `at_unix`/`local`, a nickname its own field); one story is one item with every subject in `subjects`; each item says `visibility` and `publishable`, and nothing of Elixir's posts an item that is not publishable; an ad hoc read selects what happened in its window (`basis` occurred), a poller what the record learned (observed, an earlier story marked `late`); each kind's facts are versioned (`schema`) (2026-10-11, Jamie's brief, #373-#376). [Jamie, 2026-10-11]
- **The record is the trigger** — a moment names the battle that caused it and how; absence over a guess; replays and backfills write rows, never moments. [Jamie, 2026-09-15, 2026-09-16, 2026-09-17]
- **A timeline can be cross-posted to Discord, unfiltered** — the owner turns it on in the console (never MCP or `/api/v1`) for their own timeline or an agent's; event driven from admissions with its own pointer, no backfill; one line per item with unpreviewed links back and no time of its own unless the item is over three hours old, then how long ago (2026-10-11, Jamie), edited in place as it grows; a sitting posted while still going is closed by the one timer the cross-post has, a five-minute rule that wakes only accounts whose open sitting has had no battle for 31 minutes to edit that message to its final count; the wake is the account's ordinary sync, so news a delayed admission left unsynced posts in the same run, and the timer starts no story of its own (2026-10-11, Jamie: "keep the 5-minute session-close timer"); leaders-only items and the account's own administration never post; the webhook is sealed and the relay is Clan's (a post whose outcome is unknown is never made again; a line deleted in the channel stays deleted); a webhook Discord calls gone turns it off (2026-10-10, Jamie). [Jamie, 2026-10-10; engineering: owner and Console only, no backfill, account lines left out, sealing and the delivery rules]
- **Clan's Actions may mention one Discord role, opt in, on new posts only** — a leader or co-leader may name one role (by its id) that each Action's first post mentions (`allowed_mentions: { roles: [id] }` and the role in the text); an edit never mentions anyone, and every other Discord path keeps `parse: []`. Off unless set. Actions go to Discord in Action number order, one request of at most ten a clan at a time on a queue of their own, and a forum channel's webhook makes each Action a post of its own (2026-10-11, Jamie via the delivery plan). [Jamie, 2026-10-11]
- **Discord delivery skips a refused line, paces each channel and says what Discord said** — on the timeline cross-post and a clan's activity channel a 400 skips that line alone (the rest are delivered) and its page shows Discord's words; one webhook's lines go at most 25 a minute across runs, a wait goes back to the queue as the record's visibility, and the last receive records what is left as not delivered; a webhook may name one thread (`?thread_id=`), and a forum channel's webhook, which takes no plain message, is refused at save and turns the posting off (2026-10-11, Jamie via the delivery plan). [Jamie, 2026-10-11; engineering: the pace, the last receive, refusing a forum webhook at save]
- **Timeline evidence is a read-only drill-down** — exact ordered constituent games with honest completeness; a crossing links a game only when proved; late proof keeps the original moment identity. [Jamie, 2026-10-04]
- **Verify is one battle** — a battle in the player's own log after the brief with exactly the eight target cards; the target is one of their own Trophy Road decks with two cards swapped; the proof may be in any mode. [Jamie, 2026-09-12, 2026-09-13]

## MCP contract and versioning

- **`packages/contracts` is the single source** — tool schemas, the error enum, `deck_hash` and the meta envelope; additive is a minor, a behaviour correction a patch, and an output-schema change a patch that moves the fingerprint. [engineering, 2026-09-10, 2026-09-17]
- **MCP majors track domain shifts** — an agent reasons from the current declaration; removing an unreliable field is a patch; a major is for a domain-model change that alters what a task means. [Jamie, 2026-09-25]
- **No deprecation window** — every client, first-party or an outside agent, reasons from the current declaration; `elixir_changelog(since)` is the notice; no window is promised while Elixir is small. [Jamie, 2026-09-19, 2026-09-25, 2026-10-12]
- **Omit the tag to mean yourself** — the brief names the caller; `game_clock` is subject-free. [Jamie, 2026-09-08]
- **Refuse rather than choose** — with no `clan_tag`, use the primary player's current clan or refuse (`no_subject`), never an alt's, a friend's or a tracked clan. [engineering, 2026-09-19, 2026-09-25]
- **Name the recorded population** — segment tools require `segment`: `"mine"` or exactly one player or clan; corpus and collection selectors refuse. [Jamie, 2026-10-02]
- **Windows** — unbounded is anchored at the first recorded battle; a date-only `to` covers the whole day; `applied.window` echoes season and `crosses`; no blanket refusal for a missing mode or an unbounded window. [Jamie, 2026-09-23; engineering: the first-battle anchor, a date-only `to`, applied.window]
- **One name, one meaning** — `day` not `date`; `arena` is `{id, name}`; snake_case; `clan_war_trophies` is war trophies and `clan_score` is only the clan profile score; retired names are banned from declarations and current docs by a test. [Jamie, 2026-09-25; engineering: the naming conventions and the retired-names test]
- **Meaning rides on the value** — caveats sit on the field as enums explained once in a note; guards go beside an ambiguous field, never by renaming it. [Jamie, 2026-09-19, 2026-09-23]
- **Stated conventions are schema** — `verbosity`, `days`/`weeks` and `season` are accepted wherever the instructions say; no new `include_*` flags (`battles_query.include_total` stays). [engineering, 2026-09-15, 2026-09-18, 2026-09-20, 2026-09-25]
- **Every tool publishes an outputSchema** — required fields at the top level, every leaf nullable, on `tools/list` for every principal. [engineering, 2026-09-21]
- **Booleans are always emitted** — the clipped bucket's `partial` mark (with `covers`) is the one exception. [engineering, 2026-09-21, 2026-09-25]
- **Descriptions are capped at 600 characters** — overflow goes to the docs page; every error carries a `class`; retry data is structured (`retry_after_s`). [engineering, 2026-09-19]
- **Read-only tools race a deadline; writes never do** — a read answers `query_timeout` with a request id and waits at most 5 s for a lock, on every door (MCP, Explore, `/api/v1`); each function's `statement_timeout` is a ceiling just under its Lambda kill; a priced `result_too_large` is an answer. [engineering, 2026-09-19, 2026-09-21, 2026-09-27]
- **`battles_decks` is a light list with one deck in full** — one-line `card_names` and `archetype_label`, paged by `offset`/`next_offset`; `deck_hash` returns one deck in full. [Jamie, 2026-09-26]
- **Size per door** — `clans_roster`'s per-member extras appear only where no result cap applies (`/api/v1`, Explore); `clans_participation` over MCP is a lossless table that keeps an eight-week, 50-member read under 40,000 characters. [Jamie, 2026-09-28; engineering: the roster's per-member extras, 2026-09-29]

## JSON API and integrations

- **`/api/v1` is a public, versioned product beside MCP** — people call it with OAuth (audience `/api/v1`), integrations with a key; a token for one door is never accepted at the other. [Jamie, 2026-09-23]
- **The JSON API keeps ordinary semver** — a removed or renamed field is a major of its own `info.version`; the path stays `/api/v1` because it is the OAuth audience; a test pins its version to the shape of every tool it mirrors. [Jamie, 2026-09-25]
- **Programs read the JSON API; agents read MCP** — Drop and a clan's website use `/api/v1`; the Discord agent stays on MCP unpinned and reads a missing field as unavailable, never 0. [Jamie, 2026-09-25]
- **Integrations are admin-provisioned** — platform access an admin issues, not a tier entitlement; the agent may provision the family's own; `clans:read`, `mail:send` and `facts:write` are granted only by name. [Jamie, 2026-09-25]
- **`clans:read` reads any recorded clan** — the participation, roster and war-history reads a person's grant has, so an app can work with nobody signed in; no person's token is stored for it. [Jamie, 2026-09-25]
- **Family OAuth clients are provisioned and confidential** — the `family_clients` op provisions them; registration refuses a family redirect; `client_secret_post` with the secret minted on the app's host; first-party means provisioned, and first-party clients are unmetered. [Jamie, 2026-09-27]
- **Drop's sign-in with Elixir stays** — its OAuth client, consent, account authentication and authorized API access are in scope. [Jamie, 2026-10-02]
- **No OIDC layer** — family apps sign in with Elixir's OAuth; no OpenID Connect issuer or separate SSO. [Jamie, 2026-09-28]

## Accounts, principals and privacy

- **Free public signup; collector admission stays governed** — email verification creates an ordinary member account; signup never grants a privileged tier, collector token or extra capacity, and never reopens a denied or disabled account. Launch is free, and a paid tier needs Supercell's express approval. [Jamie, 2026-10-06]
- **Email is the identity; Discord may become a way in** — no password, a code or link signs in; a Discord sign-in, when built, signs in to the same email-identified account, never a second identity. [Jamie, 2026-10-12; open #531]
- **Roles never gate visibility, and a tier is earned in the game** — tiers differ only in slots and call volume; 50 player slots at every tier; Member is signup, Leader comes with being a verified leader or co-leader of a clan you track, Family with running a collector, and Partner (full access to the API surface) stays granted by Jamie and may be removed if it confuses the page; `contracts/roles.ts` is the one source. [Jamie, 2026-09-05, 2026-09-08, 2026-10-12; open #530]
- **Principals: users, agents, integrations** — an agent's tier is capped at its owner's; `on_behalf_of` grants nothing (explicit tag > on_behalf_of > primary); no `act_as`. [Jamie, 2026-09-08]
- **Agents are never deleted** — suspend is the reversible stop and revoke the irreversible one; a suspended agent can be removed from view (out of the switcher and the Agents table, into a folded list), keeping its address, keys and history, and restored (Jamie, 2026-10-10). [Jamie, 2026-10-10]
- **The hour is the principal's, the day is the payer's** — Jamie 2026-10-08: "yes, seperate limits." The MCP hourly bucket keys on the principal that made the call (each agent its own, at its owner's tier rate); the daily tool-call quota and the live lane stay the owner's. A key with its own ceiling keeps its own bucket. [Jamie, 2026-10-08]
- **Agents track in the person's pooled slots** — one pool across the person and every active agent they own (a suspended agent's subjects take no slot and are no reason to record; resuming is refused past a ceiling: Jamie 2026-10-10, "Only active ones should count against usage slots."); a subject counts once, a clan at its widest scope; agents track over MCP as watching only, never "me"; an agent keeps the clan it acts for, and its owner can re-point it. [Jamie, 2026-09-23, 2026-10-10]
- **Primary player's clan is followed automatically** — Jamie 2026-10-08: "it should follow it automatically for the primary player assuming they have a clan set (not all players are in a clan)." Activity scope, primary only, only on an admitted profile's clan; never past a slot or in place of a person's own follow, never a clan they stopped tracking; the follow moves with the player; the approved people made before 0205 were switched on by 0206 (Jamie 2026-10-08: "Approved: backfill auto_follow_clan for the 35 existing elixir accounts"). [Jamie, 2026-10-08]
- **Removing your primary is refused while you track others** — choose the new primary first; removing your last player is allowed. [Jamie, 2026-09-25]
- **Agent-only tools** — `elixir_identify`, `elixir_my_identities` and `clans_context` are hidden from people and integrations; a personal connection ignores `on_behalf_of`. [Jamie, 2026-09-25, 2026-10-04]
- **Accounts read the record; one battle page is public** — all recorded game data is readable by every account, and account data stays private; a single battle's page is the one page of the record open with no sign-in (`/data/now` is service status, not the record). [Jamie, 2026-09-05, 2026-10-12; open #526]
- **CR tags are not PII; email is** — a tag is public game data like the rest of the record, so an issue, a note, an analytics path or a model request may carry one; masking a tag or a record id in analytics is report hygiene, never a privacy rule. Account data (claims, nicknames, connections, usage) is the account's. cr-agent-api-docs leaves real tags out as a public reference's tidiness. [Jamie, 2026-09-08, 2026-10-11]
- **Removing an account deletes the account, not the record** — on request, claims, nicknames, agents, connections, mail and usage go; the players' game history is public game data and stays until the untracked trim reaches it.
