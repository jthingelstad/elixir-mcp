# 2026-10-21 — Door pools keep an idle connection 120 s, not 240 s

#622 raised the door pools' idle timeout from 30 s to 240 s. At 01:33 CDT
the same morning RDS reported the micro "critically low on memory" and
lowered `shared_buffers` from 23,081 to 11,295 pages for the next restart
(#630). This weighs what the longer idle bought against what it held.

**How it was measured.** A week of real requests (2,833 web-api reads
that took a lease, 2,539 MCP tool calls, and the collector's leases),
replayed per environment through pg-pool's own rules: LIFO reuse, the
idle timer, `min`, the 300 s lifetime and each door's `max`. At 30 s the
replay gives web-api 61.6% fresh connections against 61.8% observed. For
06:16 to 06:52 UTC after the deploy it gives 3.73 backends on average
(max 6) against 3.86 (max 6) in Enhanced Monitoring. So the extra
backends are the pool's own idle ones, not leaked connections.

**What it buys.** Fresh connections (about 50 ms each, TLS and auth)
avoided against 30 s:

| idle | week, web-api + MCP fresh | share of 240 s's saving | extra backends, week avg (web-api + MCP) | quiet half-day avg, all doors | under acceptance load, avg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30 s | 2,665 of 5,372 | 0% | 0 | 1.21 | 2.65 |
| 60 s | 2,375 | 51% | +0.12 | 1.32 | 2.93 |
| 120 s | 2,195 | 82% | +0.34 | 1.54 | 3.31 |
| 240 s | 2,091 | 100% | +0.72 | 1.93 | 3.82 |

The saving is web-api's: its fresh share falls from 62% to about 45%,
which takes its `connect_ms` p50 from 33 ms to 0. MCP's barely moves (919
to 899 a week), and the collector was already warm (1.3% fresh).
Environments churn (703 web-api and 361 MCP log streams in the week), and
a new environment always connects, which caps what any timeout can buy.
`min: 1` with a short idle was tried in the replay and dropped: the one
kept connection lives its whole 300 s, so it held nearly as much as
240 s.

**What it costs.** About 6 to 7 MB per idle backend on this instance:
Enhanced Monitoring's free plus cached fell 16 MB for 2.4 more backends.
Swap follows the heavy reads of acceptance runs, not the timeout: at 30 s
idle the 05:50 UTC acceptance run swapped at about 2,200 (in plus out)
with 2.6 backends, and the runs after #622 at 2,300 to 2,700 with 3.5 to
4.3. The longer idle adds about one backend under that load.

120 s keeps 82% of the week's saving (89% on the quiet half-day) for
about half of 240 s's idle backends. Pool `max` stays: bursts are what
need it, and the replay's 99th percentile is set by them.
