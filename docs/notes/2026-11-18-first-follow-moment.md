# One item after following

After a successful People you know selection, show one recorded Timeline item from that selection. Open Timeline stays available while the item loads and on an empty or failed read. Unticked suggestions remain available. A new visit starts from the usual starter offer.

Use the seven-day Timeline window. Only current friend follows in the reader's Timeline qualify. The named read checks post visibility, blocks in either direction and whether the item can receive a cheer. The member uses the same item ID, reaction mutation and counts as on the Timeline. Confirmation creates no reaction or notification on its own.
