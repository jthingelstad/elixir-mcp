# 2026-11-12 — One email: the digest merges the clan and the people you follow

Jamie, 2026-11-12: "One digest email and merging clan and those you
follow is really needed since it will be common for someone to follow
all members of their clan. However, leader action emails should be
separate and on-demand when there is an action for them to perform."

## The reading

- **One scheduled mail.** The digest (`tracking_report`) keeps its
  cadence (weekly default, daily, off). The Monday `clan_report` stops,
  and so do `arena_week` (Tuesday) and `milestone` (hourly). The clan
  report is the ruling's own words; Arena week and the milestone note
  are the reading of "one digest email" (DECISIONS "The digest is the
  one scheduled mail; leaders' Actions mail stays apart", tagged
  [engineering]).
- **Each player once.** People are told under themselves only. A
  followed clanmate carries `clanmate: {tag, name}` and renders "In
  <clan>". A clan's part (`clanNews`) holds only the clan's own news: a
  finished race (place and fame, Colosseum named), joins, departures and
  role changes. A clan's battle count and gone-quiet list are members'
  play and are left out. A person's `clan_joined`/`clan_left` item whose
  clan has a part is told there, not again under the person.
- **Your part** adds your progress line and each alt's firsts and
  progress (what Arena week told about alts).
- **Left out of the digest** (they live on Clan and Stats): the war
  decks-used-of-possible trend, per-member performance, the Arena form
  line, opponents and decks.

## Who receives what

- **No one gets more mail than before** (`foldedOff` in
  `services/jobs/src/email/index.mjs`). A reader whose `clan_report`
  pref was off never gets a digest for clan news alone; one whose
  `arena_week` pref was off gets alts as a name and battle count, as the
  digest drew them before. Digest off stays off (`loadRecipients`).
- **Clan news alone** sends a weekly digest (the clan report did the
  same weekly). A daily reader gets a clan-only digest only on a day a
  race finished, so roster moves never become a daily mail.
- **A tracked clan with Notifications off** is outside `subjectsFor`, so
  its news no longer reaches that reader by mail; the clan report went
  to every tracker. This is fewer mails, never more, but it is a change
  in who receives a clan's news: flagged for Jamie.
- **Retired kinds** join `RETIRED_EMAIL_KINDS` (contracts `queue.ts`):
  generation, `deliver`, the relay, the jobs handler and the preference
  door (410 on enabling one) refuse them; Settings no longer lists them.
  Their `account_email_pref` rows stay, inert, and are read only for
  the fold. Old unsubscribe links still resolve.
- **Email tracking** counts per mail, unchanged: the pixel path names
  the kind and period, never a reader.

## Leaders' Actions mail

`clan_actions_waiting` is already on demand and unchanged in code:
Clan's daily evaluation (`ClanEvaluateRule`) calls `mailActionsWaiting`,
which mails a person only when something became theirs since their last
email, nothing when nothing waits, at most one a day per clan. Only its
Settings wording changed ("When an action becomes yours").

## Not milestones in the bell

The bell (`apps/web/src/shell/bell-logic.js`) carries reactions,
followers, answers and account notices, not milestones. Firsts reach
the reader on the timeline as soon as the record learns them and in the
digest. If the bell should carry your own firsts, that is a product
call for Jamie.
