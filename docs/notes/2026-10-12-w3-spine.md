## "Mail collapses; the timeline does not"

The timeline keeps every item at its latest revision and never thins
itself. A mail is a fold over its window's items, and only the mail
folds: milestone, none (leads, then lines); friends, counts plus a
three-fact clan and one quiet line; clan report, capped lists with
totals kept; Arena week, one tile per mode family; collector, one line
per machine; actions waiting, ten lines and a count; feedback answer,
none. A fold never removes an item from the feed and never changes its
words.

## Stage 1, built here

- `services/jobs/src/email/digest.mjs`: `windowItems` reads each
  subject's items for a window from `buildPlayerEntry`, keeps the kinds
  a mail tells and names each by its identity (`keyOf`); `digest` drops
  what the kind's journal holds (`toldKey`), orders and folds.
- `buildMilestone` composes through it. Its facts are identical to
  before: `services/jobs/test/digest.test.mjs` holds the builder's output
  on a fixture of every rank, captured before the move, and the
  existing milestone tests pass unchanged.
- The composer reads per subject through `buildPlayerEntry`, as the
  builder did, not through `buildTimeline`: the timeline's quiet gate,
  dedupe, story merge and cap are not proven neutral for milestone.
  Aligning the two is stage 2's, when friends and Arena move too.

### Stage 1 gap: the feedback answer stays on its SQL

The answer mail cannot compose from `account_feedback_responded`
without changing what is sent, so that part is not built. The item
lacks:

1. **One item per answer.** Account events of one kind within a minute
   are one item (`ACCOUNT_BURST_MS`, entries.mjs:2642-2668, #376) whose
   facts are the first event's: two answers made inside a minute are one
   item naming one `feedback_id` with `count: 2`. The mail sends one per
   answer. Pinned by `digest.test.mjs` ("stage-1 gap").
2. **The words.** Its facts are `feedback_id`, `status`, `count`,
   `apps`. The mail prints the area, category, the message, when it was
   filed, the response and `shipped_in`.
3. **A way to expand it.** Its `follow` is empty (account items lead
   nowhere, `follow.mjs:170-173`), and the read that holds the answer,
   `elixir_my_feedback` with `feedback_id`, marks the response seen,
   which is the gate the mail checks (`response_seen_at` null).
4. **One story per answer.** A revised answer is a new event, a new
   item and id, not the same id at a higher revision (timeline
   principle 2).

The delivery gates (settled ten minutes, unread, switch on, approved
person, not the owner) and the journal (`response_mailed_at`) are
delivery state and stay with the mail at every stage.

## Staged order

Each stage cuts the matching `docs/EMAIL.md` section to keys and
functions (#445). A new item kind, or new facts on a kind (its `schema`),
is an MCP minor (`npm run contract:bump`).

   feedback answer (above). Contract: none.
2. **Owner and per-player items.** First move: `account_feedback_responded`

   `follow` to `elixir_my_feedback`, one id per feedback item with the
   revision rising; the answer mail moves onto the composer. Then the

   possible, as items; the clan report recomposes; #473 stays
