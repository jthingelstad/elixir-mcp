# 2026-11-11 — The flat kit

Jamie, 2026-11-11: "minimize unnecessary boxes and borders. Flat is
better. Buttons should be easy to distinguish. We don't need borders for
borders sake." Social slice 3 (#761) makes that the kit's rule, in the
tokens and the shared stylesheet, so every page inherits it.

**Panels sit on the ground by tone.** Panels, menus, the rail, callouts,
stat cells, tiles, chips, empty states, notices, settings and timeline
rows, Clan's action folds and boxes, the battle page's towers and the
Ladder's rows lose their outlines and dividers. Where a border was a box's
only edge it now has a fill (`--panel` or `--panel-raised`), and the two
floating surfaces (the account menu and the freshness pop) have a shadow.
The page parts in Clan, Home and the rail that drew their own Tailwind
dividers (`border-*`, `divide-y`) lose them too.

**A border stays where it carries meaning:** inputs and their focus and
invalid states; table rules; focus rings; a leader's decision, dashed
(`.kit-panel--decision`); the clan's gold edge, now an inset left edge
(`--edge-clan`, on Needs you and `.kit-panel--clan`) rather than a full
outline; a chosen chip's gold edge; selected tabs and the rail's current
mark; the dashed caveat and an unread day; card rarity frames and level
badges; Drop's own brand on its links.

**Three button weights.** Gold, filled (`.btn--primary`, and `.btn--gold`,
its older name), is the one thing to do on a screen. Solid (`.btn`), a new
neutral fill (`--control`, hover `--control-hover`), is any other action;
chips and segmented controls use the same fill. A link (`.btn--quiet`,
`.btn--text`, `button.link`) is the way out. No button is outlined: the
border stays transparent so every weight keeps its size. A selected
button is an accent fill rather than an outline.

Contrast: ink on the solid fill is 11.25:1 and dimmed ink 6.41:1; the
fill against a panel is 1.36:1 and against the ground 1.49:1, so a solid
button reads by its fill and its label.

Fixed on the way: a selected quiet button showed its selection only as a
border, because `.btn--quiet` came later in the sheet than
`.btn--selected`; and the Timeline page's divider used `border-rule`,
which is not a token, so it drew in the text colour.

The palette, fonts and layouts are unchanged. Admin keeps its inline
styles. MCP and JSON API unchanged.
