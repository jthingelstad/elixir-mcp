# 2026-11-22 — A week's form on the Timeline (week_form)

A new player Timeline kind, `week_form`: a closed game week's win rate
in one mode beside the four game weeks before it, told when it rose at
least ten points. Stats' "This week vs your last 4" strip, as a moment.
MCP 15.3.0 (additive), JSON API 6.0.0 unchanged, no migration.

## What it does

- **When:** a read window holding a game week's end (Monday 10:00Z)
  looks at that week (Monday 10:00Z to the next) against the four game
  weeks before it, per Stats mode (Trophy Road, Ranked, Clan Wars, event,
  casual), never pooled.
- **Says something** only when both sides have `FORM_MIN_BATTLES` (10)
  decided battles and the week's win rate is at least `WEEK_FORM_RISE`
  (0.10, `packages/record/src/form.mjs`) above the four weeks'.
- **One item a week:** several modes rising give one item, the mode with
  the most decided battles in the week (ties by the mode order above).
- **Line:** "Pip won 67% of 12 Trophy Road battles in the week to
  Nov 16, up from 50% over the 4 weeks before." Numbers only.
- **Facts:** the member, `mode`, `week {from, to, battles,
  decided_battles, wins, losses, win_rate}`, `previous {from, to, weeks,
  decided_battles, win_rate}`; `at` is the week's end; the story is
  `moment("week_form", tag, week start)`.
- **follow:** `battles_performance` with the two windows and the mode.

## One derivation

The decided-win and decided-loss counts and the rate's rounding moved
into `packages/record/src/win-rate-sql.mjs`; `battles_performance` and
the Timeline both count with them, boat defenses excluded by
`notBoatDefense`, and the minimum and the rise are `weekForm` and
`weekFormRise` in `@elixir-mcp/record/form`. A test reconciles the
item's numbers with `battles_performance`'s `window` and
`compare_window` through the registered tool.

## Cost

Computed at read time on the player entry. One grouped query per player
read alone, one statement for the whole batch when several players are
read together, and none when the window holds no Monday 10:00Z. The news
check adds one `exists` clause, only then.

The read looks each battle up by key for its mode (event_tag), so it
costs shared buffers: an index-only gate first counts each player's
decided battles over the week and the four before (`type_class` is
denormalized on the participant), and a player under the minimum on
either side is never read further; the participant scan stays on its
covering index, and one lookup on `battle_timeline_cover` gives the
type, event tag and boat side. On the signed-in budget's synthetic
record (56 busy players, 26 weeks) the week's read is about 30,000
buffers, so the timeline's ceiling moved from 42,000 to 70,000, the
bell's from 1,100 to 1,700 and cheers' from 900 to 1,400, and the
player page and Home's built week take one statement more. A stored
reading per player and week would take that back, at the price of a
table; not done here.

## [engineering] decisions

- Read time, not a recorded event: a week's battles captured after a
  read are counted on the next read.
- The line says "in the week to <date>" (the reader's zone) rather than
  "last week", so it stays true read later.
- War is "Clan Wars" in the line and "War" on the card, as Stats names it.
- **See stats** always names the player and mode (an item cannot tell a
  primary player from an alt); it opens the current season in that mode.
- Not a small moment, so its card and link show.
- Kept out of the digest and Discord by their whitelists.

## Product questions

- Is ten points the right rise, or should it scale with the battles?
- Rises only: should a steady week or a drop ever show (to the player
  alone)?
- One mode a week: or one item per mode that rose?
