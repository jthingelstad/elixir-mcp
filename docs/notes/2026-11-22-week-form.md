# 2026-11-22 — A week's form on the Timeline (week_form)

A new player Timeline kind, `week_form`: a closed game week's win rate
in one mode beside the four game weeks before it, told when it rose at
least ten points. Stats' "This week vs your last 4" strip, as a moment.
MCP 15.3.0 (additive), JSON API 6.0.0 unchanged. Migration 0311 adds
`player_week_form`, the stored readings; a weekly jobs run writes them.

## What it does

- **When:** every Monday at 16:15Z the jobs Lambda reads the game week
  that closed at 10:00Z (Monday 10:00Z to Monday 10:00Z) against the
  four game weeks before it, per Stats mode (Trophy Road, Ranked, Clan
  Wars, event, casual), never pooled, for every claimed player (a claim
  is the only way onto a Timeline), and stores what it finds. The item
  is learned then (`observed_at` is the run's `computed_at`); it
  happened at the week's end (`at`).
- **Says something** only when both sides have `FORM_MIN_BATTLES` (10)
  decided battles and the week's win rate is at least `WEEK_FORM_RISE`
  (0.10, `packages/record/src/form.mjs`) above the four weeks'.
- **One item a week:** several modes rising give one item, the mode with
  the most decided battles in the week (ties by the mode order above).
- **Line:** "Pip won 67% of 12 Trophy Road battles in the week to
  Nov 16, up from 50% over the 4 weeks before." Numbers only.
- **Facts:** the member, `mode`, `week {from, to, battles,
  decided_battles, wins, losses, win_rate}`, `previous {from, to, weeks,
  decided_battles, win_rate}`; `at` is the week's end; the story is
  `moment("week_form", tag, week start)`.
- **follow:** `battles_performance` with the two windows and the mode.

## One derivation

The decided-win and decided-loss counts and the rate's rounding moved
into `packages/record/src/win-rate-sql.mjs`; `battles_performance` and
the Timeline both count with them, boat defenses excluded by
`notBoatDefense`, and the minimum and the rise are `weekForm` and
`weekFormRise` in `@elixir-mcp/record/form`. A test reconciles the
item's numbers with `battles_performance`'s `window` and
`compare_window` through the registered tool.

## Stored, not read-time

The first cut computed the reading on every Timeline read. On the
signed-in budget's synthetic record (56 busy players, 26 weeks) that
statement alone cost about 30,000 shared buffers even after an
index-only gate (from 37,458 to 67,240 for the whole Timeline read),
and a seven-day window always holds a Monday 10:00Z, so nearly every
Timeline, RSS and Home read paid it, on a database that stays small.
Stored, the read is the table by `(player_tag, week_start)`: one
primary-key read a player, one statement for a batch.

- **Table** `player_week_form` (0311): one row per player and game week,
  only for a qualifying week (the mode that rose, both minimums, the
  most decided battles), with the week's battles, decided, wins, losses
  and rate and the four weeks' decided and rate. Derived and
  rebuildable, not a canonical table: no foreign key, trimmed with the
  battles it read (a year, `retention.mjs`).
- **Writer** `storeWeekForms` (`@elixir-mcp/record/week-form-store`),
  run by `{week_form: true}` (`services/jobs/src/week-form.mjs`,
  EventBridge `elixir-mcp-week-form`, `cron(15 16 ? * MON *)`). It
  counts with battles_performance's own SQL, so the numbers are the
  tool's for the same two windows; a test reconciles them through the
  registered tool at write time. Fifty claimed players a statement, in
  tag order; the run stops 60 s before its 300 s deadline and fails, and
  the async retry (two, within the hour) runs the week again. By hand,
  `{week_form: {week_start, after?}}` writes one closed week.
- **Idempotent:** the counts read only battles the record had learned
  by the week's end plus the grace (`battle.created_at`), a changed
  reading is upserted (an unchanged one is left, so `computed_at` does
  not move), and a reading the week no longer gives is deleted. Running
  a week twice writes the same rows.
- **Reader:** the player entry reads the window's rows, by when they
  were written (observed) or the week's end (occurred), as a ledger
  row. The news check is one `exists` on `computed_at`.
- **Budgets:** the five ceilings are where they were. The player page
  and Home's built week take one statement more (the read is
  unconditional now); the Timeline read measured 37,469 buffers (37,458
  before week_form).

## [engineering] decisions

- Stored once a week, not read time (the coordinator's call on cost,
  above).
- The grace is six hours (`WEEK_FORM_GRACE_MS`): the run counts the
  battles the record learned by Monday 16:00Z, and a battle recorded
  after that never changes the item. A few of the slowest battle-log
  poll cycles; one constant.
- The run reads claimed players only: a followed player is claimed.
- The line says "in the week to <date>" (the reader's zone) rather than
  "last week", so it stays true read later.
- War is "Clan Wars" in the line and "War" on the card, as Stats names it.
- **See stats** always names the player and mode (an item cannot tell a
  primary player from an alt); it opens the current season in that mode.
- Not a small moment, so its card and link show.
- Kept out of the digest and Discord by their whitelists.

## Product questions

- Is ten points the right rise, or should it scale with the battles?
- Rises only: should a steady week or a drop ever show (to the player
  alone)?
- One mode a week: or one item per mode that rose?
