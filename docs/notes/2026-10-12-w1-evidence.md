# 2026-10-12 — A removal's evidence says what it rests on and what it cannot see (#532)

MCP 11.11.2 and JSON API 3.2.0 unchanged; no migration. From the Clan
brief Jamie ratified on 2026-10-12 (#525), `docs/product/clan.md`
principle 4, and the DECISIONS line "A removal says what it rests on and
what it cannot see". It builds on the same day's battle-log change
(`2026-10-12-clan-removal-runs-from-the-battle-log.md`): the clock and
when a removal is raised do not change here, only what the Action shows.

## Jamie's answer

DECISIONS, "A removal's evidence is one facts row and one sentence"
[Jamie, 2026-10-12]:

> Add a facts row to the removal Action card that names the last recorded
> battle (time and mode), the join, and the counter rise it watched, and
> one sentence: modes the battle log does not carry are not seen, so the
> leader weighs it. The Discord line and the mail stay as they are. Do not
> write a list of modes: file a claim in ../cr-agent-api-docs to verify
> which modes the battle log omits, and leave the sentence general until
> it is answered. clan-actions.md says the same.

The claim is
[cr-agent-api-docs#9](https://github.com/jthingelstad/cr-agent-api-docs/issues/9):
the player battlelog endpoint omits some game modes; which ones is
unverified. When it is answered, the sentence may name them; until then
it stays general.

## Questions and the answers taken

- **Where the row goes.** In the card's facts, after "Last battle" and
  "In the clan", keyed `rests_on`, labelled **Rests on**, on removal cards
  only (`cardFacts(v, policy, type)`). Promotion and demotion cards are
  unchanged. [Jamie, 2026-10-12]
- **Where the sentence goes.** Once, under the facts on every removal
  card; the headline ("N days without a battle: at risk at A, an action
  at R.") stays as it is, since it is also the log's raised line.
  [engineering, 2026-10-12]
- **The time.** The battle's `battle_time`, never an observation time,
  drawn with the clan's existing `stamp(…, zone, { year: true })`
  (`2026-09-22 13:00 CDT` in Chicago). The saved plain `value`, which the
  log's raised entry shows as text, writes the same instants in UTC.
  [engineering, 2026-10-12]
- **The mode.** The family's group name (`MODE_LABEL` in `@elixir-mcp/ui`:
  Trophy Road, Path of Legends, War, Friendly, Challenge, Event,
  Tournament) from the battle's `mode_group` (contracts `modeGroupSql`),
  falling back to the battle's own game-mode name, as Clan's member
  activity page already does; never an id. The engine's copy
  (`MODE_GROUP_LABEL`) is pinned equal to `MODE_LABEL` by a clan-web test.
  [engineering, 2026-10-12]
- **The counter it watched.** The profile's battle count across Elixir's
  daily profile reads (the same 212 days the clock reads): its latest
  rise (by how much, between which two reads), or that it did not rise
  across N reads from the first to the last, or that there were no reads.
  [engineering, 2026-10-12]
- **No recorded battle.** The row says "No battle recorded", and the
  join, which is then what the clock runs from. A join that predates the
  record says "in the clan before recording began, first seen …".
  [engineering, 2026-10-12]
- **Cards raised before this deploy** keep their saved facts (saved
  evidence is never rewritten); they get the sentence, since it is drawn
  for every removal card, but not the row. [engineering, 2026-10-12]

## What changed

- `packages/record/src/clan-activity-evidence.mjs`: the last battle is
  read with its mode (`last_battle_mode_group`, `last_battle_game_mode`),
  boat defenses still excluded, ties broken by `battle_id`.
- `packages/clan-engine/src/inactivity.mjs`: `inactivityEvidence` also
  returns `counter_reads` `{ reads, first_read_at, last_read_at }`.
- `packages/clan-engine/src/facts.mjs`: member facts carry the two mode
  fields.
- `packages/clan-engine/src/render.mjs`: `cardFacts` takes the card type;
  a removal adds the `rests_on` row (`value`, plus `last_battle`,
  `joined`, `counter` and `note`); `REMOVAL_UNSEEN` and `MODE_GROUP_LABEL`
  are exported.
- `packages/clan/src/manage/service.mjs`: passes the type; the verdict
  cache's `removal_evidence_schema` is 5, so cached verdicts are
  recomputed with the new fields.
- `packages/clan-web/src/components/ActionCard.jsx`: draws the row from
  its fields in the reader's zone, and the sentence under the facts of
  every removal card.
- Docs: `clan-actions.md` (the inactivity clock), glossary swept.

## Response shapes

`/api/clan` only (Clan is not on MCP or `/api/v1`). A removal card's
`evidence.facts[]` gains the `rests_on` row with the fields above;
`evidence.activity_evidence`, a member's `removal.activity_evidence` and
an Action's `removal_safety` gain `counter_reads`. Additive; no contract
change.

## Not changed

The Discord line and the mail ("#N Remove from the clan: Name",
`mail.mjs`, `discord.mjs`); the clan-chat copy; the model's draft packet
for a removal, which carries no facts.
