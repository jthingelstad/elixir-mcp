# 2026-11-12 — The race bar asks for decks from every current member

Codex on #798: `war_current` lists a current member the game left out of
the race roster in `members_not_in_race`, not in `participants`, so the
Feed's race bar asked four decks a war day only of the members in the
roster and overstated the clan's play. The ask now counts both; the decks
played still come only from current participants (`clan-race.mjs`).
