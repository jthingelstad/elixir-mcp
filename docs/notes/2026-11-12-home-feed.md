## What changed

- **One feed.** `/home` draws `HomeFeed` (`apps/web/src/home/Feed.jsx`, was
  `Timeline.jsx`) beside Your week, the clan panel and Setup details. It is
  the seven-day `GET /api/me/timeline`, so the feed's place ("New since your
  last visit") and its evidence reads are the ones the Timeline tab had.
  Chips Everyone, Friends, Clan, You (`FEED_CHIPS`, `feedShown` in
  `home/logic.js`) and, as structure Q8 keeps, State (All, New). An account
  that follows someone before it has a player of its own sees the feed under
  its first steps, as the Timeline tab showed it.
- **Feed fields.** `annotateFeed` (`services/web-api/src/feed.mjs`) adds to
  each item, on the website's read only: `groups` (which chips: "you" for your
  own players and the account's own items, "friends" for a friend claim,
  "clan" for a clan you track; Everyone is every item), `spark` for a sitting
  and `battle_url` for a moment's proving battle (`promoted_by`, `crossed_by`,
  `best_win`), as its public page. MCP's `elixir_timeline` carries none of it,
  so no contract moves.
- **Sparklines.** `sessionSpark` (`packages/tools/src/activity/entries.mjs`)
  keeps a sitting's battles in order behind a Symbol key, never serialized;
  `sparkBars` maps each to `{ r: "w"|"l"|"d", m: 1..3|null }`.
  `Sparkline.jsx` draws one bar a battle: up for a win, down for a loss, height
  the crown margin; a draw a flat tick; a missing margin a short faint bar.
  `role="img"` with `sparkWords` as its label ("5 battles: 3 wins, 2
  losses. In order: won by 3 crowns, lost by 1 crown, …").
- **Navigation.** `packages/ui/src/family.json` has the tabs Home, Find, Clan,
  You (`bar: false`) and the bell; the site's static bar (`base.njk`,
  `familyBar.js`) reads the same manifest. The wide bar is Home, Find, Clan,
  search, Help, the bell, Play Drop and the avatar; the phone's tabs Home,
  Find, Clan, You and Bell. Find (`home/Find.jsx`): search, Follow a friend,
  the clan directory, your follow link. You (`home/You.jsx`): your players'
  pages, their stats in Ladder, Settings, Drop, Admin for staff, sign out
  (`shell/sign-out.js`, shared with the avatar menu). Search opens through
  `shell/sheet.js`, which the bar and Find share.
- **The bell.** `shell/Bell.jsx` is Home's old Needs you: the dot reads
  `/api/me/home/needs` (no record reads) every few minutes, and the sheet adds
  the clan read when opened. A dot on Home marks `me.signals.timeline_pending`
  (news since the feed's place), so the Timeline tab's own news read
  (`useTimelineNews`) is gone.
- **Redirects.** `lib/moved.js`: `/home/timeline` to `/home`, `/home/friends`
  to `/home/find`, and the old console timeline and activity addresses to
  `/home`. Mail (`packages/mail/src/render.mjs`) links `/home` and
  `/home/find` directly. The install manifest's first shortcut is Find.
- **Removed from Home.** The Friends tab (`home/Friends.jsx`), whose week
  cards the digest tells, and Latest, which the feed replaces. The routes
  `/api/me/home/latest` and `/api/me/home/friends` stay so a tab still running
  an old bundle keeps working; they leave in a later change.
