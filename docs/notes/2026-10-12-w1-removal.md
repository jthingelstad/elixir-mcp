# 2026-10-12 — Removing an account deletes the account, not the record (#529)

MCP 11.11.2 and JSON API 3.2.0 unchanged; no migration. From the accounts
brief Jamie ratified on 2026-10-12 (#525), `docs/product/accounts.md`,
"When a person leaves", and the record brief's section of the same name;
DECISIONS, "Removing an account deletes the account, not the record"
[Jamie, 2026-10-12]: "on request, claims, nicknames, agents, connections,
mail and usage go; the players' game history is public game data and
stays until the untracked trim reaches it."

## What changed

- `{account_remove}` (`services/migrate/src/ops-account-remove.mjs`)
  deletes six more things for the person and every agent they own:
  `agent_policy_context_grant` (as agent or as owner),
  `timeline_discord` and `timeline_discord_told`, the account's
  `discord_post` rows, the account's `rate_limit` counters, and Clan's
  `clan_state` row `account#<account_id>`. Dry run stays the default and
  counts each of them under `removed`. The `manual` list names the relay's
  `timeline-discord-state/<account_id>/` records in the outbox bucket,
  which expire with the bucket's 14-day rule.
- `POST /api/email/unsubscribe` writes nothing for a removed account
  (`email_hash` `removed:…`): a late one-click on an old mail answers as
  done and no longer grows a switch row and an account event on the
  tombstone.
- `apps/site/src/docs/your-account.md` ("Removing the account") and
  `privacy.md` ("Keeping and removing") say the brief's list and that the
  players' game history stays under the record's retention rule, linked to
  the record brief rather than naming a date or a job: the trim (#528) is
  not built.

## Every table, before and after

Census: every `account_id`, `*_account_id`, `owner_account`,
`requested_by` and `email_hash` column in `db/migrations` through 0225,
plus text keys that carry an account id (`rate_limit.bucket`,
`clan_state.pk`). Tables dropped since (`clan_follow`,
`verification_challenge`, `event_feed`, `collection`,
`collection_member`, `integration_collection_grant`) are left out.

**Deleted by the op before this change** (22): `mcp_call_audit`,
`credential_refusal`, `service_token`, `oauth_token`, `oauth_family`,
`oauth_code`, `session`, `magic_login` (by address hash),
`claim_challenge`, `claim`, `account_clan`, `account_clan_declined`,
`player_nickname`, `timeline_reader`, `agent_identity`, `feedback` (its
`feedback_ref` rows cascade), `collector_version_event`, `email_send`,
`email_milestone`, `email_milestone_look`, `account_email_pref`,
`account_event` (one `account_removed` event is written after).
`recording` rows the account alone kept alive are stopped, not deleted.

**Added in this change** (6):

| Table | Brief's word | Why it was left |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `agent_policy_context_grant` (0202) | connection | Cascades only on an account delete. A run removed it anyway through `token_id … on delete cascade` from `service_token`, but a dry run never counted it. |
| `timeline_discord` (0213) | connection | The sealed webhook of the timeline cross-post. Cascades only on an account delete, and the account row is a tombstone. |
| `timeline_discord_told` (0213) | mail (delivery state) | Same cascade. |
| `discord_post` (0222), rows with `account_id` | mail (delivery state) | No foreign key. Only the timeline cross-post sets `account_id`; Clan's posts carry the clan instead and stay. |
| `rate_limit`, account-keyed buckets | usage | Text keys, so no cascade. `mcp#`, `mcpday#`, `liveday#`, `firstread#`, `verify#` and `rest-hour:`/`rest-person-hour:` end in the account id; `mcp#token#<token_id>` names a key; `auth#<address hash>` counts sign-in mail. Aged at 7 days by the jobs Lambda, so this only clears the last week. |
| `clan_state`, pk `account#<account_id>` | connection (Clan's remembered clan and Verify notice) | A text key in Clan's ledger; Clan has no other per-account row. |

**Kept, and why:**

- `account`: the tombstone (no address, `removed:<id>` hash, disabled).
  Rows that stay point at it. An owned agent's row is tombstoned the same
  way; its `public_id` stays, and every credential of it is gone.
- `recording.requested_by`: the record's history of who started it.
  Stopping a recording deletes nothing recorded.
- `gateway` (revoked): a collector's history. A live one refuses removal.
- `attested_fact.attester_account_id`: the clan's record, labelled with
  the attesting player's tag.
- `integration`, `integration_usage`, `integration_profile_refresh`: an
  owner of an integration is refused until it is retired; these are the
  integration's, and Jamie provisions and retires it.
- `clan_state` rows keyed by clan or player tag (Actions, holds, notes,
  awards, `mailed#`, the model key and Discord settings, whose `set_by` is
  a player tag): the clan's ledger, not the account's.
  Legacy `pref#<player_tag>` rows, read once as a fallback: keyed by a
  player, not an account, and nothing writes them any more.
- `clan_activity_discord`, `clan_activity_discord_told`, `discord_run`,
  `discord_post` rows with a `clan_tag`: the clan's.
- `email_issue`, `oauth_client`, `family_oauth_client`: an issue and the
  client registrations carry no account.
- The game record (`player`, `battle*`, `clan*`, snapshots, war tables):
  public game data, kept under the record's retention rule.

**Outside the database** (the op's `manual` list): the Buttondown
newsletter address (by hand); sent-mail bodies under `mail/sent/` (keys
returned, deleted by hand); call captures under `calls/` (90-day
lifecycle); the relay's `timeline-discord-state/<account_id>/` records in
the outbox bucket (14-day lifecycle, new on the list). Backups roll off in
about a week.

## The questions, and the answers taken

1. **What counts as each word of the brief?** Claims: `claim`,
   `claim_challenge`, `account_clan`, `account_clan_declined`. Nicknames:
   `player_nickname`. Agents: the owned accounts, their keys and
   identities. Connections: sessions, OAuth grants, codes and tokens,
   service tokens, the policy-context grant, the timeline's Discord
   webhook, Clan's remembered clan. Mail: sends, switches, milestones,
   the cross-post's delivery state. Usage: the call log, refusals, rate
   counters. [engineering, 2026-10-12]
2. **Delete the tombstone instead?** No. `recording`, `gateway` and
   `attested_fact` point at it, and the record keeps them [engineering,
   as the op already decided in #129].
3. **What does the public page promise about the history?** That it is
   public game data and stays under the record's retention rule, linked
   to the record brief. No date and no job: the trim (#528) is not built,
   and the limits page changes when it lands (record brief, Open).
4. **Run the op to check?** No. No documented test account exists; the
   op's tests in `services/migrate/test` are the gate, against a scratch
   database with every migration applied.
5. **Contract?** No tool or `/api/v1` surface changes; no bump.
