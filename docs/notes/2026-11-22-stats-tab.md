# 2026-11-22 — Stats is a tab of its own

A player asked for it on 2026-11-22: reaching Stats took Profile, then
**Your stats**, and "it will be a lot easier to see and engage" with a tab.
Jamie approved product changes that make the experience better the same day.

- The shell manifest (`packages/ui/src/shell.json`) lists Stats between Find
  and Clan, so the phone draws five tabs (`.kit-bottom` is a five-column
  grid) and the wide top bar shows Timeline, Find, Stats and Clan. The
  site's static pages draw the same list.
- `barArea` lights Stats on every Stats page. A followed player's stats
  (`?player=`) light it too: the address is the same page.
- The Profile › Stats tabs (`YouTabs`) are gone; Stats' own frame head
  already names the season and the player. A followed player's stats keep
  their way back to that player's page. Profile keeps **Your stats**.
- Docs: Reading your stats and the quickstart say to tap Stats.

No API, contract or migration change.
