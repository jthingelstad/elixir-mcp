## The questions, and the answers taken

1. **May Elixir's own mail use a model?** No. Elixir funds no model use:
   its own voice (mail, timeline text, Ladder, the site) is written from
   the record by code, and models run only on a clan's own key in Clan or
   in a person's own agent. `docs/product/mail.md`, "Elixir's own voice is
   deterministic"; DECISIONS, "Ultimate Champions and Card of the Week are
   retired, and Elixir funds no model use" [Jamie, 2026-10-12]. This
   closes the question that `2026-10-11-rules-review.md` (item 2) left
   open; that note is history and stays as written. The rule is now on
   the public About page and the email page says it of every kind.
2. **How many kinds of mail?** Seven: four weekly reports (clan, Arena,
   friends, collector) and three that come when something happens
   (milestone, Clan's actions waiting, answers to feedback).
   `ACTIVE_PRODUCT_EMAIL_KINDS` has seven; the brief says seven [Jamie,
   2026-10-12]. The ledger line said six and left out feedback answers
   (added 2026-10-08), and `docs/EMAIL.md`'s console section said "the
   two event kinds" and "all six". Feedback answers are one mail per
   answer, so they join milestone as exempt from one-a-day.
3. **Is Clan's clan switcher a gap?** No, it is the design: Clan sees one
   clan at a time, each with its own policy, standing, Actions and
   awards, and a leader switches. A family (a main and its feeders) is a
   recording concept of the Family tier. DECISIONS, "Clan sees one clan
   at a time" [Jamie, 2026-10-12].
4. **Does Clan know which clans make a family?** No, and the pages do not
   say it does [engineering, 2026-10-12]: nothing links a main to its
   feeders. Clan's clan list is the distinct clans of the person's own
   players, primary and alts (`clansOf` in `packages/clan/src/gate.mjs`),
   and the choice is remembered on the session
   (`select` in `packages/clan/src/handler.mjs`). The pages say "the
   clans your players are in", which is what runs.
5. **Who talks with members in Discord?** The family's Discord agent
   (elixir-mcp-discord), run by a clan on its own tokens. Clan posts:
   Actions and activity stories reach a clan's Discord through webhooks
   connected in Clan, for any clan, sealed per clan
   (`packages/clan/src/manage/discord.mjs`). Clan has no ask lane and no
   inbound Discord handling. DECISIONS, "Clan's Discord posts; the
   family's Discord agent converses" [Jamie, 2026-10-12];
   `docs/product/clan.md` principle 10; `docs/product/timeline.md`
   principle 7. The example "Can my clanmates ask this in our Discord?"
   still answers yes: that is the agent.
6. **Can a member act from Discord?** Not built, and deliberately
   undecided while the posting and the agent show what members do there.
   Default in force (`docs/product/timeline.md`, Open): Discord is a
   channel; every write happens in Elixir. The pages say so and promise
   nothing.
7. **The agent's own guide.** elixir-mcp-discord's AGENTS.md said "Ask
   Elixir goes away and members connect Elixir MCP to their own agent";
   the timeline brief retires that direction [Jamie, 2026-10-12]. Its
   one-line correction is a pull request of its own in that repository;
   the running bot is not rebuilt for it (no `src/` change).
