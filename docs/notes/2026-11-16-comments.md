# 2026-11-16 — Comments, checked before they post; blocks and reports

Jamie, 2026-11-16: "I'm open to comments and I think we could use Haiku
or something similar to review every comment before it is submitted to
avoid moderation issues." This change is the server half: the schema,
the check, the API, blocks, reports and the admin queue. The app's
screens follow in their own pull request. Everything here is off until
the stack's `CommentsEnabled` parameter is true.

Migration 0280 follows 0279 (#1071, cheers on your play), which must
merge first: the migrate runner takes dense ids.

## What a comment is

- Plain text, at most 280 characters, tidied (NFC, invisible and
  direction characters dropped, spaces collapsed). No links, no
  @-mentions, no images, one level: no replies to replies.
- On one timeline item (`tl_…`) about a player whose owner is an
  approved person with that player Verified (primary or alt). Items with
  no verified owner, a clan's news or a player nobody verified, take
  none [engineering]: nobody could set who may comment, remove one or
  block on that player's behalf.
- Written by a signed-in person with a Verified player of their own, on
  an item in their own feed now (the same rebuild cheers use), as the
  owner's comment setting allows:
  - `followers` (the default): the writer follows the owner's account,
    or one of the owner's Verified players.
  - `following`: the owner follows the writer, the same two ways.
  - `nobody`.
  The owner may always comment on their own items. Agents never do; no
  MCP tool and no `/api/v1` route reads or writes a comment.
- Seen by the readers of the item (the feed's card shows the count and
  the latest two; the item's own list up to 100), never by anyone in a
  block with the author, and in the bell by the item's owner only. Never
  in mail, RSS, Discord, a shared public page or MCP.

## The check

`services/web-api/src/comment-check.mjs`, before every post, and fails
closed:

1. Cheap filters (`comment-filter.mjs`): empty, too long, links in their
   disguises ("dot com", `discord gg`), email addresses, phone numbers
   (digits or words), "add me on <app>", "my <app>:", "dm me", @handles.
   A filter refusal never reaches the model.
2. `claude-haiku-5-5` (checked against the live Models API on
   2026-11-16), `effort: low`, `max_tokens` 64, a strict `verdict` tool
   (`allow`, `category`), the policy as a cached system prompt, the item's
   line and the comment fenced as data. Categories: harassment, sexual,
   hate, threat, self_harm, spam, link, personal_info. The model's own
   safety stop is a refusal (`declined`).
3. No verdict (an error, a timeout, a stop without a call, the relay
   with no key) is no post: "Couldn't check that just now; try again."

The call rides the clan model bridge as a new method, `moderate`, which
carries no key: the API seals the request into the outbox and the email
relay, which alone reaches Anthropic, adds `MODERATION_API_KEY` and
answers. The bridge refuses a `moderate` request that names a key or asks
for more than 256 tokens. No new IAM: the API already writes the outbox
prefix and the relay already reads it and reaches Anthropic.

Every check is a `comment_check` row: verdict, stage, category, model,
tokens and cost. Only a refusal keeps the words, for the admin queue; the
sweep deletes every row at 35 days. The month's spend is the sum of
those rows (UTC calendar month); at the cap (`CommentsMonthlyCapUsd`,
default $10) a check is logged `paused` and the author is told comments
are paused. A call that reported no usage is charged a cent, so a run of
errors still reaches the cap.

Limits, counted from the checks: 5 a minute, 40 a day, and an hour's
cooldown after 3 refusals in an hour. The same words from the same author
within a day are refused as spam without a model call.

### Eval

`services/web-api/eval/moderation.mjs` runs `moderation-cases.json`
(65 cases: game banter that must pass in five languages, and refusals
in their disguises: leetspeak, spacing, emoji, Spanish, French, German,
Portuguese, prompt injection) through the real request and the relay's
own client. Run on 2026-11-16 against `claude-haiku-5-5`, two runs:
130 of 130 right (0 of 60 false refusals, 0 of 70 missed refusals),
68 of 68 refusals in the expected category, 6 caught by the filters
alone, $0.0082 for the 130 checks (about $0.00006 a check, so $10 is
over 100,000 checks). Never run in CI.

## Blocks

`account_block` (0280), silent, either way for what each sees, up to
1,000. A person blocks from a comment, a player (its verified owner) or
an account page. On block, the follows between them end both ways
(account follows and friend follows of each other's own players; the
blocked side's unfollow is not logged on their account). Unblocking
restores nothing a block ended.

| Surface | While a block stands (either way) |
| --- | --- |
| Comments | neither sees the other's; neither may comment on the other's items; a report of theirs is refused |
| Follow | `POST /api/claims` friend follow of the other's verified player, and an account follow, answer not found; a new verification drops friend follows held by blocked accounts |
| Cheers | may not cheer the other's items; the other's cheers leave your counts and the bell |
| Bell | the other's cheers, follows and comments are left out |
| Activity mail and push | the other's cheers and follows are not counted (they read the bell's reactions and followers) |
| People to follow, Find, the map, handle search | the other's players and account page are left out |
| Account page | `you.blocked` is your own block only; the blocked person is never told |
| Game data | nothing is hidden: battles, player pages and records stay readable to anyone signed in |

The follow, cheer and comment refusals hold whenever a block stands, and
so do the filters that ride an existing statement (cheer counts, the
bell's cheers and follows, the activity mail and push). The people
lists, the account page's `you.blocked` and the bell's comments read
blocks with a statement of their own, so they do it only while comments
are on: a page with comments off makes no extra statement (the signed-in
statement budgets are unchanged).

## Reports and the admin queue

- `comment_report` (0280): one per reporter per comment, a reason
  (bullying, sexual, hate, threat, self_harm, spam, personal_info, other).
- The reporter stops seeing the comment at once. Two open reports from
  different accounts hide it from everyone but its author, who is never
  told.
- Admin (`GET /api/admin/comments`): reported and hidden comments with
  their reports, the latest refusals with their words, the month's
  checks and spend, and suspended accounts. Remove (kept 30 days, then
  swept), dismiss (it shows again) or suspend an account's commenting for
  some days (0 ends it).
- The item's owner may remove a comment on their item (kept for admins
  30 days); an author's own delete is immediate.

## API

Person-only (an agent is refused; no agent console reaches them), each a
404 `comments_off` while comments are off:

- `GET /api/me/comments?item=<id>&subjects=<tags>`, `POST /api/me/comments`
  `{ item_id, subjects, text }` (201; 422 refused with `category` and one
  plain `message`; 429 `rate_limited` or `cooldown`; 503 `unavailable` or
  `paused`; 403 `not_verified` or `not_open`), `DELETE /api/me/comments/<id>`
- `POST /api/me/comment-reports` `{ id, reason }`
- `GET` and `PUT /api/me/comment-setting` `{ who }`
- `GET /api/me/blocks`, `POST /api/me/blocks` `{ account_id | player_tag
  | comment_id }`, `DELETE /api/me/blocks/<page id>`
- Admin: `GET /api/admin/comments`, `POST /api/admin/comments/remove`,
  `POST /api/admin/comments/dismiss`, `POST /api/admin/comment-suspensions`

Home's feed items carry `comments: { count, latest, open }` (or `null`
when the item takes none), read live per page in one windowed statement
and never stored. `GET /api/me` carries `features.comments`. The bell
carries `comments`: comments on your items in the last week. Comment ids
are bigints and travel as strings.

## Schema, secret, parameters

- Migration 0280: `timeline_comment`, `comment_report`, `comment_check`,
  `account_block`, `comment_setting`. No cascade from `account`: account
  removal deletes them by name (`ops-account-remove.mjs`).
- Retention: comments a year (TRIMMED, like cheers), removed comments 30
  days after removal and checks 35 days (`sweepOperational`); reports go
  with their comment; blocks and settings are kept.
- Secret: app-secret key `moderation_api_key`, read by the email relay
  only, only under `CommentsEnabled` (`docs/SECRETS.md`, Moderation key).
- Parameters: `CommentsEnabled` (`false`), `CommentsMonthlyCapUsd`
  (`10`), both preserved across deploys. Web API env `COMMENTS_ENABLED`,
  `COMMENTS_CAP_USD`; relay env `MODERATION_API_KEY`.
- No IAM change.
