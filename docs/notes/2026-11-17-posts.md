# 2026-11-17: Posts, questions and cards from the game

Jamie, 2026-11-17: "now that we have commenting and a moderation
capability it feels like a player could start a new post with just text.
Like a question they are asking? Maybe a question post? A variant would
be a question and they paste a deck link from the game and elixir
understands the deck link and renders the cards as a deck in the post. In
general a player could attach cards to a post."

Everything here is off until the stack's `PostsEnabled` is true, and it
takes effect only while `CommentsEnabled` is true too.

## What a post is

- Plain text, 1 to 500 characters, tidied like a comment (NFC, invisible
  and direction characters dropped, spaces collapsed). No links, no
  @-mentions, no images, no uploads. A URL in the words is refused by the
  filters; the words are never drawn as a link or as markup.
- Written as the author's Verified player (primary, else an alt), by a
  signed-in person with a Verified player of their own: the commenting
  rule. A person paused from commenting is paused from posting
  ("You can't post right now."). Agents never post.
- Optionally a **question**: "Ask a question" in the composer, a
  "Question" label on the post, and "Answer" in place of "Comment".
- Optionally **a deck** or **up to 8 cards**, never both:
  - A deck comes from the game's deck link, pasted into the words. It is
    taken out of the words; the post keeps the eight cards (and the tower
    troop), never the link. Every id must be a card in the catalog
    (`kind = 'card'`), eight distinct ones, and a tower troop must be a
    support card; otherwise the post is refused ("That deck link names
    cards Elixir doesn't know."). A link that is not a deck link stays
    words, and the filters refuse it as a link.
  - Drawn with DeckRow's art (never altered), the archetype label
    (`classifyDeck`, the same label the record uses), the average elixir
    to one decimal, the tower troop's name, and a Copy deck button that
    copies the game's link built back from the cards (`deckLink`).
  - Loose cards are picked by name from the catalog (the composer's
    search), at most 8, and drawn as art with their names.
  - "Played N times" is left out: a deck link carries no player, and
    counting a deck across players is corpus statistics.

### Deck links read

`packages/contracts/src/deck-link.ts`. Three spellings, each with the
same `deck` parameter (eight ids, `;` or `%3B`) and an optional `tt`:

- `https://link.clashroyale.com/en/?clashroyale://copyDeck?deck=26000000;26000001;26000010;26000014;26000021;27000000;28000000;28000008&tt=159000000`
  (the web form wrapping the app form; what Elixir's Copy deck already
  hands out, on a battle's page since before this change)
- `https://link.clashroyale.com/deck/en?deck=26000000;26000001;...&tt=159000000`
  (the older web form)
- `clashroyale://copyDeck?deck=26000000%3B26000001%3B...` (the app form)

Any language segment (`en`, `pt`, `zh-hant`). Percent-encoding is undone
once; `l`, `slots` and `id` are ignored. Another host, fewer or more than
eight ids, a repeated id or an id longer than nine digits is not a deck
link. The forms are the ones Elixir's own Copy deck has used and players
paste; they were not re-captured from the game for this change, so no
cr-agent-api-docs pull request [engineering].

## Who sees a post

- **Timeline:** the author, and readers who follow the player it was
  written as (a friend follow of the tag) or follow the author's account.
  Overlaid live on the week's feed like a cheer, never stored in
  `timeline_item`.
- **Clan Feed:** members whose Verified player is in the same clan as the
  post's player now.
- **Profile and the player page:** a "Posts" list (newest 30), read
  through `GET /api/me/posts?player=` or `?account=` with the same
  audience rules, so a page shows only posts its reader may see.
- **The bell:** cheers on your own posts of the last 7 days ("Your post:
  …", "Your question: …"), with counts, never who. Comments on your posts
  reach the bell as any comment on your items does.
- Never anyone in a block with the author, either way; a reporter stops
  seeing it at once.
- **Comments on a post** follow the author's comment setting (followers by
  default). A clanmate who does not follow the author may read and cheer
  the post but not answer it [engineering; listed as a product question].

### Where posts never go

Posts are built in `services/web-api` only, never in `@elixir-mcp/tools`
or the record's timeline. Every surface below builds from the tools or
the record, so none can carry one; `services/web-api/test/posts.test.mjs`
pins that the record's timeline has no post and that no code outside the
web API, the schema, the sweeps and account removal names
`account_post`. Post cheers store null `subject_tags`, so the activity
mail and push (which read cheers by subject) never count them.

| Surface | What carrying posts would take |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly digest | A post section read from `account_post` with the Timeline's audience, a per-mail budget, and escaping in mail. A product call on whether people's words belong in a mail. |
| Activity mail and push | A new kind ("a question from someone you follow", "an answer to your question"), its cap, and a setting; push's caps and its privacy paragraph would change. |
| RSS | RSS is anonymous by token; it would publish people's words to whoever holds the token. Needs its own consent line. |
| Discord | Clan's webhooks post to a server Elixir does not moderate; the words would leave Elixir. Needs a per-clan setting and a Terms line. |
| MCP and `/api/v1` | A read tool (contract minor) and the "agents read the social layer" rule; a write would break "agents never write it". |
| Public signed-out pages | A shared post page like a highlight's `/h/<id>`, with no names beyond game names; the Privacy page's "never a comment" lines would need a post clause. |

## Limits and the check

- Every post passes the comment check first: the same filters (links,
  contact details, other apps, handles), then the model with the post
  policy (`POST_MODERATION_SYSTEM`), failing closed. Logged in
  `comment_check` with `kind = 'post'`, under the same monthly cap; a
  refusal keeps the words 35 days for Admin.
- The post policy adds to the comment policy: no asking for or giving
  personal details ("where is everyone from", "how old are you all"), no
  account, gem or pass trading or giveaways, no invitations to talk
  somewhere else (another app, a group chat, a DM) or meet. Inviting
  someone to a clan in the game by its name or tag is fine.
- 1 a minute and 5 a day per account; the comments' hour of cooldown
  after 3 refusals in an hour counts post and comment refusals together.
  The same words within a day are refused as spam without a model call.
- Delete by the author is immediate, with its cheers. Comments on it
  are marked removed and follow the comments' rule (kept 30 days, then
  deleted). Two open reports from different accounts
  hide it from everyone but its author; an admin removes it, dismisses
  the reports (it shows again) or pauses the author (commenting and
  posting). A removed post is kept 30 days for review, then deleted.
  Posts go at a year, like comments.

## API

- `GET /api/me/posts` (`?player=`, `?account=`, `?mine=1`): posts with
  cheers and comments; `POST /api/me/posts` (`text`, `question`,
  `deck_link`, `cards`): 201 with the post, 422 refused with a one-line
  message, 429 limited, 403 `not_verified` or `not_open`;
  `DELETE /api/me/posts/<id>`; `POST /api/me/post-reports`;
  `POST /api/admin/posts/remove` and `/dismiss`. Each answers 404
  `posts_off` while the switch is off; `/api/me` says `features.posts`.
- `POST /api/me/blocks` takes `post_id`.
- `GET /api/admin/comments` adds `posts_enabled` and `posts` (the reported
  posts queue). No MCP or `/api/v1` change.

## Schema and infra

- Migration 0285: `account_post` (identity `post_id`, a random `tl_` item
  id, account, player tag, body 1 to 500, question, deck jsonb of eight,
  cards int[] of at most 8, status visible, hidden or removed),
  `post_report`, and `comment_check.kind` (comment or post, default
  comment). No cascade from `account`: account removal deletes posts and
  reports by name. `db/migrations.sha256` and the schema fingerprint are
  re-pinned.
- Parameter `PostsEnabled` (`false`, preserved across deploys,
  `infra/scripts/parameters.mjs`), web API env `POSTS_ENABLED`. No IAM
  change, no new secret, no new resource.
- A deploy with the switch off applies 0285 (two empty tables and a
  column with a default) and changes nothing anyone sees: no composer, the
  routes answer `posts_off`. With it on (and comments on): the composer
  appears for people with a Verified player, and posts begin to show.
