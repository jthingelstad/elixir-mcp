## What the audit found

Nothing serves a subject's record without an account except the battle
page, and the one subject a status page names is the operator credit
Jamie kept. No route closes, no field leaves, and the site build is
untouched. What ships is the rule written down where it holds and a test
that holds it: `services/web-api/test/public-boundary.test.mjs` walks
every route the web API registers with no credential and fails if one
answers that is not on the public list below, or if a listed route no
longer exists; it pins the `/api/public/stats` fields and the operator
credit to the primary player. The handler exposes its route keys
(`handler.routeKeys`) for it.

None is a game statistic and none names a subject; nothing closes.

### `/api/public/status`

Every field counts the recorder: `health` (last fetch and admission
ages, dead letters, battles admitted this hour, capture audit polls and
gaps), `budget` (rate, reserve, fetches and capacity by day and hour,
useful fetches, charges by lane, tokens), `queue` (due, starved, due by
endpoint, queued, leased, ticks), `jobs` (ledger counts, the pace),
`capture_series` and `capture_5m`/`capture_24h` (fetches, admitted and
rejected per collector card name). `collectors[]`: card name and icon,
status, last success and heartbeat, version, signature state, lane,
fetches this hour, `yield_24h` and `edge_filtered_24h` (shares of that
collector's own fetches), and `operator`, `operator_tag`: the operator's
primary player, the one subject named, by Jamie's answer.

### `/api/public/efficiency`

`rule`; per day (`days[]`, `today`, `last_hour`): battle log polls,
productive and nothing-new polls, battles captured, audited polls, gaps,
snapshot intervals with and without a gap, the battles the game's own
counter expected and the recorder captured in each, the shortfall,
`noise_rate`, `lost_battles`, `players_with_gaps`. Each measures the
recorder's capture against the game's counter and names no subject.
`noise_rate` is the share of the counter the battle log never shows
over gap-free intervals: a rate of the recorder's source, not of anyone's
play, so it stays. [engineering, 2026-10-12]

## Questions and the answers taken

- **Does the operator credit make `/api/public/status` the record?** No:
  it stays, and the record brief's Open line names it as the one
  exception. [Jamie, 2026-10-12]
- **Are corpus totals the record?** No, while each field counts what the
  recorder holds and names no subject; every field passes. [Jamie,
  2026-10-12]
- **Does the battle page's sitting and meetings stretch "one battle"?**
  No: the page is the page as built. [Jamie, 2026-10-12]
- **`/api/public/efficiency` under answer 2's test.** It counts the
  recorder's own capture; `noise_rate` is a rate of the source, not a
  game statistic. Kept. [engineering, 2026-10-12]
- **The card catalog.** The game's own list of cards, with no subject and
  no play statistic (the global card statistics left on 2026-10-02); not
  the record, kept public as reference. [engineering, 2026-10-12]
- **How the rule is held.** A test over the handler's own route map, so
  a new unsigned route fails until it is listed with its class.
  [engineering, 2026-10-12]
