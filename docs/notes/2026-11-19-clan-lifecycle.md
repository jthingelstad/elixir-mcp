# Member introductions, role changes and departures

Elixir's signed-in clan Timeline publishes a contextual introduction when the roster records an arrival. Leaders carry the same paragraphs into game chat. A promotion tribute can be edited until the leader confirms the promotion. Confirming an in-game decision, observing the role or roster change and recording each chat send are separate events.

The recorder writes a lifecycle row in the roster transaction for new observed arrivals, exits and Member–Elder transitions. The five-minute job composes paragraphs and raises eligible Actions. The membership start and previous role transition identify repeated occasions. Leader confirmations publish in the same transaction as the Action decision. The signed-in web reader overlays published words after checking current clan membership. The timeline used by public links and external readers keeps game words. Clan-only interactions retain their audience as the record grows.

Earlier timeline identities, Action decisions and receipts keep their historical interpretation. Existing agent-facing role and departure attestations keep their provenance.
