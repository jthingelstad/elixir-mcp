# 2026-11-24: Run is five tabs, and each page fails on its own

The last part of the Run redesign (the leaders' area of a clan), after
Members, Rules and leader exceptions shipped.

## What changed

- **Tabs.** Run's grouped side list is now five tabs across the top: To
  do, Members, Exceptions, Rules, Setup (`RunTabs` in
  `apps/web/src/clan/views/Run.jsx`). They wrap onto a second line on a
  phone (pills, 44px tall), so all five are in reach at 320px with no
  sideways scroll. To do counts the Actions waiting, Exceptions counts the
  ones a leader set (a member's own away is not counted), and Rules
  carries the unsaved-draft mark (a dot, or "!" when a change needs a fix).
- **To do** is the Actions inbox, with Waiting and Decided as two chips
  under its head (`/run` and `/run/actions` are Waiting, `/run/decided`
  Decided). Refresh suggestions sits in its head.
- **Setup** (`/run/setup`, new) lists Awards setup, Discord, Model & voice
  and Clan settings as rows. Each of those pages keeps its address and
  gets a "Setup" link back.
- **One back link on a phone.** The list's "Run" back link is gone: the
  tabs are always on screen. A member's page keeps its one "Members" link.
- **Per-panel errors.** `components/ReadError.jsx`:
  - `ReadError` says why a read failed in plain words (Elixir was slow,
    Can't reach Elixir, signed out, not yours to see, or didn't load),
    with Try again where trying again can help. Run's pages and setting
    panels use it in place of "could not be read just now".
  - `PanelBoundary` wraps the page under the tabs, so a page that throws
    while drawing says so in its place; the tabs and an unsaved rules
    draft above it stay. The kit's `ErrorBoundary` takes an optional
    `fallback` for this.
  - `useGated` keeps its last good answer when a refetch fails, and the
    clan's `me` read keeps its last answer too, so a slow background read
    no longer blanks a page that had drawn.

## Addresses

Every old address keeps working. `/run/review` still forwards to Members,
and any unknown `/run/<section>` now forwards to To do.

## Calls made in the build ([engineering])

- `/run` opens on To do at every width (the side list's phone view
  opened on the list).
- Decided keeps its name.
- Setup is a list of rows with a line each, not tiles with state chips.
  No "Who can run the clan" page. Clan settings stays one page (not split
  into Member map and Recording).
- The list's own Rules save is gone; the Rules tab is marked instead,
  and the sticky save bar on the Rules page does the saving.
- A failed background refetch keeps the stale answer without saying so.
