# 2026-11-06: Needs you reads alone; Latest stays the full timeline's

Second pass after the first (`2026-11-06-home-speed.md`). Production on the
worst-case account, six warm visits: `/clan` 3.9 s to 7.8 s, `/latest` 5.0 s to
10.5 s, and Clan's line had `clans_participation` at 1.6 s to 3.6 s. Needs you
waited on all of it, though it needs store reads only.

## Needs you

`GET /api/me/home/needs` answers `{ needs }`: Clan's `/needs` through the same
in-process door, gate and role checks as the card, with the unlock notice. Clan's
`needsView` is the part of `homeView` that was already store reads, and `homeView`
now calls it, so the `who.verified === false` rule is one line in one place. It
reads no participation and no roster. About 10 statements, against the card's 110.

- The card still carries the same answer. When the panel's read fails, the rows
  come from the card; when the card fails, they still come from the panel. Only
  when both fail is there no Needs you, and the card says it could not read.
- "Set up how your clan works" stays on the card's read: it compares the clan's
  size from the participation read to Clan's minimum. It comes last and only for
  a leader with no policy, so it can still open late.
- The Clan gate reads the size Clan last noted (`openActionsFor`), where `homeView`
  noted a fresh one just before. A clan crossing ten members shows the change a
  read later.

A visit now counts four on `home#<account>`, whose limit is four times the shared
bucket's, the same visits an hour. The visit can hold four leases at once for as
long as the needs read lasts, on a pool of `DB_POOL_MAX` 4.

## Latest

The same seven-day window as the timeline, and never a shorter one first. The
dedupe keeps an identical moment at its first instant in the window, so a repeat
of an arena move reads as new in a one-day window and as five days old in the
timeline. A sitting's standout is judged by what the window learned too. A test
pins the repeat case, and the top five against the full timeline.

A skip of players with nothing in the window (`newestItems`) shipped in #708 and
came out the same day. Production, six visits on the worst-case account: the checks
cost 479 ms to 2,483 ms to leave out one player, and the panel stayed at 5.9 s to
11.6 s. Latest is plain `buildTimeline` again.

`{"at":"home_panel","panel":"latest_parts","parts":{...}}` stays, with each
builder's ms. On those visits `clan.learned` (about 1.0 s to 2.0 s) and
`player.battles` (about 0.8 s to 1.7 s) lead, and no one read dominates. Making this
panel much faster means storing the feed rather than building it per visit, which
is not a small change to a read mail, the Timeline page and MCP share. It loads last
in its own panel, so one account's slow build holds up nothing else.

## The clan card

It reads whether the account tracks the clan directly, and only an untracked clan,
whose Track button needs them, reads `myClans`' slots. Four statements become two.
Nothing else: `clans_participation` is what's slow and its meaning is not touched.
The card's copy of the actions stays as Needs you's fallback.

## Defaults chosen in the build [engineering, 2026-11-06]

- Needs you has no failed state of its own: it falls back to the card's copy, and
  the card's failed words already cover both failing.
