# Stories from recorded play

Home groups visible friends’ sessions by the game’s event tag. A pair’s Together tab counts wins separately for the same side and opposite sides. A session marks a duel comeback only with three resolved games in loss, win, win order. A close tower finish shows a surviving tower’s reported HP, up to 100, from a winning singles battle. Migration 0309 stores the battle log’s C.H.A.O.S. modifier strings by participant. Previously discarded choices remain unknown unless a battle is collected again.

The member's Timeline features one clanmate and links to their player page. Find compares repeated collection decks only in the same mode and in the current verified player's clan. Card frequency uses up to 30 battles over 14 days. The ordinary roster and block rules determine who appears. Reading stories creates no follows or deliveries. Clan Feed joins closed race ranks, observed membership, positive deck use, pre-reset donations and tournament tags. The player page reads recorded Mastery steps, deck switches, collection levels and same-season progress.

A player initiates deck attribution. The member-only post reader checks visibility again and matches the verified player’s later battle against the post’s eight card IDs and its specified tower troop. The original post and the battle retain their own audiences. A copy-deck link cannot establish why a player used those cards.

A 12-win badge counter remains a player milestone. Assigning that completion to a particular event requires evidence connecting the two.

MCP 15.0.1 and JSON API 6.0.0. The composed stories are website reads.
