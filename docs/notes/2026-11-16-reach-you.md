# 2026-11-16 — The bell reaches you when you are away

Jamie, 2026-11-16: "overall the app doesn't have enough to bring people
back". He asked "should we be sending push notifications via the web?"
and "we need a way to let members know about activity on their games
without them logging in… if the email they get normally is weekly, would
we send something operational about engagement on their posts today."

One pipeline, two channels, and the bell's own items are the only source:
nothing is derived a second time. This note is the design for both; the
activity mail shipped first (#1071); web push followed, as built in
`docs/notes/2026-11-16-web-push.md`.

## The pipeline

The bell's reads about you (reactions, followers, answers) moved from the
web-api into `packages/tools/src/activity/bell.mjs`, so the bell, the
activity mail and the push drain read one function. `readAwayActivity`
reads the reactions and followers with `since` set to what the person
has been told already. The bell's week takes `maxAgeMs`, so a channel
sees an item made in the last few hours (the jobs read 15 minutes, the
bell's own freshness, not its three-hour serve).

A cheer's item id names a happening, not a player, so finding whose item
was cheered meant rebuilding every account's week. Migration 0280 adds
`timeline_cheer.subject_tags`, stamped by the cheer route from the item
as the cheerer's feed holds it. It is read only to find whom to ask;
what a channel says comes from the bell's read. Cheers before 0280 keep
null and never cause a mail.

## The activity mail: "Cheers on your play"

- Kind `activity_today`, bulk, its own switch on Settings › Emails, from
  Home. Event mail beside Clan's Actions mail, apart from the digest.
- On by default for person accounts: `ACTIVITY_MAIL_DEFAULT_ON` in
  `packages/contracts/src/queue.ts` is the one constant (read through
  `emailKindDefault` by the recipients' query and `GET /api/me/email`).
  Flip it to make the mail opt-in; a stored switch always wins.
- An hourly rule (`ActivityMailRule`, minute 7, `{"activity_mail":
  true}`) mails the accounts whose local hour is 18. The stack parameter
  `ActivityMail` (`on` by default, preserved) disables the rule.
- Who is asked: person accounts, approved, with an address and the switch
  on, whose own verified players had another person's cheer or a
  person's follow in the last day.
- What it tells: cheers per item and new followers per own player since
  the latest of the last bell look (`bell_seen`), the last activity mail
  and a day ago. With push, a notice the person opened moves its
  section's start too. Nothing new, nothing sent.
- Counts, never who: the follower is never named, though the bell names
  them. Item lines are the timeline's own text (game names, never a
  nickname), escaped. Each line links to the battle on Elixir's origin,
  else the player page.
- One a day: the ledger's issue is the person's local date. Tracking is
  the mail's one pixel and campaign tags, per mail.

## Web push (the plan; as built in its own note)

- A small service worker (`/sw.js`) whose only jobs are `push` and
  `notificationclick`; no fetch handler, no caching, so the app shell,
  SSR, sign-in and the cookie-free `/mcp` and OAuth doors are untouched.
- Settings › Notifications: turn on for this device (permission asked
  only after the tap), the account's devices with a remove, and a switch
  per kind. On iOS Safari not installed, how to add Elixir to the Home
  Screen instead. The bell carries one quiet, dismissible prompt.
- Kinds: cheers on your items (coalesced, "3 cheers on your 12-battle
  session", never who), a new follower (counted), a Clan Action that
  became yours, an answer to your feedback.
- Coalesced and capped per account so a busy evening is a few pushes.
  Tapping opens the item and marks the notice opened; an opened notice
  is not mailed again.
- Sending is the relay's (the only function with internet), from outbox
  objects the jobs drain writes, the mail path's shape. Gone
  subscriptions (404, 410) are pruned from the relay's receipts.
- The VAPID pair is in its own secret, `elixir-mcp/web-push`; the relay
  reads the private key at runtime (never an environment variable), and
  a stack parameter switches push off without a code change.

## Product questions, with the default taken

- **Send hour.** 18:00 in the account's zone (UTC when unset): the
  evening, after a day's play, so a day's cheers come together.
  [engineering]
- **Window.** At most a day back, and never what the bell, the last
  activity mail or an opened push already told. Activity after 18:00
  arrives the next evening. [engineering]
- **Followers by name.** Never in mail or push, though the bell names
  them by their verified player: the brief says counts, never who.
  [engineering, from the brief]
- **Which players.** Only the account's verified own players, as the
  bell. A claimed, unverified player gets no mail. [engineering]
- **Default on.** On for person accounts, one constant to flip.
  [Jamie, 2026-11-16, via the brief]
- **Answers and Clan Actions in the mail.** Not in it: each already has
  its own mail. [engineering]
- **Cheers on cheers before 0280.** Never mailed. [engineering]
