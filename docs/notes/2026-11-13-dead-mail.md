# 2026-11-13 — Retired mail's code goes

The clan report, the arena week, the milestone mail, Top 100 and Card of
the Week stopped sending earlier (`isRetiredEmailKind`, `foldedOff()`). Their
builders, renderers, specimens and preview op stayed behind. They go now.

## Removed

- Builders in `services/jobs/src/email/`: `build-arena.mjs`,
  `build-clan.mjs`, `build-milestone.mjs`, `clan-preview.mjs` and
  `shared.mjs`, with `ctx.mjs`'s tool registry (`makeRegistry`,
  `accountCtx`, `callTool`) and `digest.mjs`'s `windowItems`, which only
  they used.
- The `clan_report_preview` op: its jobs branch, its `JOBS_READS` entry and
  the ops skill's example (`series_metrics` is the example now).
- Renderers in `packages/mail/src/render.mjs`: `arena`, `clan` and
  `milestone`, and the helpers only they called (race rows, deck boxes,
  trend and activity blocks, the "you" chip, and `make()`'s `mine` and
  `verifiedClans` options). `deliver.mjs` no longer passes `mine` or
  `verified_clans`. `cards.mjs` loses `deckStrip` and `cardTiles`;
  `shell.mjs` loses `MODE_COLOR` and the "This email has ended" line.
- Specimens: `packages/mail/fixtures/{arena_week,clan_report,milestone}.json`
  and `packages/mail/test/fixtures/` (a clan report and a milestone). The
  preview's clan, arena and milestone variants went with them.
- Tests for the removed code (`email-arena.test.mjs`, the clan report and
  milestone cases in the jobs and mail suites).

## Kept, and why

- Every kind still sent: the digest (`tracking_report`),
  `collector_activity`, `clan_actions_waiting`, `feedback_answer`, sign-in
  and welcome mail.
- The retired kind names in `KIND_LABELS`, `MAIL_SOURCE` and `foldedOff()`:
  web-api labels stored sends and preferences with them.
- Stored rows (`email_milestone*` among them) and the `mail/sent/` archive.
  A sent mail's record page shows the archived HTML (`readSentMail`), so
  nothing re-renders a retired kind and its renderer is not needed to read
  it.

## Left for later

- `services/jobs/test/digest-fixture.mjs` still exports `milestoneFixture`,
  `PRIMARY`, `ALT`, `OPPONENT` and `WINDOW`, now unused. Another session is
  rewriting the fixtures, so this change does not edit them.
- `MILESTONE_MAIL_KINDS` in `packages/tools/src/friends-week.mjs` keeps its
  name; the digest reads it.

Settings › Emails' footer no longer promises milestone mail, and the comments
that said the digest goes out on Wednesday say Monday 11:00 UTC.
