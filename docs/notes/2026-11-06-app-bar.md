# 2026-11-06: The signed-in bar reads as the app

Jamie, reading the bar after the redesign: "it took me a beat to realize that this
was part of the Applications navigation", "it looks like site content navigation,
not the app", and one item was broken.

- **The broken item:** `.kit-top__icon` never reset the browser's button border.
  It has no border now, and an e2e test pins that.
- **Tabs:** `.kit-top__nav` carries the phone tabs' icons, stands the bar's full
  height, sits after a divider from the brand, and marks the current place in gold.
  The static pages' signed-in bar has the same icons.
- **Signed out:** `.kit-top__out` is unchanged, since those are site links.
- **Search under 1000 px** collapses to its magnifier, because the wider tabs left it
  a clipped sliver at about 800 px. Its words stay as its name.
