# Posting and play in the daily and weekly digests

Jamie approved counting who posted and who played separately in both digests. The subject and opening line use the same counts. A question and a post about a battle each count as one post. A player who posted several times still counts as one author. A player who also played counts in both groups.

The builder counts activity from the reader's existing digest sources before selecting twelve followed players and three items per player. The reader's own section sits outside those totals. The renderer keeps recorded trophy changes and progress when an author has no recorded battles. The same copy reaches the plain-text alternative.
