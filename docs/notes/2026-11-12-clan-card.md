# 2026-11-12 — Clan's head as a clan card, the race bar on the Feed (G5)

The canvas check of 2026-11-12 (G5) found Clan's head with no rule chips and
no race bar.

- **The clan's line.** Under the name: tag, members and open places, how the
  clan takes members (`type` from `clans_roster`: open, invite only, closed)
  and its trophy floor (`required_trophies`, said only above zero). Each part
  only when the roster read carries it. Who you are moves to its own line.
- **Rule chips.** `policyChips` (`clan-engine/src/render.mjs`) builds them
  from the values the leaders SAVED (`saved_values` beside the effective
  values in the manage service's `policyFor`), never from `effectivePolicyValues`:
  a field added since the save takes a starting value for judging, but the
  card shows no chip for it. Chips: Clan Wars intent (participating or not;
  unknown has none), the war and ranked minimums over the minimums window,
  the donation minimum (a weekly average), the trophy minimum, "Any one
  minimum" when two or more are met by any one, and the removal day
  (`removalDay`). The chips ride on `/api/clan/me`'s `policy` summary; the
  policy is public in the clan directory (A2). `policySummaryOf` is
  unchanged, so `clans_my_view` and MCP are too.
- **The race.** A new Clan read, `GET /api/clan/clans/<tag>/race`, for every
  member, injected from web-api like the Feed: `war_current` through the
  request's in-process invoker (no new recorded-client read), shaped by
  Home's `shapeRaceNow` (a place only when it is one) plus the count of
  clans, the policy grid's week end, and the clan's war decks this race week
  over four a war day for each current member up to the finish
  (`clan-race.mjs`). It is a pure view: no clan lock.
- **Weekly, not daily.** DECISIONS "War facts are weekly aggregates": the
  War tab shows no day's count, so the bar is the week's `decks_used`
  summed over current members, never `decks_today`.
- **The close.** `period.week_end_nominal`, said as "about" in the reader's
  zone: the race closes in the half hour before the 10:00 UTC grid, per race.
- **Order.** The Feed's timeline now also waits for the race read, as it
  waits for the other side reads (G3).

No contract, API version or migration change.
