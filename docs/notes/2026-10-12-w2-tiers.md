# 2026-10-12 — Tiers are earned in the game; the collector points economy retires (#530, #539)

MCP 11.11.3 (a correction: `elixir_collectors` keeps `points` and
`quota_credits`, always 0; `meta.quota.calls.max` has no collector credits;
the slot-refusal hints name the earned tiers). JSON API 3.2.0 unchanged.
No migration. From the accounts and collectors briefs Jamie ratified on
2026-10-12 (#525) and three DECISIONS lines: "Roles never gate visibility,
and a tier is earned in the game", "Running a collector earns the Family
tier, and nothing else" and "Collector points rewarded `new_facts > 0`,
and the economy retires".

## Jamie's answers

`docs/product/accounts.md`, principle 3 [Jamie, 2026-10-12]:

## Product questions and the answers taken

1. **What is "running a collector"?** [engineering, 2026-10-12] A
   collector whose gateway status is `active` (its first admitted submit
   made it so, #540). Probation, draining and revoked do not count. The
   brief: "running a collector earns the Family tier".
2. **What happens to a Leader or Family granted by hand before today?**
   [engineering, 2026-10-12] It is lost when its ground is not found:
   the stored `account.role` of `leader` or `family` no longer counts, and
   the tier is read from the game ("a tier earned is lost when its ground
   goes", #530). Admin shows the stale grant (Stored grant) and can move
   it to Member or Partner; it can never set Leader or Family
   (`tier_is_earned`, 400).
3. **What happens to someone above their tier's ceiling?** [engineering,
   2026-10-12] Nobody is cut and no row is deleted. The live count
   refuses only the next add, as it already does for an override
   lowered below use. Inferred from "slots are the only gate".
4. **Which Leader counts?** [engineering, 2026-10-12] One of the
   account's own players (primary or alt, never a watched one) whose
   claim is verified, in an open membership as leader or co-leader of a
   clan the account tracks. Verified because several accounts may claim
   one tag unverified (`packages/clan/src/gate.mjs`); the shape mirrors
   `packages/auth/src/clan-context.mjs`.
5. **Both grounds?** The higher earned tier wins: a leader who runs a
   collector is Family. Partner, admin and owner are grants and stand.
6. **Agents.** An agent's stored role was a clamped snapshot. Its tier
   now follows its owner's earned tier, capped at leader (DECISIONS:
   "Principals: users, agents, integrations"); integrations keep their
   provisioned role.

## What changed

- `packages/contracts/src/roles.ts`: `tierSql(alias)`, the tier as SQL,
  replaces `a.role` in every loader (OAuth token and service account,
  session, `/api/me`, the agents list, the claims pool, Admin, mail
  recipients). `familyGroundSql` and `leaderGroundSql` are its two
  grounds. `OPERATOR_BONUS` and the `operator` option are gone;
  `canSetRole` refuses Leader and Family; `ADMIN_SETTABLE` is Member and
  Partner.
- `packages/tools/src/quota.mjs`: the daily ceiling is the tier's base
  (or the override); the gateway points read is gone.
- `POST /api/me/role-request` is removed (404), with Profile's request
  form and Admin's pending-request chip. Profile's tier panel says what
  earns the next tier.
- Collectors: the ladder, `/api/me/gateways`, the collector page and
  `/api/public/status` no longer read points; the ladder orders by
  enrolment. `gateway.fetch_points` is a dead column, still counted by
  ingest; dropping it needs a migration and is not done here.
- The Sunday collector mail: each collector's week counts its fetches
  that added new facts (`new_facts`), and a "Your tier" paragraph
  replaces the points and credits lines. Stored issues with credits
  render without them.
- Census: `{tier_census: {dry_run: true}}`, a read-only migrate op
  (`services/migrate/src/ops-tier-census.mjs`; MIGRATE_READS
  `exactly({dry_run: true})`): every person's stored role, the tier read
  now and its ground, who moved and which way, hand grants lost, and who
  is above a ceiling. Accounts by id prefix and public id only.

## Kept on purpose

- `role_upgrade_request` stays in `contracts/src/queue.ts` and the
  email-relay templates so a notice already queued drains; removing them
  is later cleanup.
- `apps/site/src/_data/examples.js` keeps a real transcript that mentions
  points and credits: a transcript is a record of what was said.
- Historical What's new entries and notes are not edited.
