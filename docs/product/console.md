# Console

*Folded, 2026-11-12: the Console is no longer a surface of Elixir, so this
page keeps no brief of its own (DECISIONS "Console folds into You,
player pages and Settings" [Jamie, 2026-11-12]). It stays so that links
to it still land.*

Where its subjects live now:

- **A player's record and profile:** the player page's Record tab, in
  [Home](home.md).
- **Finding a player or a clan:** Find and the search sheet, in
  [Home](home.md); a clan's record is the clan directory, in
  [Clan](clan.md).
- **A clan's war weeks:** Clan's War tab, in [Clan](clan.md).
- **Your own season:** Stats, under You, in [Stats](ladder.md).
- **The account's machinery** (players and follows, mail switches, time
  zone, connections, agents, feedback): Settings, in
  [Accounts and privacy](accounts.md); an agent's page, in
  [MCP and agents](agents.md).
- **Running a collector:** Settings › Collectors, in
  [Collectors and operators](collectors.md).
- **Admin:** Settings › Admin, for Elixir's staff.

`/console` and every address under it only redirect
(`apps/web/src/lib/moved.js`).
