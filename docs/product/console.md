# Console

*Draft. Not yet ratified.*

## What it is for

The Console is where a person runs their account: what Elixir records
for them, what it sends them, who may act as them, and what happened
(their timeline). Ladder and Clan read the record back; the Console is
where the record is set up and where its machinery is visible. [Jamie,
2026-09-23, 2026-10-03]

## Who it serves

The signed-in person, and each of their agents: "the console switches
into an agent", a place of its own at `/console/agent/<id>/…` with the
same pages scoped to it. Integrations stay out; Admin is the
maintainer's. [Jamie, 2026-09-23]

## Principles that decide the details

1. **Orientation first.** A person opening Elixir understands it is a
   recorder and sees a clear next step: add your player, follow a
   friend, bring your clan, connect an agent. [Jamie, 2026-10-03]
2. **Tracked means recorded, and Tracking shows it.** Add a tag and
   watch the first capture land; a wrong tag is said within seconds with
   the fix in place; the primary's clan is followed for you; slots are
   the only gate. [Jamie, 2026-09-05, 2026-10-08]
3. **The person's switches live here.** Relationships, notifications per
   subject, every mail kind, time zone, connections and their grants,
   agents (never deleted: suspended or revoked), feedback and its
   answers. [Jamie, 2026-09-23, 2026-10-10]
4. **Time is the account's.** The console tells time in the account's
   zone, named; a new account starts on its signup browser's zone;
   day-bucketed charts stay UTC and say so. [Jamie, 2026-09-23,
   2026-10-08]
5. **Explore is the record, read directly, for power users.** "The web
   lets people explore their record": useful without an agent, no
   general BI platform or global game analysis. Explore stays a
   power-user corner, kept and linked but not polished; Ladder, Clan and
   the timeline are where ordinary members read the record. A battle has
   a public page; the console's status is signed in, public health is
   `/data/now`. [Jamie, 2026-09-25, 2026-10-01, 2026-10-02, 2026-10-12]
6. **Feedback is one system.** From anywhere in Elixir, into one record,
   answered by the maintainer (sometimes with help from AI agents), and
   mailed back once. [Jamie, 2026-10-08, 2026-10-11]

## What it never does

Expose another account's data; gate reading by role; carry a referral
id; run analytics the servers can see.
