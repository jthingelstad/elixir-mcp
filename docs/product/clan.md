# Clan

*Ratified by Jamie, 2026-10-12, in the sitting that drafted it.*

## What it is for

Elixir Clan is where a clan's leaders run the clan against its own
record, and where every member sees where they stand. "Elixir Clan is
the part of Elixir for being in a clan." It never acts in the game and
never decides for anyone; the leaders do, in the game. [Jamie, 2026-09-25,
2026-10-02]

It is for any clan, not for POAP KINGS: "every clan runs differently,
so Elixir Clan has no rules of its own." Nothing is judged until the
clan's leaders write its policy, and nothing clan-specific enters the
code. [Jamie, 2026-09-25]

## Who it serves

- **Leaders and co-leaders**, once verified: they write the policy,
  decide Actions, set holds and notes, set up awards and grant leaders'
  picks, connect Discord and the clan's own model key.
- **Elders**: welcomes, Scout, and the leaders' picks that allow them.
- **Every member**: the roster, their own numbers, the week, the season,
  standing as the clan lets them see it, award races and history, the
  map, Recruit, and their own away notice.
- **People, and each person's own agent.** Clan is behind sign-in.
  Today an agent gets one narrow read of the clan's war intent with an
  explicit grant [Jamie, 2026-10-04]; the direction is that a person's
  agent reads exactly what that person sees in Clan (the Actions waiting
  for them, their own standing line, the policy), and nothing more: never
  another member's clock or removal, and it never decides an Action.
  [Jamie, 2026-10-12]

A person is in Clan as the role they hold in the game, once their player
is verified; until then they read as a member. The record learns a role
change on its next roster read, so a leader's change waits for a roster
read within the hour. [Jamie, 2026-10-12]

Where a person lands follows the same roles. Asked whether leaders
should land on Actions instead of Roster, Jamie answered: "If there are
actions to take, yes. Otherwise no." A leader or co-leader with Actions
waiting for them lands on Actions; with none, they land on the roster,
as elders and members always do. [Jamie, 2026-10-20]

## Principles that decide the details

1. **The policy is the clan's agreement, and it is versioned.** Leaders
   write what the clan counts (Clan Wars, ranked play, donations, trophy
   road), the minimums, how Elder is earned, the clocks and which Actions
   to raise. Every save is a new version; evidence saved on an Action
   keeps the version that raised it. Presets are starting points, never
   defaults that run on their own. [Jamie, 2026-09-25, 2026-10-04]
2. **Standing is participation, not skill.** Wins, win rates and trophies
   won are not weighed. A clan may rank its members for Elder by its own
   weights; the result is shown as groups and evidence, never as a
   score, percentile or rank. Elixir itself rates no player; a clan's
   judgment is the clan's, under its policy. [Jamie, 2026-09-19, 2026-10-01]
3. **A change has to hold.** A promotion or demotion is raised only after
   consecutive reviews, and a declined one waits before it comes back.
   When the evidence is unknown, nothing is raised. [Jamie, 2026-10-01]
4. **Absence is stated, with what is unknown.** Inactivity is the days
   since the member's last recorded battle: "You have the entire battle
   log inside of Elixir MCP", and gaps in it "are very uncommon … only
   happening if a player plays a ton, which would never trigger an idle
   kick action". At risk matches the game's idle warning at seven; the
   removal Action comes at the policy's own day ("10 being the absolute
   number"); a member at risk is asked whether they are away. [Jamie,
   2026-10-12] The Action's evidence says what it rests on and what the
   record cannot see, so the leader weighs it. Clan never acts; the
   leader decides. [Jamie, 2026-10-12] This replaces the 2026-10-04 rule
   that no removal could be raised while all-mode absence was unproven,
   and the enforceability hold of 2026-10-12 (#426).

5. **Actions are atomic and human.** One Action is one call for one
   person or role: the evidence, the rule and policy version that raised
   it, the words to send. Make the change in the game, then mark it; the
   promotion and its message go together. Clan watches the record for the
   outcome and says whether it was confirmed. Nobody below co-leader sees
   a removal or the clock. [Jamie, 2026-10-03, 2026-10-05]
6. **Messages start in chat.** Routine recognition and updates default to
   clan chat; awards, standings and rule changes offer a durable Inbox
   alternative. A receipt is a human record of what was sent, never
   inferred, never resent. [Jamie, 2026-10-05]
7. **Awards are the clan's own, decided from the record.** A clan names
   up to twelve from five kinds; a season is judged once it is closed and
   whole; a computed decision is final; leaders' picks stay flexible.
   Weekly standings reach the clan as one Action; nothing posts to the
   game automatically. [Jamie, 2026-10-03]
8. **Clan drafts words; people decide.** The clan's own sealed key drafts
   messages from the record, told the clan, what happened and the member
   by name, never a tag, a note, a hold or a removal's rationale. A draft
   says at least what Elixir's own words would, then more of the member:
   "meaningfully more in-depth and personalized" (Jamie, 2026-10-20). A
   draft about one member may tell them their decks and win/loss counts,
   lifetime donations, years played, masteries and Path of Legends
   league; a demotion thanks them for older, positive history, never
   uses the recent window's numbers and never says why. In clan chat a
   draft is a few lines, each its own send. The clan has one voice, its
   leaders' words in Settings, for its drafts and its activity editor
   alike: given to the model as written, it sets the tone, never the
   facts, and lifts no rule. An Action's log keeps what the model wrote
   for its leaders and co-leaders; anyone else who reads the log sees
   only that a draft was made. Elixir Clan chooses the
   model for each use; the key and its monthly cap are the clan's. The
   one use nobody reviews is the activity channel's editor, so the
   engine's checks decide what it may post. [Jamie, 2026-10-03,
   2026-10-08, 2026-10-11, 2026-10-20]
9. **Facts go back to Elixir, labelled.** What Clan attests (a kick or a
   leave as a leader classified it, awards, where the races stand) is
   written apart from the game record, visible to the clan's verified
   members and their agents by the type's rule; an away only to its
   leaders. Private Clan state otherwise never reaches MCP. [Jamie,
   2026-09-25, 2026-10-02, 2026-10-04]
10. **Clan's Discord posts; the family's Discord agent converses.**
    Actions go to one channel as one message each, edited when they
    close. The clan's activity goes to a channel of its own as stories,
    one per moment, with no time, no link and no mention, written from
    the record or edited by the clan's model in a voice the leaders
    write. [Jamie, 2026-10-10, 2026-10-11] That is the posting half, for
    any clan through webhooks. Conversation (members asking questions,
    the ask lane) is elixir-mcp-discord's, and a clan that wants it runs
    the bot. [Jamie, 2026-10-12]

## What it never does

Act in the game; decide for anyone; post to the game; show a score,
percentile or rank; show a member another's removal or clock; carry a
POAP KINGS default; let a model complete or post an Action; move a
saved model. [Jamie, 2026-09-25 to 2026-10-11]

## How it fits the rest

Clan is part of Elixir: one session, one app, one web API, one Postgres,
one deploy; its code is `packages/clan*`. It reads Elixir through a
closed in-process reader with the person's own credential. A verified
leader or co-leader of a tracked clan earns the Leader tier, so their
clan can be recorded comprehensively, which is what gives Clan fuller
battle numbers. [Jamie, 2026-10-02, 2026-10-12]

## One clan at a time

A family (a main and its feeders) is a recording concept of the Family
tier. Clan sees one clan at a time: each has its own policy, standing,
Actions and awards, and a leader switches between them. [Jamie,
2026-10-12]
