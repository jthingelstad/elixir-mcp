# Clan leadership readiness for Monday

This release improves messages on existing actions. It does not change
participation calculations, policy, awards, membership or the season clock.
The [vision](../../VISION.md) gives the next product priorities.

## Complete by Sunday, October 4

1. Each leader and co-leader signs in with their own Elixir account, checks
   that their primary or alt is theirs and verified, and opens the right
   clan. Following another leader does not grant their role. Unverified
   accounts may read member views but cannot decide or draft actions.
2. Read the saved policy and version, then Standing and an action's evidence.
   Unknown capture stays unknown; do not infer inactivity from missing data.
   Check current roster roles and holds before making a decision in the game.
3. In Recruit, review the clan's own words. These supply the model's voice.
   In Manage → Settings, check safe model status: set, readable, owner still
   leads and in use. A key in a personal profile alone proves none of these.
   Do not copy or expose the stored credential.
4. On an open action, edit the template or ask for a draft with a fixed chat
   tone or a short Leader Message note without member details. Review the count and
   filter warnings, then copy into the game. A removal message is for after
   the human decision and actual game change. Drafting completes no action.
5. Mark the separate action only after doing it in the game. The log retains
   its policy/evidence and human decision; a welcome shares the edited words
   actually sent. Check the record's later confirmation, not just the click.
6. At season close, distinguish race finish from season rollover and allow
   temporary absence of the next race. Check completed-season awards and the
   morning evaluation/mail receipts without rerunning them for verification.

Finish readiness and validation on Sunday, not Monday's business day.
The connected clock reads on October 3 place the nominal season/week boundary
at Monday, October 5, 10:00 UTC (05:00 CDT), with POAP KINGS in the final
Colosseum week. The actual race close varies within 09:30–10:00 UTC
(04:30–05:00 CDT); the nominal boundary is not an exact attack deadline.
Verify the clock and observed race rather than assuming they coincide.
See [Elixir's policy-day evidence](https://elixir.poapkings.com/docs/clocks#the-policy-day)
and [Supercell's first-Monday season rule](https://support.supercell.com/clash-royale/en/articles/seasons.html).
