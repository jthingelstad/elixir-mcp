# The family pages are gone

- The family pages at `/family` are gone, and nothing links to them
  any more. A clan's own Discord bot is described on
  [Building an agent](/docs/agents).
- [Privacy](/docs/privacy) now says plainly which facts Elixir adds on
  top of the game: Clan's, like a clan's awards and a leader's note on
  why a member left. Each one says where it came from.
- [Roles](/docs/roles) and [Users and agents](/docs/connections) say
  it plainly: you have one MCP endpoint for your own agent, and a
  clan's agent has one of its own.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
