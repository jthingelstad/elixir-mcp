# Late battles reach Home

Home's **N new** button now counts a battle Elixir records late, even one that belongs further down your feed, so it no longer slips in unseen. And when you tap the freshness chip to check your own battles, the new ones show in your feed straight away; the button is for the times Elixir checks on its own.

MCP 13.2.2 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
