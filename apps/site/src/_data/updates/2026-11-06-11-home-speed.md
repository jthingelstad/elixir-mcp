# Home opens first, then fills in

Home now draws as soon as Elixir knows who you are. Your week, your clan and Latest each read on their own and show their own loading state, so a slow one no longer holds up the others, and one that can't be read says so while the rest carry on. Your week also does less work. The head of the page now calls your player by your nickname, as Ladder and Friends do.

MCP 11.18.3, JSON API 3.4.0 unchanged.
