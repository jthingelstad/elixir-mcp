# Your digest replaces Your friends this week

Wednesday's email is now **Your digest**: what the people you follow played
and posted, then your clans and your own stretch. Each person comes with
their battles and record, their highlights first in their own words, and
the big firsts (a new arena, a league, a clan joined or left), each with a
link to the battle that did it. Friends come first.

It comes weekly on Wednesdays by default. In Settings › Emails you can make
it daily, or turn it off. It never comes empty: a week, or a day, when no one
you follow played or posted, and neither did you, sends nothing.

It uses the old email's switch, so if you had turned Your friends this week
off, the digest is off too, and an old turn-off link still works.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
