# Who writes Elixir's words, one clan at a time, and who talks in Discord

The documentation now says three things plainly. Elixir's own words, in every email, on the timeline, on Ladder and on this site, are written from the record by code, and Elixir pays for no language model; a model writes only where someone brings their own, a clan's key in Elixir Clan or your own AI agent. Elixir Clan works in one clan at a time by design: each clan has its own policy, standing, Actions and awards, and a leader whose players are in several clans, such as a main and its feeders, switches between them. In Discord, Clan posts the clan's Actions and activity through the webhooks its leaders connect, and conversation belongs to the Discord bot a clan runs on its own keys; Clan answers no questions there, and every decision is still made in Elixir. Nothing about how Elixir works changed. MCP 11.11.2 and JSON API 3.2.0 unchanged.
