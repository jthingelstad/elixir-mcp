# Clan signs you in like the rest of Elixir

Open a Clan page while signed out and you now get the same **Sign in
first** page as Home and every other page of the record. Sign in and
you land back on the Clan page you asked for.

If your player holds Elder, Co-leader or Leader in the game but is not
verified yet, the note you see after signing in is now Elixir's own
**Verify your player**, the same one You shows, with **Continue** to go
on as a member.

Who sees what in Clan is unchanged. MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
