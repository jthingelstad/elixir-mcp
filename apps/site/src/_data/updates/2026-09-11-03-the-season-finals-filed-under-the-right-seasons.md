# The season finals, filed under the right seasons

This morning's finals backfill fetched each season's final board by a bare number and took that number for the season the game clock counts — it is the position in the API's own list of seasons, eight higher. What the record called the S135 final was December 2025's; the eight finals since, January through August 2026, were never fetched. Every held final is relabelled (S89 through S127, October 2022 onward), the missing eight are being fetched now, and finals are fetched by the API's own name for a season from here on — the month it started in. That name is also accepted as the season argument (2026-08 beside 135), and the snapshot carries both. Contract 1.5.0. For the record there are three season numberings in play: the game clock's (S136 is September 2026), the in-game Pass's (Season 87, which the API never mentions), and the API's YYYY-MM.
