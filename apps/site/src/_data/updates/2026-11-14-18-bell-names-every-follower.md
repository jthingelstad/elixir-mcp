# The bell names every follower

A new follower who hasn't verified their player yet now shows in the bell
by the player they say they are, marked **Not verified**, with no gold
mark. Tap **Follow back** as you would for anyone.

Only accounts with no player at all are still counted, not named. It's the
same as the followers list on your player page.

MCP 13.2.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
