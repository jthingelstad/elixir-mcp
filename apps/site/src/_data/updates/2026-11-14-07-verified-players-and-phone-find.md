# Verified players stand out, and Find fits your phone

Verified players now wear a gold avatar ring and a gold check beside their
name. The ring works with card art or initials, including when an image
cannot load. Picking an image alone never gives a player a verified mark.

Find’s choices stay visible on a phone, with flatter people lists and
comfortable Follow buttons. Cheers open with a short motion that respects
your reduced-motion setting.

MCP 13.1.1 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
