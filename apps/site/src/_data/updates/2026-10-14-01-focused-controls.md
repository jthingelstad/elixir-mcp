# Fewer setup fields, clearer everyday pages

Clan Discord shows the channel, what it posts and its voice as three rows.
Manage opens the controls you need; closing discards unsaved text. Posting
problems remain visible, and deliveries are still available on the page.

Emails from Elixir lists each mail type with its schedule and switch. Recruit
opens on editable copy for the format you choose and keeps each format’s draft
while you switch. The pitch and full clan facts expand below it.

Profile, member activity and awards put repeated explanations behind help
controls, with record gaps, partial evidence and action consequences still in
view. Away and Scout use shorter introductions.

MCP 11.13.0; JSON API 3.2.0. Contracts unchanged.
