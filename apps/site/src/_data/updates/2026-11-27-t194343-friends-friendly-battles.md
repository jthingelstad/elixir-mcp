# Your friends' friendly battles

People to follow and Find's Suggested now include players your friends had a friendly battle with.

- If a friend you follow played someone in a friendly battle in the last 30 days, that player can show up for you. You might know them too!
- One friendly battle is enough. The players your friends played most come first.
- Each says who and how many: "Ada played 3 friendly battles with them".
- Your own players, people you already follow and your clanmates are never suggested.
