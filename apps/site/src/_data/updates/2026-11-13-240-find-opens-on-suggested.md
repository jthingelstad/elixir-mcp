# Find opens on Suggested

Find now opens on **Suggested**: a list of people to tap through, each
with a line on why. "You played a friendly battle", "Your 2v2 teammate",
"Ada follows them", "In your clan".

- Ten show first. Tap **Show more** for up to 30.
- Every row says what following costs, and asks before it follows.
- Nobody to suggest yet? It says what fills the list, with Search and
  your Follow me link right there.

Search is still a tab. It finds players Elixir has seen by name or tag,
and **Follow by player tag** follows anyone else.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
