# Your clan's house rules

A clan's **Policy** tab now reads like house rules. Each rule gets its
own card: Minimums, When you go quiet, Becoming an Elder, and the rest.
The numbers that matter sit up top, big enough to read at a glance.

On **Standings**, your group now shows the rule behind it. Tap it to
jump straight to that rule.

Leaders: when you change a setting in Run, it says what the saved policy
had. The save bar stays small and out of the way, and Versions shows the
one in use.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
