# Your profile on Home: six steps to the All Set Stamp

Home's welcome steps are now **Your profile**: six steps, with the one to
do next on top and the Stamp it earns beside it. Tap **See all steps** for
the rest.

Add your player, verify it's you, pick your picture, follow someone who
plays, choose your email, and set your place on the clan map (or tap
**Not now**). All six earn **All Set**.

The follow step tells "they haven't played this week" apart from "Elixir
is still catching up", so it never guesses. Finished the older welcome?
You see only what's left, and **Hide** puts it away.

MCP 13.1.1 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
