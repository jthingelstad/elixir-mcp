# Pick your @handle, and You is your account

Your account can have an @handle now. It's optional.

- Pick one in Settings › Profile. Elixir suggests one from your main player's name.
- 3 to 20 letters, numbers or _. Nobody else can have yours.
- Members see it on your account page, in follow lists and in search. `/home/@yourhandle` opens your page.
- Change it or remove it any time.

Tap **You** and you land on your account page: your players, your Stamps and your timeline. Each player opens its own page, and **Your stats** is one tap away.

MCP 13.3.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
