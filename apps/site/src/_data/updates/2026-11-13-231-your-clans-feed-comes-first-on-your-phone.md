# Your clan's feed comes first on your phone

On a phone, a clan's **Feed** now shows the clan's timeline right under
the race, with the clan's week and your own numbers after it. When
nothing is waiting for you, that's one short line instead of a big box,
with **Refresh suggestions** still beside it. The chips under the clan's
name sit in one row you can swipe sideways, instead of four rows.

In the [clan directory](/clan/directory), every filter but search folds
behind **Filters** on a phone, so the clans are on your first screen. It
says how many filters are set.

On a wider screen only the short line is new. Nobody sees or can do
anything new.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
