# Post a shared highlight in your clan's Discord

When your clan's leaders turn on **Shared highlights** for the clan's
Discord channel, a highlight you have shared can go there too: choose
**Post in your clan's Discord** on the battle's page. It is posted once,
with the player's game name, the result, your words and the link, and
the link shows no preview. Leaders find the switch in Clan's Social ›
Discord; it starts off.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
