# A Timeline with rhythm

Your Timeline now gives the big moments room, and lets the quick ones step back.

- **A short session is one line**: who, when, the result and its trophies, and its Cheer. Tap it to open the session.
- **Short sessions in a row fold into one row**, like "4 short sessions from Pip, Ash and 2 others". Tap it to open them in place. Each keeps its own cheers.
- **A close friend's sessions never fold**, and their items show the small star only you see.
- **Moments carry their art.** A new card, Evo, Hero or Card Mastery shows the card's own art. A new best, a new league in Ranked and a new Arena read as the day's headline.
- **Clan's Feed draws the same way**, with the same pieces as your Timeline.

Nothing moves: the feed stays newest first, and the chips, the counts and **New since your last visit** work as before.

MCP 13.4.1 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
