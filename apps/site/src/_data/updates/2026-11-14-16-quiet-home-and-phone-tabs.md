# A quiet Home says why, and tabs stay in one row

When your week on Home is empty, it now tells you why: the players you
follow have been quiet, or Elixir is still catching up on them. Tap
**Find your people** for players from your battles and your clan, each
with why. **People to follow** moves up under that line, so on a phone
you see it first.

**People you know** stays at the top of Home until five of the players
you follow have played in the last 7 days, so following quiet players
doesn't put it away.

On a phone, the tabs on a player's page, in Clan and across Elixir stay
in one row. Swipe the row for the rest, and an arrow shows the side that
has more.

MCP 13.1.1 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
