# Exceptions replace holds in Clan

Leaders can now tell the rules to leave a member alone in one way, until a date: Keep in clan, Keep as Elder, Not for Elder, or Away. Each one ends, and leaders pick whether only leaders or the whole clan can see it. Find them under Run › Exceptions.

A kept Elder doesn't take one of the earned Elder seats. Every hold became an exception: a member's own away is now an Away the clan can see, and a leader's hold is a leaders-only Keep in clan. A hold with no end date now ends 90 days from today.
