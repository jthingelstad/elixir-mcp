# Who sees what in your clan, on every rule

Each rule on a clan's **Policy** tab now ends with a quiet line saying
who sees what about you under it: your weekly numbers, where you stand,
your days without a battle and your away. Under the rules, it says who
reads notes about a member. The policy's past versions are now for the
Leader and Co-Leaders only; everyone reads the policy as it stands.

**Closed in the last 30 days** is gone from Actions for members and
Elders. Closed Actions are for the Leader and Co-Leaders, under Run's
Waiting and Decided. A closed Action's number answers that there's no
such action, unless you lead the clan.

Your digest has a new **Clan news** switch under it in Settings ›
Emails. Turn it off and the digest leaves out your clans' races, joins,
departures and role changes, and tells a clanmate you follow who joined,
left or changed role under them instead.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
