# Find and the clan directory wear the new look

Find is drawn the way the approved design draws it: your clan opens as a
panel with its crest and a gold top, each clanmate shows their role and,
in green, when following them is free, and Following is a pill. How many
of your follow slots are in use sits beside every Find section. The clan
directory's filters are joined button rows, each clan's tile carries its
crest, and Plays Clan Wars is picked out in gold; your own clan's tile has
the gold top.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
