# Home's feed comes thirty at a time, and reads faster

Home's feed now shows the newest thirty items of your last seven days, with
**More** for the next thirty. The counts on the chips are still the whole
week's. A busy week was up to 150 items read at once, which could take
longer than the page waits; when that happened the feed stayed on
"Reading…".

The read behind it is quicker too: the players you follow are read together
rather than one after another, and your clan's line no longer checks every
battle anyone's players played that week to find the clan's.

If the feed doesn't load, it says so and offers **Try again** instead of a
blank space. Your week, your clan and People to follow read on their own
beside it either way.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
