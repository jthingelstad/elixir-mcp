# A clanmate's Clan record is on their player page

A member's page in Clan has moved onto their player page, as a **Clan**
tab. It shows what the member page showed: their last battle in the
clan, their standing, war decks race by race, week by week and recent
battles here. It appears only when you have a player in that clan. Leaders and
co-leaders still get the activity clock with notes and hold; nobody
else does.

Old member addresses in Clan, from Review, an Action's "Open their page"
or mail, open the player page on that tab. Your own place in a clan
keeps its address.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
