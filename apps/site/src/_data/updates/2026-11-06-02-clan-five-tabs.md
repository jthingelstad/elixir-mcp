# Clan has five tabs

Clan's left rail is gone. Every clan now reads across five tabs: Today for what is waiting and how the week is going, Members for the roster, Standing, Review, the map, Recruit and Scout, Season for the running week and every closed week and season, Awards for the races and the trophy case, and Run, for the leader and co-leaders, with the policy section by section and the clan's settings. Everyone lands on Today. If you are in more than one clan, the picker sits beside the clan's name and keeps you on the page you were on. Old addresses still arrive, and links in mail still open their action.

Discord stories now say "Season 136, week 2 finished in 1st place", and a departure no longer counts the days. On a phone the five tabs fit one row.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
