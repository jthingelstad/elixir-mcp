# A busy digest is never cut off

Some email apps cut a long email short and hide its end. When a lot of the people you follow played, your digest now keeps itself short enough to arrive whole.

- The first friends get their sessions in full.
- Later friends' sessions are one line each.
- In a very busy digest, the last friends get a count of their sessions and a link to their page, and their highlights and firsts are counted with a link to your timeline.

The links at the bottom, including turning the email off, are always there.

MCP 13.4.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
