# Clan wears the new look

A clan's page opens on a card with its crest, the clan's name in gold and
your place in it above, and its war standing and rules as chips. The race
bar sits beside This week and You here, with a gold fill. Decisions waiting
for a leader keep the dashed edge that only a leader's decision gets. On
Members each player has their avatar, Follow everyone is the gold button,
and table heads across Clan are small capitals. Who sees which tab is
unchanged.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
