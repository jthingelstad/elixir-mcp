# Run › Members, and each member's history

Leaders and co-leaders have a new **Members** page in Run, in place of Review. It shows everyone and where they stand: Elder seats taken, who did their part, and who has gone quiet. Filter by who needs you, who's getting close, who has too little on record, and who has an exception.

Tap a member to see their page: their last battle and kick clock, Elder rank, donations beside the clan's median, their notes and hold, and their history in the clan, newest first. It shows joins, role changes, Actions, race weeks, awards and notes, only as Elixir recorded them. [The leaders pages](/docs/clan-leaders-pages) has the details.
