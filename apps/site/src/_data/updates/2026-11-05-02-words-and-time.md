# Plainer words, and one way of saying when

Every signed-in page now says when something happened the same way, on your clock: "Sat 8:42 pm" within a week, "Fri Oct 23, 5:07 pm" further back. A war week reads "Season 137, week 4". The words got plainer too: the record, not the corpus; a read, not a poll or a capture; your limits, not your tier. Casual battles are called Casual everywhere, and Elixir Clan is Clan. Standing now says what counts instead of what to do. Support says what a collector raises and how long history stays, and the live examples no longer use real friends' names.

MCP 11.15.0 and JSON API 3.3.0, unchanged.
