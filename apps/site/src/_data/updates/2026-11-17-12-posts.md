# Posts and questions

You can now write a post of your own on your Timeline, to the people who follow you and your clanmates.

- **Tap Write a post or ask a question** at the top of your Timeline, or **Post** on a phone.
- **Ask a question** marks it a Question, and people answer under it.
- **Paste a deck link** from Clash Royale's **Copy deck** and the post shows the deck: the cards' art, its name, its average elixir and a Copy deck button.
- **Add cards** by name, up to 8.

Posts are words only, up to 500 characters, with no links or photos. Every post is checked before it goes up, and it can be cheered and answered. Your posts are also on your Profile and your player's page. Treat a post as public: for now it shows on the Timeline, in your clan's Feed and on those pages.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
