# A cheer is one tap

Cheering used to take two taps: **Cheer** opened the six, then you chose one. Now **Cheer** cheers.

- **Tap Cheer** and the item is cheered with **Well played** at once. Tap it again to take your cheer back.
- **The smile beside Cheer** opens all six in a row under the item, to cheer with another or change yours. On a phone, holding **Cheer** opens them too, and with a keyboard the arrow keys move along them.
- **Yours stands out:** Cheer turns violet and shows your cheer.

The six have new glyphs, drawn the same way on your Timeline, a session's page, a battle's page, a player's page and in Notifications: a thumbs up for Well played, a flame for On fire, a crown for Royal, sparkles for Wow, a laughing face for Ha and a flexed arm for Grind. Counts still say how many, never who.

A session's page now takes a cheer too, while the session is in your Timeline. And across Elixir, back and forward links, stars and checks now use the same glyphs as the rest of the app.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
