# Account pages, and who claims a player

Every player is now **Verified**, **Claimed** or **Imported**. Verified players keep their gold ring. Claimed players, where an account says it is that player but hasn't proved it, wear a new grey ring. A player's page now says which account verified or claims it.

Tap that account to open its **account page**: the player they say they are, their alts, their Stamps and one timeline of everything they do. Tap **Follow** to see their Stamps, places and verified players on your Home.

Verifying is the big one: once you verify a player, nobody else can claim it.

MCP 13.2.2 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
