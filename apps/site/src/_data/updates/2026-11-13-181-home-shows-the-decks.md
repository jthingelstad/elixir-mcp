# Home shows the decks

A session on Home now shows the eight cards of the deck it was played with
most, each with its elixir cost and its Evolution or Hero form marked. A
highlight shows both decks side by side with the score between them and
the player's words on the gold rule, and a moment's battle says which mode
it was played in. A session that ran past midnight says both days.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
