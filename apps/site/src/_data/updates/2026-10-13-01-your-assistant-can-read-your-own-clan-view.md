# Your assistant can read your own view of your clan

What you connect to Elixir as yourself, Claude or another assistant signed in as you, can now read your own view of your clan in Elixir Clan with `clans_my_view`: the clan's policy summary, your own standing line, and the Actions waiting for you, each with a link to open and decide it in Clan. It needs a player you have verified who is a current member of the clan, and it reads Clan's last evaluation. It never shows another member's line or clock, never a removal Action (to a leader either), and never an Action's evidence or log, and it never decides anything. An agent or an integration does not get it: it is yours, with no switch to grant it. See [Your own view](/docs/clan-policy#your-own-view). MCP 11.12.0 (`clans_my_view`); JSON API 3.2.0 unchanged.
