# Your RSS feed is kept for up to an hour

Your feed reader checks your RSS link all day. Now Elixir keeps a copy of your feed for up to an hour and answers your reader from it, so a check inside that hour never waits for Elixir to read your week.

What that means for you:

- A new battle or post can take up to an hour longer to show up in your reader.
- Removed posts, reports and blocks also reach your feed within an hour.
- When you reset your link, turn it off or close your account, the old link stops within an hour, not at once.

Your Timeline in Elixir is not affected. It is as fresh as before.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
