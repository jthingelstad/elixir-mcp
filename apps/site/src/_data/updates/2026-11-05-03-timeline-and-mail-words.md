# The timeline and mail say it plainly, and a week has one battle count

Timeline lines now read the way a person would say them, the same in the app, in mail, for agents and in Discord: "played 10 battles in one sitting", "finished Season 137, week 4 in 2nd place". A departure says "departed (was Member)", and "was removed" once a leader says so. How long someone stayed is gone from those lines. Mail subjects name the season week instead of dates, mail times read like the rest of Elixir in your own time zone, and Clan's leader pages say "In line for promotion" and "Another member takes the seat". Slipping and Rising now say what they mean: a demotion or a promotion building.

Clan's The week, the clan report and Your friends this week now show the same battle count for a clan's week: from Monday's reset to the next, by when each battle was played, each battle once.

MCP 11.16.0 and JSON API 3.3.0, unchanged.
