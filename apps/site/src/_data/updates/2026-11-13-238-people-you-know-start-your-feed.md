# People you know start your feed

While you follow fewer than five players, Home now opens with **People
you know**: your clanmates, the players you had a friendly battle with and
your 2v2 teammates. Each says why ("You played a friendly battle") and
what following them costs.

Everyone is ticked. Untick anyone you'd rather not follow, then tap
**Follow these**. Nothing is followed until you tap, and nobody is told.
**Not now** puts it away for 30 days.

People to follow and Find's From your battles now put friendly battles
and 2v2 teammates first too. And when someone new follows you, the bell
has **Follow back**.
