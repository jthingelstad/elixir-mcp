# Every session has its own page

A session on Home reads like a highlight now.

- Its headline says what happened: "Won 8 of 12 on Trophy Road, 5 in a row".
- Chips show three-crown wins and your best win. The card names every deck you switched to.
- One or two battles make a smaller card, so the big sessions stand out.

Tap a session to open its own page. You get every deck you played and every battle, newest first: the score, who you played (tap them for their page) and their deck, the trophies, and the battle's page. Battles that stood out say so, like **Three crowns** or **5th win in a row**. On your own session, **Highlight** is one tap from each battle.

MCP 13.3.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
