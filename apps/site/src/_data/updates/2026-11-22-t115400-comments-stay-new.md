# Comments on your items, in My conversations

When someone comments on your items or posts, that thread now shows in **My conversations** too. Find it in the bell.

The comment stays **New** until you open it. Opening the bell doesn't clear it anymore, so a comment you never scrolled to won't slip by. Tap it to go straight to that item's comments.

Your bell also lists the newest first now, whatever kind of line it is.

MCP 15.4.0 and JSON API 6.0.0.
