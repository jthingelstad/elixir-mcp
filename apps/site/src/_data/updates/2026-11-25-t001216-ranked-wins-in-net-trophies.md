# Ranked wins in net trophies

For agents: `battles_performance` and `battles_compare` now say how much of `net_trophies` came from Ranked below Ultimate Champion. There a win reports +30 and a loss reports nothing, so that part counts wins only. It is league progress, not trophies won and lost.

The new field is `unrated_ranked_trophies`, beside `net_trophies`. A note tells you when it is above 0. [Reading the numbers](/docs/reading-the-numbers) has the details.
