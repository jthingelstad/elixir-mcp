# Clan is one view: Feed, Standings, War, Members, Policy and Run

Clan's tabs are now **Feed**, **Standings**, **War**, **Members** and
**Policy** for every member, and **Run** for the clan's leader and
co-leaders.

- **Feed** is where a clan opens: the clan's last seven days, newest
  first, with its members' highlights and their words, and what waits on
  the clan.
- **Standings** is what Awards was, and **War** is what Season was.
- **Policy** reads the clan's policy to every member: how Elder, the
  removal clock and the rest work here, and Elder's weights.
- **Run** holds the leaders' tools: Actions (Waiting and Decided), the
  policy editor, Discord, the model and its budget. Anyone else who opens
  it is told it is for the leader and co-leaders, and the server refuses
  them too.
- A name in Clan opens that player's page.

Every old address still works and lands on its new tab, so links in mail
and in Discord keep opening. On a phone the tabs scroll sideways.

MCP and the JSON API are unchanged.
