# Prepare clan rules and inspect member activity

Verified leaders can prepare Policy before a clan reaches ten members; operational management stays paused until then. The editor explains minimums, preset effects and save timing, clears stale previews and protects newer versions. Roster names and member Actions link to clan-scoped recorded battles, session groups, weekly contributions, war usage and capture evidence. Award races and Award history distinguish current standings from earned records. Switching clans keeps navigation current. Contracts unchanged.
