# The bell opens faster

The bell no longer rebuilds your week each time it opens. It keeps your
verified players' items from the last seven days and refreshes them in
the background, at most fifteen minutes behind while you use Elixir.
Cheers on items it holds, highlights, followers, answers and Clan
Actions are read fresh every time; cheers on an item made since the last
refresh reach the bell once it refreshes.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
