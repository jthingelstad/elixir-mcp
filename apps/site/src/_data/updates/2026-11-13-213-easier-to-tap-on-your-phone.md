# Easier to tap on your phone

On a phone, every button and link at the top and bottom of Elixir is now
easy to hit with a thumb: the logo, help, your account and the links at
the foot of each page. They look the same; they just take a bigger tap.

The same goes for players' names on Home, in Find and in your clan's
lists, **Open the battle** in a clan's feed, the cards in a battle's
decks, and the 13-or-older choice when you create an account.

On a battle's page, **Side by side** now fits your screen instead of
scrolling sideways.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
