# Battles Elixir learns late show up now

Sometimes Elixir only hears about battles a day or more after they were played, like right after you tap **Check for new battles**. Those battles now show on the player's page and in your Timeline at the time they were played, in the same session as the rest.

Nothing announces them as new: they're not mailed, pushed or posted to Discord as if they just happened, and an old batch never counts as a player coming back.
