# Clan's Feed comes in pages, with your panels beside it

Clan › **Feed** shows the clan's last seven days thirty lines at a time, with
**More** for the next thirty, instead of every line at once. **You here** and
**This week** now sit in a column beside the feed on a computer and above it
on a phone, so they are no longer below a long week.

The feed now waits for those panels and what is waiting for you, and starts
once they have answered. Read together, the four slowed each other down until
all of them timed out, so the panels could stay on "Reading…".

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
