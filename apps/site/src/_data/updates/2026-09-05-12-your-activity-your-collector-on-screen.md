# Your activity, your collector, on screen

Account gained two pages. Activity shows three views of your own account: every MCP request your agents made (tool, timing, errors - the debugging view), your account's event history, and your notification pipe (with unread rows past your agents' cursor bolded; reading here never marks anything seen for them). Collector shows what your machines have actually done: a 30-day fetch chart per collector, endpoint mix, points, and the running version.
