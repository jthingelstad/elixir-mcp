# Clan fits your phone

On a phone, all of a clan's tabs now fit across the screen. No more
swiping sideways to find Run.

The **clan directory's** filters each take a full row, and the removal
filter shows all four choices at once.

Small controls are easier to tap: the switches in **Settings › Emails**,
the digest's Weekly, Daily and Off, and the checkboxes and buttons in
**Run**.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
