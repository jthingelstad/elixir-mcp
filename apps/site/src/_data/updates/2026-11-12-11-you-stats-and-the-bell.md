# You with Stats, a fuller bell, and a simpler way in

**You** is now your own player's page: once you have verified your main
player, its Timeline, Highlights, Moments and Follows, with a fifth tab,
**Stats**. Stats is what Ladder was, unchanged: the scope bar and Season,
Battles, Decks and Cards, at `/home/you/stats`. Every old Ladder address,
in an old email too, opens the same page there. Your other players are
listed under You, to you alone; with no verified player, You says how to
verify one.

The **bell** holds what is about you, not your friends: the cheers others
gave your items this week (counts, never who), accounts that started
following your players (named by their verified player, otherwise counted),
Needs you as before, and answers to your feedback. Its dot shows what came
since you last opened it. Nothing in it is mailed or sent to Discord.

Signed out, the top bar is **Docs**, **Sign in** and **Create account**, and
the front page is the way in. **Cards** and **Updates** are in the footer, as
public as before. **Play Drop** left the top bar for your account menu and
the footer.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
