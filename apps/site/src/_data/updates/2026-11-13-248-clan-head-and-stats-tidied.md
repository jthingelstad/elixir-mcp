# Clan and Stats, tidied

More polish.

- Clan's head is shorter on a phone. Its line wraps between facts, never
  inside one, **Roster read** sits at its end, and **Follow** shares a
  row with the clan picker.
- Clan's six tabs scroll sideways, like every other tab row, instead of
  squeezing together.
- Stats keeps its chrome to one quiet line on a phone, and the Battles
  tile's "since" date no longer breaks mid-time.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
