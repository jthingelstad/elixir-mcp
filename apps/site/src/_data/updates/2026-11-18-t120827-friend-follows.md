# One way to follow a player

Every other player you follow is now a friend. Watching has become Friend,
with your player tags, private nicknames and notification choices kept.
Your recording and player slots stay the same.

Find all those players under Friends, open their Stats, and keep up with
them in your Timeline and digest. The change sends no new follower notice.

MCP 14.0.0; JSON API 6.0.0.
