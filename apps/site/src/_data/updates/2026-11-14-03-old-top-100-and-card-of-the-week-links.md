# Old Top 100 and Card of the Week links

- The Top 100 and Card of the Week emails are gone for good. If an old
  one is still in your inbox, its turn-off link now says the email has
  ended, with nothing to turn off, and points you to your
  [email settings](/docs/email).
- Those emails still show in your list of sent emails, as "Retired
  email", and still open.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
