# Profile images

- Pick a Clash Royale card as your picture in
  [Settings › Profile](/docs/your-account#your-profile-image).
- It shows on the players you've verified, your main and your alts: in
  feeds, on player pages, in Find, in clan lists and on shared posts.
- Players nobody has verified keep their initials. Remove yours any time.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
