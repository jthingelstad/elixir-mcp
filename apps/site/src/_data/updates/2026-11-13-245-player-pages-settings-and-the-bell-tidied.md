# Player pages, Settings and the bell, tidied

More polish. Nothing you can do changed.

- A player's page lights **Find** in the bottom tabs, since that is where
  you went looking for them.
- A player Elixir has not recorded yet reads "Player" with their tag
  beside it, and **Follow** is right there.
- On a player's page, **Following** is plain words, not a button shape.
- On a phone, Settings' section picker is one quiet line above the page.
- Search closes with the same X as the bell and help.
- The bell's cheers section is called **Cheers on your items**.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
