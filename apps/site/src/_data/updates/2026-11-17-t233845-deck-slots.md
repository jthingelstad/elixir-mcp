# Decks follow the game slots

Decks show an active Evo first, a Hero or Champion second, and another
special card in the Wild position. The other cards fill the remaining
places. The same order appears in the app, emails and shared pictures.

Card forms and levels stay as recorded. Copy deck keeps its original
order. Other deck formats keep their recorded layout.

MCP 13.12.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
