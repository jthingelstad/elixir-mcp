# Home opens at once

Home's feed used to read seven days of everyone you follow each time you
opened it, which could take ten seconds or more for a busy account. Elixir
now builds your week ahead, keeps it, and rebuilds it every quarter hour
while you use Home, so the feed opens at once.

The newest battles can take up to about twenty minutes to reach the feed. A
highlight, a cheer and your **New** marker still show at once, and following
someone builds your week again on your next visit.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
