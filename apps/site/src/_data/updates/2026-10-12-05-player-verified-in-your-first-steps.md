# Verifying your player is one of your first steps

Account → Overview now lists **Player verified (needed to act in Clan)** after Battles on record, with a link to Verify, so the step Elixir Clan needs before you can act in your game role is no longer something you find later. It is shown for your own players (your primary and alts), not for a friend. MCP 11.11.2; JSON API 3.2.0 unchanged.
