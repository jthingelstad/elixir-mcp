# Where your clan stands, and when Colosseum is

The clan ladder now says where your own clan stands: its rank when it is on the board, or its clan score and how far it is below the last place when it is not. The clan roster shows the clan's score and war trophies. The game clock says whether a week is Colosseum and when this season's Colosseum starts, since seasons run four or five weeks. The in-game events calendar takes a season, and says when a day in your window was read outside it. Contract 7.1.12.
