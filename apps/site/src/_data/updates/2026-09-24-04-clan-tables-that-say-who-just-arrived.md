# Clan tables that say who just arrived

The Elixir Gym's fourth run over the clan tools. Standings and participation now point out members who joined partway through the period, whose counts include battles from before they arrived, and the missed-battles warning covers the period you asked about rather than always the last week. A clan's recent roster events say when older ones were left out, and its daily history defaults to the last 30 days. Contract 7.1.4.
