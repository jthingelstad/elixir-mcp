# The top of Elixir is clear again on an iPhone

If you added Elixir to your iPhone's Home Screen, iOS 27 blurred and dimmed the bar at the top: the Elixir name, help, the bell and your account went soft, and smeared as soon as you scrolled. That is fixed. The page now starts below the phone's clock, where iOS leaves it alone.

On a phone the top bar also stays where it is while you scroll, like the tabs at the foot, so the bell and your account are always one tap away. On a wider screen nothing changes.

You do not need to add Elixir to your Home Screen again. The fix is there the next time it opens.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
