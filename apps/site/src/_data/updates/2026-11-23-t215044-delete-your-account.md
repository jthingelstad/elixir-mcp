# Delete your account yourself

You can delete your Elixir account on your own now, in Settings › Profile. Tap Delete account, type DELETE, and it's gone right away. Your address comes off the mailing list, and the copies of mail we sent you go too.

Battles, profiles and clans stay: they're game data, kept like any player's. [Your account](/docs/your-account#removing-the-account) lists what goes and what stays.
