# A shorter front page

Signed out, the front page is now the way in and little else: the gate,
then three short rows on following your friends, your clan's week and
sharing a battle you are proud of, a line for Drop, and the call to join.

The agent exchange, the typing window with its three questions, is now on
[Connect a client](/docs/connect-a-client), above the steps. The record's
counts are on [Status and data](/data), as they already were.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
