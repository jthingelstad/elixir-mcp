# Find clanmates who play your cards

Your Timeline can now introduce you to a clanmate who plays your cards. Open **Find › Your clan** to see everyone with a recorded match. Elixir can show that you both play the same eight cards, which clanmate uses a familiar card regularly, and how often it appeared in each player’s recorded battles.

Your Timeline can now bring friends’ sessions in the same event together. A player’s Timeline connects recent Mastery and collection milestones to decks they actually played. Clan’s Feed remembers first recorded war weeks, standings reversals, donation totals and shared tournaments.

Open a battle session for close tower finishes and duel comebacks. On a deck post, **I tried this deck** can find a recorded battle of your own and open it for you to write about.

MCP 15.0.1 and JSON API 6.0.0.
