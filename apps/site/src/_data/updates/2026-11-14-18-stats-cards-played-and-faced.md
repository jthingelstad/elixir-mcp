# Stats Cards: what you played, what you faced

Stats' **Cards** tab answers one question now: the cards you played, and
the cards you faced, in one mode this season.

Each card is one short row: its art, its name, how many battles, the share
you won, and your record. Tap a row to open the card's page. Each list
says which mode it read, like "35 Trophy Road battles", so nothing mixes
your games together.

The order choice, the level gap and the list of opponents are gone. They
answered other questions, and the page reads better on a phone without
them.

MCP 13.2.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
