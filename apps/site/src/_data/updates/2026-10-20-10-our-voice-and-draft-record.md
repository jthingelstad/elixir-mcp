# One voice for the clan's drafts, and the draft kept in the log

In Elixir Clan, **How our messages sound** is a new row in
**Manage ▸ Settings**, beside the clan's model. The leader and
co-leaders write, in their own words, how the clan's messages should
sound, in up to 400 characters. The clan's model reads it both when it
drafts a message on an Action (**Draft in our voice**) and when it
edits the clan's Discord activity channel. It sets the tone and the
wording, never the facts: everything a draft may say, and everything
never sent, stays as it was. The activity channel's own voice became
this one; **Social ▸ Discord** now shows it and links to Settings.

An Action's log now keeps what the model wrote on its **Drafted by the
clan's model** entry, exactly as it wrote it. Only the leader and
co-leaders see those words; elders and members who can read the log see
that a draft was made.

MCP 11.13.1 and JSON API 3.2.0, unchanged.
