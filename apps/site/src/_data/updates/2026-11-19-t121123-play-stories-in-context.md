# See what changed in a friend’s deck

When a friend keeps seven cards and swaps the eighth, or keeps all eight and switches one card’s form, their player Timeline can show the two decks in **Compare decks**. Their Mastery milestones now say how often they brought the card to Ranked or Trophy Road.

Open **Your games with…** from a session you played together. You can see when you teamed up and when you faced each other. Shared events on the Timeline link your friends’ posts about games in that run. In Clan’s Feed you can open games from a tournament your clanmates played and see when an old war rival returns for another season.

MCP 15.0.1 and JSON API 6.0.0.
