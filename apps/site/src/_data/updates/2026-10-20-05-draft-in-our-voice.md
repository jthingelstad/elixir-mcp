# Draft in our voice says more of the member, never less

In Elixir Clan, **Draft in our voice** now starts from Elixir's own
words for the Action and goes further: it says at least what the card
would send, then two to four facts that belong to the member, each said
for what it means. A draft that says less of the record than Elixir's
words is marked for you to check.

In clan chat a draft can run to more than one line, each its own send:
up to three for a promotion and two for a welcome, a demotion or a
farewell. The card shows a field and a copy button for each line, and a
welcome records every line you sent. A welcome or a farewell takes a
short note on what to stress beside its tone; the words after a removal
take only a tone.

A demotion's draft thanks the member for what they did before, from
their time in the clan and the awards they hold, and never says or hints
why. A heading that ends its own sentence no longer gets a second colon
in chat.

MCP 11.13.1 and JSON API 3.2.0, unchanged.
