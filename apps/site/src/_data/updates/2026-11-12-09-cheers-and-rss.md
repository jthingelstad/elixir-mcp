# Cheers, and your timeline in a feed reader

Every item on Home about a player or clan you follow can now take a cheer:
👏 **Well played**, 🔥 **On fire**, 👑 **Royal**, 😮 **Wow**, 😂 **Ha** or
💪 **Grind**. **Cheer** under the item opens the six. You give one per item:
another changes yours, and tapping yours again takes it back. An item shows
how many gave each cheer and "You and 2 others", never who. Cheers stay on
Home: no mail, Discord or bell.

Your timeline can also be read in a feed reader app. **Settings ›
Connections & agents › Your RSS link** makes one private link to your own
feed. Anyone holding it reads your timeline, so keep it like a password; it
is shown once, when you make it, and **Reset** or **Turn off** stops it at
once. It carries game names and the game's facts, never your nicknames,
your email or your account's own business.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
