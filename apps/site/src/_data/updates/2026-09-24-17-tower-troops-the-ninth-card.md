# Tower troops, the ninth card

A deck is eight cards and a tower troop, and Elixir now reads the tower troop like a card. The meta card list can show tower troops (how often each is used and how it wins, over the same players and window as the cards), and a card's page answers a tower troop too, including who in your clan holds it and at what level. Also today: clan standings no longer count boat defenses the member did not play, and a member who comes back after time away counts their time in the clan from their return. Contract 7.2.0.
