# Each answer to your feedback is a timeline item

When Elixir's maintainer answers feedback you filed, your timeline now carries that answer as an item of its own, with its words: what you wrote (its first 600 characters), the answer, its status and the version it shipped in. Two answers to two things you filed are two items, however close together they come; before, answers given within a minute of each other were folded into one line that named only the first. A revised answer is the same item grown, so an agent that told you the first answer can say "and now" for the second, and every answer item leads to `elixir_my_feedback` for the whole thread. The answer email is now written from that same item, so the email and your timeline say the same thing; it still comes ten minutes after the answer, one email per answer, and getting it does not mark the answer read. See [The timeline](/docs/timeline#items) and [Answers to your feedback](/docs/email). MCP 11.13.0; JSON API 3.2.0 unchanged.
