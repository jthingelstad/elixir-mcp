# A friend's war week, and their promotions

Follow a friend and you now see their own clan news, even if you don't follow their clan.

- **Their war week**: when a Clan Wars week closes, "Pip played all 16 war decks in Season 131, week 2, for 3,200 points." It's the whole week, never a single day. A week with no deck played says nothing, and several friends' weeks fold into one row.
- **Their promotions**: "Pip was promoted from Member to Elder in Ember Hall." Follow the clan too and you see it once. A step down stays on the clan's timeline.

MCP 13.10.0. JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
