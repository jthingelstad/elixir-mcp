# Turning an email off lands on a real page

The turn-off link at the foot of every email now opens an Elixir page, with the same bar, type and footer as the rest of Elixir. Pressing **Turn it off** used to answer with a bare "ok". Now it says what was turned off, that your other emails are unchanged, and where to turn it back on, with a way on to Home, your email settings or the email docs. An old or broken link says so on the same page and changes nothing.

MCP 11.18.4 and JSON API 3.4.0 unchanged.
