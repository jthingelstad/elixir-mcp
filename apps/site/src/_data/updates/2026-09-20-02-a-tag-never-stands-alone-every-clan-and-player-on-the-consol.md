# A tag never stands alone: every clan and player on the console is named

A pass over the console for places that showed a clan tag or a player tag with no name beside it. Agents: the clan an agent acts for read as its tag on the agent's page, in the agents table and on Connections, and the create-agent form's clan picker promised a name it never had; the name now travels with the tag everywhere a principal's clans are listed. Explore: a search result's clan, a battle record's own player, a war week's clan, and the titles of a player's battles, decks and war-weeks lists all read as tags; the envelopes behind them now carry the last-observed name (battles_query, battles_decks, war_history, players_search; contract 6.3.0, additive) and the pages show it. Smaller: the Explore 'try' chip for your own player reads your name (or nickname) instead of your tag, an account event about a player or clan is named from the record, and the admin usage table names each account's primary player. Same rule on Elixir Clan: who decided a card, who set a hold, who granted an award and who saved a policy version were stamped as tags alone and now carry the name (rows written before today are named from what the ledger already knows), the public How-Elder-works page names the clan, and feedback carries the clan's name beside its tag.
