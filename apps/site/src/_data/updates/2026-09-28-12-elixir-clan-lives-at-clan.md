# Elixir Clan lives at /clan

Elixir Clan, where a clan's leaders run their own policy against the record, has moved from its own address to elixir.poapkings.com/clan, beside the site and the console. A clan's page is /clan/ followed by its tag, and the Clan button in the bar at the top goes there. The old clan.poapkings.com address is being retired, so update any bookmark. Nothing about a clan's policy, standing or actions has changed, and no change to the tools.
