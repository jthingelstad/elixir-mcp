# How a friend finished the Ranked season

Your Timeline now says where a friend ended the Ranked season: "Pip finished the Ranked season in Master III."

- **At the season's end**: the moment sits at the hour the season closed, even though Clash Royale shows the result a little later.
- **Rating only at the top**: Ultimate Champion shows the rating. No global rank, ever.
- **Only if they played**: Master I is the league everyone starts in, so it shows only when they played Ranked that season.
- **Only for friends you already followed**: someone you just followed doesn't bring an old season with them.

It's your friend's own moment, so it's on their page and in your feed, not on the clan's timeline.

We also fixed a slip: a season's Ranked result read in the hours after the season ended could be filed under the wrong season. Elixir now waits until the profile has moved on, and fixes older ones on the next read.
