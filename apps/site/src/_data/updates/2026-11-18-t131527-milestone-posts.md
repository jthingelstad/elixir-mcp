# Write about your milestone

Tap **Post** on an achievement recorded for a player you have verified, such as
a Mastery level, promotion or first win with a new Evo. Add your words and the
post keeps the recorded milestone and its original time. Your usual followers
and clanmates can read and reply, with the same post settings and limits.
