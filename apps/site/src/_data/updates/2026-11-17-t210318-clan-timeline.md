# Your clan's Timeline

Clan opens on its **Timeline**, with the newest activity first. **All activity** keeps your clanmates' moments and posts together with clan news. Choose **Clan news** for joins, departures, role changes, races and the clan's own notices.

There is more room for activity on a phone. **About this clan** opens recruitment details and policy chips, and the race sits below the Timeline. Notices that need your attention stay above it.

MCP 13.11.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
