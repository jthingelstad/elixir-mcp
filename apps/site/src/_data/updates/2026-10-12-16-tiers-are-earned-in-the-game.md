# Tiers are earned in the game

Leader and Family are no longer asked for or granted: Elixir reads them every time you use it. You are a Leader while one of your verified players leads or co-leads a clan you track, and Family while a collector you run is active. The higher one wins, and a tier goes when its ground goes; nothing you already track is removed, only the next add waits for room. Partner is still granted by hand. Running a collector earns the Family tier and nothing else: collector points, call credits and bonus slots are retired, so the Profile tier panel says what earns the next tier instead of offering a request button, and the Sunday collector mail counts the fetches that added new facts instead of points. [Roles and tiers](/docs/roles#upgrades) has the details. MCP 11.11.3 (`elixir_collectors` keeps `points` and `quota_credits` at 0); JSON API 3.2.0 unchanged.
