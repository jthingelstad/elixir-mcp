# A departure says the role they held then

A leader's word on a departure now names the role the member held when they departed, even if they have since come back with another. Where the record holds no such stay, the line names no role. In Clan, a member's recorded activity now runs its weeks, donations included, from Monday's reset to the next, so every battle it lists falls in a week it totals.

MCP 11.16.1 and JSON API 3.3.0, unchanged.
