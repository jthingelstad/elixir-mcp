# A friend's first win with a new card

Your Timeline now cheers the first win with something new: "Pip won with Evo Musketeer for the first time.", with the card's art.

- **New cards, Evos, Heroes and tower troops**: anything they didn't have at Elixir's last look at their profile.
- **Their own deck**: a draft or a ready-made deck doesn't count, and neither do boat battles.
- **Once**: the first win with it, and that's it.

It starts with cards they get from now on, so nothing old shows up.

Also: a friend who finishes the Ranked season in Master I now gets their moment even when their Ranked battle reaches Elixir after their profile does.

MCP 13.9.0. JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
