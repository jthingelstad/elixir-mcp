# You follow players and clans now, everywhere

Elixir's word for adding a player or a clan is **follow**. Settings ›
**Players & following** (it was Players & tracking) is where you follow a
player or a clan and stop following one, and every page, button and help
page says follow, followed and following where it said track.

Nothing about what a follow does changed: following a player or a clan is
still what records it, a follow still costs a slot only when Elixir records
the player because of you, and **Watching** stays its own kind of follow (in
your digest when they played, with no Stats), beside a friend you follow on
purpose, your alts and your own player.

The names an agent or an app calls (the MCP tools and the OAuth scope) are
renamed in their own release.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
