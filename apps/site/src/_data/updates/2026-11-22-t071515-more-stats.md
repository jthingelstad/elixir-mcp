# More numbers in your Stats comparisons

"This week vs your last 4", "This season vs last" and the before and since your main deck tables now show three more numbers beside the rest:

- **Close games (one crown)**: the battles you won, and lost, by just one crown.
- **Three-crowned against**: how often your opponent took all three towers.
- **Longest win streak**: your longest run of wins in a row.

Each one sits beside the battles it counts, like the others.

MCP 15.2.0 and JSON API 6.0.0.
