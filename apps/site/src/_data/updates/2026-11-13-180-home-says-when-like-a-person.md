# Home says when, the way you would

Home's feed now says "Tonight, 7:40 to 8:32 pm" or "Yesterday, 4:10 pm"
instead of a bare clock time, in your own zone; point at a time for the
exact one. A session shows when it started and ended, its line names its
widest win ("best win 3-0"), and beside its figures it notes a run of wins
or the wins that took all three crowns. The Everyone, Friends, Clan and You
chips sit beside Home's title, with New as one more toggle in the same row.
Each item opens with a button ("Open session", "Open battle"), and a race
week shows its place among the five clans as a bar.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
