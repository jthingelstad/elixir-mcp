# Console follows your recorded player

Console now points to your next step in Tracking or Ladder instead of scoring optional setup as unfinished work. Clan war history and AI clients are clearly optional. The record distinguishes pending capture, a profile, recent or older battles, and recording that has stopped; active client connections remain separate from observed data reads. Quickstart starts with using Elixir in your browser. Contracts remain MCP 11.2.3 and JSON API 3.0.0.
