# Timeline items say where they lead

The timeline says that something happened. Each item on a full read now also says where to read more: the reads that tell the rest of that story, each a tool and the arguments to call it with. A sitting leads to its own games and to the performance over its span. A promotion or a new best leads to the battle that proved it. A finished war week leads to that week's history, and a bracket to the rivals in it. An agent writing about a moment calls these as given, and no longer has to work out which tool holds the story.
