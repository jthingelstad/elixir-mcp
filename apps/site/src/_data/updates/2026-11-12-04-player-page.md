# Every player's name opens their page

Tap any player's name, on Home, Friends, search, a battle, Ladder or in an
email, and it opens one page for that player: their timeline for the last 30
days, their highlights and moments, and, when the player's account has
verified them, who it follows, who follows it and its badges. Followers are
named by game name and tag; nicknames, Discord usernames and email addresses
never show. Ladder and Clan are a link away from it.

**Follow** on the page asks first and says whether it is free or uses one of
your player slots. **Follow everyone** in a player's clan is one question,
priced the same way, and is refused whole when you lack the slots.

Your own page has **Copy my Follow me link**. Whoever opens it is asked to
join or sign in, then offered your player to follow. The link names players
and nothing else.

Search now shows each player's tag, clan and trophies.
