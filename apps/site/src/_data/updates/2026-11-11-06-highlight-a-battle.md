# Highlight a battle, then share it

A battle you played can now go on your timeline with your own words. On the
battle's page, a player you have verified who played it gets **Highlight
this battle**: write up to 500 characters, or nothing, and choose **Post to
your timeline**. Everyone who follows that player sees it in their timeline.
**Edit words** and **Delete** stay on the battle's page for you.

**Share** gives the highlight its own link that anyone can open without an
account: the player's game name, your words and the battle, both decks and
how it ended. It unfurls in a chat with your words and the battle's picture.
Deleting the highlight stops the link opening.

MCP 11.20.0 (`elixir_timeline` has a new kind, `battle_highlight`, with the
words); JSON API unchanged.
