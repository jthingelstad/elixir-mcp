# Introduce a clanmate and celebrate a new Elder

Open **Clan news** to meet a new member. Elixir picks a detail from their recorded play. When someone becomes Elder, the clan can see what they contributed. Friends following the player see the move or promotion once the roster records it.

Leaders can share the exact Timeline paragraphs in clan chat. Copy each paragraph, send it in the game, then mark it sent. Another leader can pick up where you left off. Role changes and departures keep their own decisions and sharing history.

MCP 15.1.0 and JSON API 6.0.0.
