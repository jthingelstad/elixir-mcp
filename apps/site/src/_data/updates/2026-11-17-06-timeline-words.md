# Timeline, Profile and Notifications

The tabs have new names. What is in them is the same.

- **Home is now Timeline**, at `/timeline`: everyone you follow, your clan and you, newest first.
- **You is now Profile**: your own account page, with Your stats.
- The bell's list is called **Notifications**: what happened to you. Settings › Notifications is how you are told, on your phone or computer.
- Every old `/home` link, in an email, a bookmark or the installed app, still works and opens the same page.
- A run of battles is a **session** everywhere, including the timeline your connected agent reads with `elixir_timeline`.

MCP 13.4.1 (wording only) and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
