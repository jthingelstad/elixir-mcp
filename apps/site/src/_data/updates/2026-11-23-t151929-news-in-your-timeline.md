# News from Elixir now shows up in your Timeline

When Elixir gets something new you'd want to know about, you'll see it right in your Timeline, from Elixir, with a link to read the rest. This is the first one.

It only comes now and then, so your friends and your clan stay up front. It sits in its place by time like everything else and stays for a week. There's no bell, push or email for it.

Every change, big and small, is still listed in What's new.
