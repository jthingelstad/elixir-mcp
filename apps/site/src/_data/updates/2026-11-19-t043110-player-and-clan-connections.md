# Your posts and your clan’s stories

Your Elixir connection can tell you when a friend writes or edits a post.
Ask which of your posts received cheers to see the counts, including posts
with a battle attached.

A clan agent can read just its home clan’s stories and check the public war
policy for that clan. It can still scout the other clans it follows separately.

MCP 15.0.0 · JSON API 6.0.0.
