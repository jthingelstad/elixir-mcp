# Elixir Clan suggests removals again, from the battle log

Inactivity is the days since a member's last recorded battle, and a member past the policy's removal day becomes a **Remove from the clan** Action for the leader and co-leaders again. **Removal action at** is now the day itself: with at risk at 7 and the removal action at 10, the Action comes on day 10, not day 17. A member who reaches at risk is asked whether they are away, when the policy asks quiet members. The **Not yet enforceable** label from this morning is gone. MCP and JSON API unchanged.
