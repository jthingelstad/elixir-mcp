# Your timeline opens faster

Your timeline in Settings, and your agents' timelines, now ask much
less of Elixir's record to open, so they show up sooner. Your RSS feed
does too.

- Late battles are counted once for the whole week, not once for every
  player you follow.
- Every page's first read, and Settings' Players list, find your
  players straight from their tags.
- Clan's Map reads everyone's place at once.

What each page shows, and who can see it, is the same.
