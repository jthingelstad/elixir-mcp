# A leaders' pick can be granted during the season

Leaders no longer have to wait for a season to close to grant a leaders' pick. On **Awards**, the season being fought now has a **Grant** button for each leaders' pick the leaders (or elders, where the award allows) may give, and the page says the season is in progress. The pick is granted at once and counts as that season's; the races Clan decides from the record are still settled when the season closes. When the clan announces its awards, the pick gets its announcement Action right away, and the season's announcement at close leaves out any pick already sent. An older season that is still open, or one the record has not seen, takes no pick.

MCP 11.18.6 and JSON API 3.4.0 unchanged.
