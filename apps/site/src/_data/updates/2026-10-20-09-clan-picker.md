# In Elixir Clan, the clan you are in heads the rail

The top of Elixir Clan's rail is now the clan you are in: its name, its
tag, your role there and, while your player there is not verified,
"unverified". If your players are in more than one clan, it opens to
each of them, with the player you are there and your role, and to All
your clans…; picking one opens it the way coming to a clan does, on
Actions for a leader with Actions waiting and on the roster otherwise.
On a phone it sits beside the folded rail, above the page.

The rail's Clans item, the switcher beside the roster's name and your
name at the foot of the rail are gone. Your players are where they were,
under You ▸ Players.

MCP 11.13.1 and JSON API 3.2.0, unchanged.
