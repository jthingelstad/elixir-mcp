# A big week shows on the Timeline

When a game week ends, a friend's big week can show up on your Timeline: "Pip won 62% of 26 Trophy Road battles in the week to Nov 23, up from 48% over the 4 weeks before."

- It shows when one mode's win rate is at least 10 points above the 4 weeks before, with at least 10 battles won or lost on each side.
- Only a rise shows. Draws and boat battles don't count.
- It shows up a few hours after the Monday reset. Battles recorded after that don't change it.
- Tap **See stats** to open their Stats in that mode.

Your own weeks show up on your Timeline too, and your agent can read them with the rest of the Timeline.

MCP 15.3.0 and JSON API 6.0.0.
