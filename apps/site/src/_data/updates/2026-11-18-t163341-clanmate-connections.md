# Your clanmates are already connected

Open **Find › Your clan** to see who shares a clan with your verified players.
Their posts and questions appear on your Timeline. Tap **Friend** on a player
you want to follow more closely. If you unfriend them, they’re still your
clanmate. When either of you leaves the clan, any friendship you chose stays.
