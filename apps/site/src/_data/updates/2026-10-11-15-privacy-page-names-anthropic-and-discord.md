# The privacy page names a clan's AI key and its Discord posts

[Privacy](/docs/privacy) now says what happens when a clan's leaders add their own Anthropic key: Elixir Clan sends Anthropic what it needs to draft the clan's messages, member names included, on the clan's key. It also says that a clan's connected Discord gets the clan's activity and Actions. Feedback is answered by Elixir's maintainer, sometimes with help from AI agents, and the account and agent pages now say so. Copying or sharing the Bring your clanmates invite is counted in aggregate, by channel; the link stays the same for everyone and nothing records who copied it. MCP and JSON API unchanged.
