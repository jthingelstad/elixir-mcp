# Stats opens for the players you follow

A player you follow now has a **Their stats** link on their player page.
It opens Stats on their season: the same Season, Battles, Decks and Cards
pages you have for your own player, read from the same record, with a
link back to their page. Stats opens only for your own players and the
players you follow; anyone else is refused.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
