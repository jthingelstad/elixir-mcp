# Elixir keeps a year

Elixir now keeps what it records for one year, then deletes it. Battles,
daily readings, clan membership, war weeks and timeline moments older
than a year go, along with your account's own history of the same age:
account events, the list of mail sent to you, answered feedback, cheers
and finished claim checks. The call log your agents' calls make is kept
28 days. [Limits](/docs/limits#retention-windows) lists every window.

MCP 13.0.1 and JSON API 5.0.0, unchanged.
