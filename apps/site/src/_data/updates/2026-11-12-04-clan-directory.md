# Find a clan in the clan directory

The new [clan directory](/clan/directory) lists every clan whose leaders
saved its rules in Clan, as a grid of tiles. Each tile shows the clan's
name, tag, size, how to join and its in-game description, with its rules:
whether it plays Clan Wars, how strict it is, what counts, and when a
removal comes.

Filter by those rules, by room, or by your trophies. Clans are listed by
name, never ranked. Open it from search (**Clan directory** under Look up),
from your clans, or from Clan when your player has no clan. You need to be
signed in.

Leaders: saving a policy is what lists your clan.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
