# One email: your digest

Elixir now sends one scheduled email, your digest. It tells the people you follow and your clan's week as one story, and each player appears once. A clanmate you follow is told under their own name, marked with your clan, and your clan's part keeps to the clan's own news: a river race that finished, who joined and departed, and role changes. Your own week and your alts' firsts come with it.

The Monday clan report, Tuesday's Your week in the Arena and the milestone note are no longer sent. What they told that matters week to week is in the digest; a clan's whole week stays on Clan and your season on Stats. Your firsts still reach your timeline as soon as the record learns them.

Nobody gets more mail than before. If you had turned the clan report off, a clan's news alone never sends you a digest; if you had turned Arena week off, your alts stay a name and a count. A digest you turned off stays off, and weekly, daily or off is still your choice in Settings › Emails.

Leaders' "Actions waiting for you" mail is unchanged: it comes only when an action in your clan becomes yours to do, never on a fixed day. Sign-in codes, answers to your feedback and collector mail are unchanged too.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
