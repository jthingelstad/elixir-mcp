# Home is one feed

Home is now one feed of the last seven days, newest first: the players you
follow, your clan and you, together. Chips choose whose items it shows
(**Everyone**, **Friends**, **Clan** or **You**), and **New** keeps what came
since your last visit. Your week and your clan sit beside it.

A sitting of battles carries a small bar chart, one bar per battle: up for a
win and down for a loss, as tall as the crown margin. The same facts are
written out for a screen reader, and **View games** opens the battles in
place. A moment links its battle, and every player's name opens their page.

The top bar is **Home**, **Find** and **Clan**, with search, Help and a bell.
On a phone the tabs are **Home**, **Find**, **Clan**, **You** and the bell.
**Find** holds search, **Follow a friend**, the clan directory and your own
follow link. **You** holds your players' pages, their stats in Ladder,
Settings and signing out. The bell is what needs you, as Home's Needs you
was, with a dot while anything does; a dot on Home marks news since you last
opened it.

Home's Timeline and Friends tabs are gone, and their old addresses open Home
and Find. Wednesday's digest still tells what the people you follow did.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
