# Clan is called Clan

When leaders connect a clan's activity channel, the hello line Elixir
posts in Discord now links "Clan in Elixir" instead of "Elixir Clan".
Clan is part of Elixir, not an app of its own, and the words say so.

[Bring your clan](/docs/bring-your-clan) no longer asks you to sign in
to Clan again after you verify your player: Clan uses your Elixir
sign-in, so you just come back to it.

Nobody sees or can do anything new. Who gets mail, and when, is
unchanged.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
