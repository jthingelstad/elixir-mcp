# Ladder names one next step while your record is new

For your first 28 days of recording, Ladder's season home says how old your record is and offers one next step: **Verify your player** if it is not verified, **Open Clan** if it is and its clan is recorded, otherwise **Follow a friend**. It is a link, not advice about play, and it goes away on day 28. MCP 11.11.2; JSON API 3.2.0 unchanged.
