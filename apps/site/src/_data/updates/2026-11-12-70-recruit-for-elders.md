# Recruit is for elders, co-leaders and the leader

Recruit, the clan's pitch as a personal note or a recruiting post, is now
a tool for elders, co-leaders and the leader, like Scout. You find both
at the foot of Clan › Members. Members keep Standing and the line to
bring clanmates in; a member who opens Recruit's address sees the role it
needs.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
