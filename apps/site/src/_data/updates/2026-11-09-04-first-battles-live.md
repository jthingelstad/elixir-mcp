# Your battles arrive with your profile

When you add a player, Elixir now reads their battle log straight away, right after their profile, instead of waiting for its next scheduled round. Your profile and roughly your last 30 battles usually arrive within a minute; at busy times it can take a few. Home's first steps, Ladder, Settings, the welcome email and the docs now say so.

It is the same for a player you follow, a player you add through an agent, and the tag you gave at signup. Each player slot gets one of these battle log reads a day, beside the profile read it already had; past that the scheduler reads it, as before.

MCP 11.18.6; JSON API 3.4.0 unchanged.
