# Cheers on your play, in your inbox

When people cheer your play or follow you, you hear about it even when
you're not in Elixir.

- **Cheers on your play** is a new email: how many cheers each of your battles and posts got, and how many new followers you have. Never who.
- It comes at most once a day, around 6 pm in your time zone, and only when there's something you haven't seen in the bell yet.
- Tap a line to open the battle or the player.
- It's on for your players you verified. Turn it off any time in Settings › Emails.

MCP 13.3.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
