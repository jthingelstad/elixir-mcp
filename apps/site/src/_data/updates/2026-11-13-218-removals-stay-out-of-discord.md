# Removals, promotions and demotions stay out of your clan's Discord

A clan's Discord channel for Actions no longer gets removals, promotions
or demotions. Only the Leader and Co-Leaders see one of those while it's
open, and that channel also carries welcomes, which are Elders' to take.
Departures, welcomes, and award and policy announcements post just as
before.

One posted earlier stays as it was: Elixir doesn't edit it when it
closes. Once a member is removed, promoted or demoted, the clan hears of
it as before.

On a clan's **Policy** tab, **Becoming an Elder** now says that only the
Leader and Co-Leaders see a promotion or demotion before it's done, and
**When you go quiet** no longer says a removal may go to Discord.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
