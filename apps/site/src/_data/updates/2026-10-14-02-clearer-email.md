# Clearer emails, from sign-in to the weekly reports

Elixir's emails use a quieter frame, readable headings and fewer boxes.
Reports keep their figures and coverage notes, and narrow screens have more
room for names, records and card art. Actions waiting are rows with new ones
marked. Feedback emails lead with the answer or question and link back to
the conversation.

Sign-in mail names the app you are signing in to and mentions a link only
when it includes one. Welcome mail opens Tracking so you can start your
record. Collector notices and operator mail share the same design.

Clan reports leave battle activity unknown when only roster and war activity
is recorded, and describe roster departures without assuming how someone
left. Your delivery choices and schedule are the same; sent records remain
as they were sent.

MCP 11.13.0; JSON API 3.2.0.
