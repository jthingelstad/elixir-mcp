# A new Home to try

There is a new Home, not yet where you land: a "Try the new Home" link on Overview opens it. Today says what needs you, your week by mode, your clan and the latest; Timeline runs by day; Friends tells the week of Wednesday's mail, and following someone there makes them a friend. Your Overview and everything else stay where they are for now.

MCP 11.16.1 and JSON API 3.3.0, unchanged.
