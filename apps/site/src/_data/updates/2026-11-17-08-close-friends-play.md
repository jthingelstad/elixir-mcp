# Close friends' play

Star a close friend, and Elixir can tell your phone when they've played.

- **One notification when they finish a session** of 3 battles or more, or a shorter one with a climb in Ranked or a 5-win challenge run. Their name and the result, like "Won 3 of 4 on Trophy Road". Tap it to open the session.
- **A new best, a new league in Ranked or a new Arena** is told in the Timeline's own words.
- **Never mid-session**, and once per session. Friends who finish together come as one, like "Pip Ember and 2 other close friends played", which opens your Timeline on Close friends.
- **At most three a day**, inside the usual one every ten minutes and eight a day.
- **Its own switch** in Settings › Notifications, on until you turn it off.
- **Only you see who you starred.** Your friend is never told, and it never comes if either of you blocked the other. It's never in an email or the digest.

MCP 13.4.1 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
