# Elixir on your phone's home screen

Elixir now installs on a phone and opens like an app: full screen, under its own Elixir icon, with Home, Ladder, Clan and Search as tabs at the foot. On an iPhone, open it in Safari and choose Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android, Chrome offers Install app. The installed app keeps its own sign-in, so sign in once inside it with the emailed code or link.

The tabs at the foot of a phone are easier to spot: they sit on a raised bar with a bright edge, and the place you are in is marked with a filled pill behind its icon. Text fields no longer make an iPhone zoom in when you tap them.

MCP 11.18.6 and JSON API 3.4.0 unchanged.
