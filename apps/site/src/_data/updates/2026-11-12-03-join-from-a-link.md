# Join from a shared battle, then follow from there

Opened a battle or a highlight someone sent you? Signed out, the page now
offers **Join Elixir** and **Sign in**, and either brings you back to the
same page. The link names only the battle or the post, never who sent it.

Back on the page, signed in, **Follow from here** lists its players: a
highlight's player first, then the rest of the battle. Each says before you
tap whether following is free or uses one of your player slots.

Signing up now asks whether you are 13 or older; Elixir accounts are for
people who are. Answer no and nothing is sent or saved. Plain sign-in no
longer opens a new account: it brings you back to the signup form to answer
first.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
