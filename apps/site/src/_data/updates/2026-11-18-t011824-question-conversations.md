# Keep a question's conversation together

Choose Questions on your Timeline or your clan's Timeline to read just
questions. Your clan also has Unanswered for questions the author has not
marked Answered. Each shows how many answers it has, including zero. The author
can mark it Answered, or mark it unanswered again, while replies stay open.

Replies to a post now share one notification, with the latest reply and
new reply count. Open it to go straight to that post. Reply words stay in
Elixir.

MCP 13.13.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
