# Your RSS feed answers faster

Your feed reader checks your RSS link all day, even when nobody has played. Each check used to put your whole week together again. Now it reads the week Elixir already keeps for your Timeline, and when nothing has changed your reader is told so and downloads nothing.

One thing to know: if you only read the feed, a battle can show up one check later than it used to. Posts are always current.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
