# Your clan on Home opens faster

Home no longer rebuilds your clan's card each time it opens. It keeps your
standing, your week and the war line and refreshes them in the background,
so they can trail by up to about twenty minutes. Who may see the card, your
role and the Actions waiting for you are read fresh every time; a role
change, a policy change or leaving the clan builds the card again at once.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
