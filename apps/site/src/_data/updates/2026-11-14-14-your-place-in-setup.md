# Set your place right from Home

The last step of **Your profile** now opens the place picker right on
Home. Tap **Set a place**, pick your country, region or city, and you're
done. You don't need a clan, and it works even when your clan's map is
off. **Not now** still counts.

Your place also has a home of its own: **Your place** in Settings ›
Profile, where you can set it, change it or remove it any time. Your
passport's **On the Map** Stamp opens it too.

Who sees it: your clan's verified members, on the clan map, when its
leaders have the map on.

MCP 13.1.1 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
