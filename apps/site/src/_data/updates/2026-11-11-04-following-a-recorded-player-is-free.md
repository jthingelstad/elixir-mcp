# Following a player Elixir already records is free

A player slot now pays only for what Elixir records because of you. Following someone who is in a clan Elixir records in full, or whom another account already follows, takes none of your 50 slots. A clan of yours that Elixir follows only for its roster and war does not record its members, so following one of them still takes a slot.

Home's Follow, the search sheet and Settings › Players & tracking say what a Follow costs before you tap it: **Free · already recorded**, or **Uses 1 of your 50 player slots** with how many are in use. Each player on Players & tracking says whether it uses a slot. At the limit, a follow that would take a slot is refused with the count and the way out; a free one still goes in.

If a free player's reason goes away, they keep recording and start using a slot. Nobody is removed, and nothing you track today starts using more slots than before.

MCP 11.19.0 (`elixir_track_player` returns a `slot` block); JSON API 3.5.0 (`POST /me/players` carries the same block).
