# How hard your war rivals really play

The Elixir Gym's third runs over the war and player tools. Rivals now show their points, not just their fame: fame pays for where a clan placed each day, so a rival could look strong on fame while playing a fraction of what your clan did. Player profiles show the King Tower level and lifetime donations. The seasonal Trophy Road no longer shows players who never climbed it as standing at 14,000. Asking a player or clan timeline about a season works everywhere, including a season that has not started yet. Contract 7.1.3.
