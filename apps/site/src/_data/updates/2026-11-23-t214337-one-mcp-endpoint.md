# One MCP address, and it is yours

For people who connect an AI assistant to Elixir. `/mcp` is now the one MCP address, and every connection to it is a person's own: it answers as you, about your players and the people you follow.

Clan agents are gone, and with them their addresses at `/a/<id>/mcp`, the `on_behalf_of` argument (with `display_name` beside it) and the `elixir_my_identities` tool. A client that still sends `on_behalf_of` is told the tool does not take it, and to read the tool list again. Leave out `player_tag` to mean your primary player, as before.

The tool list is the same for every connection, so its fingerprint has moved: have your assistant read the tool list again.
