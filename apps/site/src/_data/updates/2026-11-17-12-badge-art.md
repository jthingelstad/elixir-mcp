# Badges show their art

A badge moment in your Timeline now shows the badge's own picture from the game, at the level it tells: a new badge, a badge's new level, or 12 wins in a challenge that raised the badge. The art is Supercell's, shown as it is.

Elixir picks up each badge's art as it reads profiles, so art fills in over the next day or so. Until a badge's art has arrived, the moment shows its usual mark. A day of Card Mastery levels still shows the cards.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
