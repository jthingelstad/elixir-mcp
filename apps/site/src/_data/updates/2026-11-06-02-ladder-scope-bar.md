# Ladder fits on a phone

Ladder no longer has a menu down the side. A bar at the top says whose season, which season and which mode you are reading, and that stays put as you move between four tabs: Season, Battles, Decks and Cards. Casual has its own chip now, and Other appears when your season holds a Challenge or a Tournament, the two kept apart.

The calendar moved onto Season, where it counts one mode's battles a day and tells a day with no battles from one nobody could read. Days played became Battles, the season day by day and sitting by sitting, and the old address still arrives with the same player and mode. Decks shows one mode at a time, with real cards for every deck, War's included. On War, every tab says this week's decks and points and links to the race in Clan.

MCP 11.17.0, JSON API 3.3.0 unchanged.
