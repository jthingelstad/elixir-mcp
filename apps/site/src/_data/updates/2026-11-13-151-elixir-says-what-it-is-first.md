# Elixir says what it is first

The front page, the docs home and the share card now open the same way:
Elixir is the missing social layer of Clash Royale: follow your friends and
your clan, see how they play, and cheer them on.

The Quickstart follows Home's welcome steps: sign in, add your player,
follow a few people, and choose your digest. The About page leads with
following people and your clan, and describes the record, the collectors
and your own agent as what feeds it. An AI client now names the server
plainly: Elixir.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
