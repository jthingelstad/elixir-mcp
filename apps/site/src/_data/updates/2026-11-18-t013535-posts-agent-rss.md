# Posts in your RSS feed and own agent

Your private RSS feed includes posts and questions from the same people
you follow on your Timeline, with their deck or card names. Open a post
in Elixir to read and reply after sign-in.

Your own connected agent can read those posts too. Blocks and reports
still apply. Comments stay in Elixir, and no public share link is made.

MCP 13.13.0; JSON API 5.1.0.
