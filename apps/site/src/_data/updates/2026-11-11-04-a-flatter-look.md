# A flatter look

Elixir has dropped most of its boxes and borders. Panels now sit on the
page by shade rather than inside an outline, and buttons come in three
kinds you can tell apart: gold for the one thing to do on a screen, a
solid button for anything else, and a link for the way out. Borders are
left only where they mean something, such as a text field, a chosen
filter or a leader's decision.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
