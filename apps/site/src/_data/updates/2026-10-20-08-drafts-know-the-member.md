# Draft in our voice knows more about the member

When a leader drafts a promotion, demotion, welcome, removal or farewell
line for one member, the clan's model is now told much more of that
member's record: every war week they played in the clan over the last
year, their donations here week by week and in their career, highlights
from their game profile (years playing, favourite card, masteries,
legendary badges, best Path of Legends league, Clan War Wins badge),
their decks over the last 28 days with wins and losses, each mode on its
own, their milestones and their role changes here. Elixir also picks up
to four story angles from that record, strongest first.

A demotion is told only what the member gave before the policy's recent
weeks, so it can thank them; never those weeks' numbers. A removal or
farewell is told only the war weeks they played.

MCP 11.13.1 and JSON API 3.2.0, unchanged.
