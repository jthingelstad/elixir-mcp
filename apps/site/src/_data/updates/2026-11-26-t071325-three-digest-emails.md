# Pick your emails: daily, weekly and season

Your friends by email now come three ways: a Daily brief on yesterday, a Weekly recap after the Monday reset, and a Season recap when a new season starts. Turn on any of them, all of them or none in Settings › Emails.

If you had the weekly email, you still do, and the Season recap is on too. If you had it daily, the Daily brief and the Season recap are on. If you had it off, all three stay off. Each email's turn-off link turns off just that one.
