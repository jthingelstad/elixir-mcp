# Follow your clan, see your slots, set your place

**Clan › Members** has a head over the roster: how many members there
are, how many of them you follow, **Follow everyone** and **Clan map**.
Every member sees it, not only leaders. **Follow everyone** asks first and
says what it costs: how many are free and how many take one of your player
slots. Your own players are left out. Past your limit it stays shut, and
the reason is beside it; nobody is followed in part.

**Settings › What members see** now holds two controls. **Location** is
your place on the clan map, the same one the map sets. It says who sees
it: only the verified members of each clan your verified player is in, on
that clan's map. **Your follow slots** is a meter of the slots in use out
of your limit, the same number every Follow says. Under it are the
players using a slot because of you, each with **Stop following**, and
apart from them the follows that are free because Elixir records the
player anyway.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
