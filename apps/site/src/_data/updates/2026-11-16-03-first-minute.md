# A friendlier first minute

New to Elixir? The first minute reads more simply now.

- Your welcome says what happens next: add your tag, and your battles show up within about a minute. Connecting an AI is still in Settings, not on your first page.
- Verifying is three numbered steps: build the deck, play one battle, come back. While you wait, the page says it is waiting for your battle and keeps checking for you.
- A battle someone sends you has a **Join free** button up top if you're not signed in yet.
- Stats panels say one line each, with the rest under **More about these numbers**. On a phone, Days played shows your last week first, and **Show all days** opens the rest.
- Your **Follow me link** sits on one line, with a Copy button.
- Clan says **47 of 50 members** instead of "47 members, 3 open".

MCP 13.3.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
