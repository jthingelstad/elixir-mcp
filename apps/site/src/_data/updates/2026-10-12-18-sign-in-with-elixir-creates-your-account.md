# Sign in with Elixir creates your account, and Drop asks no consent

The page an app sends you to when it signs you in with Elixir, as Elixir Drop does, now lets someone new create an account there: enter your email and the code, and your free Elixir account opens once the code is proved, exactly as signing up on the website does, with the same product-news box and welcome email. Signing in with an existing account works as before, and a denied or disabled account still cannot sign in. The page no longer talks about approved accounts or requesting access.

Elixir's own apps no longer show a consent page. Signed in to Elixir already, Drop sends you straight back, signed in; otherwise you enter your email and code and the page lists what Drop gets, with nothing to tick. Any other app connecting to your account still shows its consent page and its boxes. The sign-in pages share the website's limits on sign-in email. MCP 11.11.3 and JSON API 3.2.0 unchanged.
