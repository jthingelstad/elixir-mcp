# Notifications drops down from the top

The bell moved off the bottom of your phone. It now sits at the top right, next to your profile picture, on every screen.

- Tap the bell and your notifications drop down right under it. You stay on the page you were on.
- On a phone the list is nearly the screen's width. A long one scrolls inside, and the page behind stays put.
- Tap a line to open it, or tap outside, **Close** or Escape to put the list away.
- The tabs at the foot of your phone are now **Timeline**, **Find**, **Clan** and **Profile**.

Everything in the list is the same as before, and the number on the bell counts the same things.
