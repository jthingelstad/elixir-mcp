# Near you and the player map

Find has a new tab, **Near you**: players on the player map in your state or region, each with where they play ("Plays near you, in Austin") and a Follow button. People in your city come first, then by name. Never ranked. Below it, the **player map** shows everyone who chose it, at the city or region they picked and never closer.

You choose. When you set your place, pick **My clan maps, the player map and Near you**, or **My clan maps only**. A place you set before today stays on your clan maps until you say yes on Profile, and **Leave the player map** keeps your place on your clan maps.

MCP 13.2.2 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
