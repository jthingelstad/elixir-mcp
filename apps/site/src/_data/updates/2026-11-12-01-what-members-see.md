# What members see, on one page

Settings has a new page, **What members see**. It gathers who sees what of you as Elixir works today: what signed-in members see of your players, what a battle link or a shared highlight shows to someone with no account, and what only you see, such as your nicknames, your email, your Discord name and your account's records.

It also shows your follow slots as Players & tracking counts them, and links to the pages where your choices live: Discord, your highlights, Players & tracking and your place on the clan map. Nothing on the page changes a setting.

A lens draws your main player's latest battle as the battle page shows it to a member who is not you, and to someone holding the link.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
