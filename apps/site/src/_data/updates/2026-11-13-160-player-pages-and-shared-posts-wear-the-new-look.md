# Player pages and shared posts wear the new look

A player's page opens on a card with their initials, their name, the
verified badge and their following, followers and highlights counts, each
a tap to its tab. The timeline is a card per item under the day it
happened, each with its medal; highlights, moments and follows took the
same look.

A shared highlight's page is one card: who won and the score, both decks
side by side with the score between, the elixir numbers and the towers'
hitpoints, how it ended, and **The full battle** for everything else.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
