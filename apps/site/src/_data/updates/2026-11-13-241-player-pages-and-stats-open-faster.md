# Player pages and Stats open faster

Your own player page and Stats now ask much less of Elixir's record to
open, so they show up sooner.

- A player's page asks for its cheers and your clans at the same time
  as the page, not after it.
- Stats' Season, Cards and Decks tabs read only your battles in the
  window, however long your record gets.

What each page shows, and who can see it, is the same.
