# Your place shows on every map

Your place now also shows on the **player map** and in **Near you**, not only on your clan's map. There is no choice to make any more: your clan's verified members see it on the clan map, and anyone signed in to Elixir sees it on the player map and in Near you. It is still a country, a region or a city, never closer.

To stop showing it, remove your place: **Settings › Profile › Your place**, then **Remove**.

MCP 13.2.2 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
