# The privacy page covers connecting Discord

[Privacy](/docs/privacy) now says what Elixir keeps when you connect Discord or sign in with it: your Discord user id and username, used to sign you in and to tell your clan's Discord agent who you are. Discord's sign-in also gives Elixir your email address when Discord has verified it. Elixir uses that address only to find or create your account, and keeps nothing else Discord sends. Disconnect on Profile removes the id and username. Discord is now on the list of services Elixir uses. MCP and JSON API unchanged.
