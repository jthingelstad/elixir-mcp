# Together with players you met

A player's page now has a Together tab for anyone you played in the last 30 days, whether you follow them or not. The tab shows how many games you have together, and opens on the games where you teamed up or played against each other. Tapping someone in People to follow or Find because you met them in a battle opens their page on Together.

Boat battles no longer count as meeting someone, so attacking a clan's boat never puts its members in your suggestions.
