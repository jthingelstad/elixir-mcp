# The rail stays in reach, and Ladder follows the mode you chose

On a wide screen the rail down the side of the Console, Ladder and
Elixir Clan stays in view as a long page scrolls, with Send feedback at
its foot. On a phone, the folded rail carries one mark when something
waits on another page: an unread timeline, or Clan Actions waiting for
you. Send feedback in the Console now opens a sheet over the page you
are on, as it does on Ladder and in Clan, and the sheet links what you
have filed.

Ladder's Decks and Days played put the mode in the address first. On
Decks its decks lead, and a row of links jumps to each mode's; on Days
played its mark leads each day, with the other modes still there,
quieter.

In Elixir Clan, Scout sits under Manage for elders too, and links into
the Console open without leaving the app. The Discord nudge on Actions
opens Settings on the Discord setting, and Add your player on the
Console's Overview lands on the tag field.

MCP 11.13.1 and JSON API 3.2.0, unchanged.
