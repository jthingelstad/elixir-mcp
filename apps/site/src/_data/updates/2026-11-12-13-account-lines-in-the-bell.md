# Your account's changes move to the bell

Home's feed is now people's play: sessions, firsts, moments, highlights and
your clan. What changed on your account (a player or clan added, recording
started or stopped, a player another account verified) is in the **bell**,
under **Your account**, and a burst of them is one line, such as "Recording
started for 24 players". Following many players at once no longer fills
your feed with notices or pushes play out of it.

Sign-ins stay in Settings › Sign-ins & devices. Settings' Timeline page and
your RSS link are unchanged.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
