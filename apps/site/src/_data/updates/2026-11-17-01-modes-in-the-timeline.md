# Ranked, challenges and 2v2 in your feed

Your feed now tells each mode the way the game does.

- **Ranked** shows the league you played in, and a climb names the win that got you there. Ultimate Champion shows your rating from start to finish. A Ranked win below Ultimate Champion no longer counts as +30 trophies.
- **Challenges** show their record and how far your run got ("up to 5 wins"). Event names fit on one line, and a drafted deck has no archetype name.
- **Played together.** A 2v2 names your teammate, and a friendly or clanmate battle names who you played, when Elixir already follows them.
- Path of Legends is called **Ranked** everywhere now, like in the game.

MCP 13.4.0. JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
