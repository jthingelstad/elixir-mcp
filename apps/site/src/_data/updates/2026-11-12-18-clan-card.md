# Clan's head is a clan card, and the Feed has the race on top

The head of every Clan page now says what the clan is under its name: its
tag, its members and open places, whether it is invite only, and the
trophies it asks to join. A row of chips follows: the clan's place and fame
in this week's race, such as "2nd in the Colosseum · 84,200 fame", and the
rules its leaders saved, such as "In Clan Wars", "16 war decks in 2 weeks"
or "Removal at 10 quiet days". A rule the saved policy does not set has no
chip.

While a race is on, Clan › **Feed** opens with it: "Clan Wars · race open,
day 2 of 4", a bar of the clan's war decks this race week against four a war
day for each member so far, and its place of the clans, its fame and about
when the race closes. The decks are the week's, never a day's.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
