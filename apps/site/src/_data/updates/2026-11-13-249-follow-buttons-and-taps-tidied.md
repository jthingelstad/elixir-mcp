# One Follow button, and easier taps

More polish.

- Every **Follow** looks the same now: the purple pill, wherever you
  follow from.
- Sent emails in Settings, battle rows and card names on Stats are easier
  to tap on a phone, and so are the product news box on sign-in and the
  front page's links.
- Headings no longer leave one word alone on their last line.
- On a wide screen, long notes keep to a comfortable line length.
- Hero and Evo badges stay on one line on small cards.
- An empty Highlights tab on your own page says how to post one.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
