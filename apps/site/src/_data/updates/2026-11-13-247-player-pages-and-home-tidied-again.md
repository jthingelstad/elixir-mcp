# Player pages and Home, tidied again

More polish.

- On a player's page, **Following** and **Their stats** share one row, and
  the timeline starts higher. Tap **Following** to unfollow; it asks first.
- **Follow everyone** in a player's clan is now on their **Follows** tab.
- Your own players' pages light **You** in the bottom tabs.
- Home's filter chips no longer tuck one chip under **New** on a phone,
  and People you know sits tighter.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
