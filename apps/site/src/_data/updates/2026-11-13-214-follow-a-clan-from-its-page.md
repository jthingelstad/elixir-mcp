# Follow a clan from its page

A clan's page now has **Follow** beside its name, just like a player's
page. Tap it and Elixir asks first: it says what gets recorded (the
clan's roster, who joins and who goes, and its races). It takes one of
your clan slots, or none if an agent of yours already follows that clan.
Then it reads **Following**.

Your own clan is usually followed for you already, so there it just
reads **Following**. You stop following a clan under **Settings ›
Players & following**.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
