# Home wears the new look

Home is drawn the way the approved design draws it: each item is a card
with its player's initials, a moment has its medal and its battle's result,
score and opponent, and a sitting shows its battles, wins, losses and
trophies as figures above its bars, with the deck it was played with most.
A clan's items carry the clan's name and its gold edge. The buttons, chips,
panels and top bar every page shares took the same look, and the bell
shows how many things are new.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
