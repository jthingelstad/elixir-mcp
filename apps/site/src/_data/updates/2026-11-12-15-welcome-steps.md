# Home says what's left to set up, and Your week counts cheers

A new account's Home now opens with **Welcome to Elixir, 2 of 4 done**
above the feed: add your player, verify it's you, follow a few people and
choose your digest. Each step says what was done or links to where you do
it. When all four are done the steps go, and so does the folded **Setup
details** they replace.

**Your week** also counts the cheers other people gave the items about you
since Monday. It's a count, never who.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
