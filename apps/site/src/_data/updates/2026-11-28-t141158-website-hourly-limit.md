# Browsing Elixir leaves your AI's limit alone

Using Elixir's website and your connected AI no longer share one hourly limit.

- Your Timeline, Find, player pages and Stats have an hourly limit of their own.
- Your AI's calls have theirs. Tapping around the website never uses it up.
- Usage and limits now shows only your AI's calls. Your own reads on the website were never counted against your daily budget, and now they're not listed there either.
