# New posts reach you

New posts and questions now join **N new** while your Timeline is open.
Tap when you are ready to refresh. An edit changes the words without
counting as another new post.

Your existing digest can include new posts, questions and attached decks
or cards from the people and accounts you follow. **Read and reply** opens
the post in Elixir after sign-in. Your weekly, daily or off choice stays
the same.

Posts hidden, deleted, reported or blocked before the digest is prepared
stay out. Copies already prepared or delivered by email cannot be taken
back.

No MCP or JSON API version change.
