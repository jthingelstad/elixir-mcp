# Add your player tag when you create your account

The signup form has an optional **Your player tag** field. A tag that is not a Clash Royale tag is refused on the form before any email is sent, and an empty field is fine. Once you verify your email, the new account tracks that player as your primary and opens on its page with the first capture on its way; without a tag it opens on Tracking with the tag field ready. Plain sign-in has no tag field, and an account that already exists keeps its players. MCP and JSON API unchanged.
