# Your clan's Rules

Clan's policy is now your clan's Rules, and it's much simpler. Leaders answer six short questions in Run › Rules, see the card players will see in the clan list as they go, and get a heads-up when something won't work, with a one-tap fix. Everyone else gets a Rules tab showing how the clan plays, with your own numbers beside each weekly goal.

How serious a clan is now reads Chill, Steady or Hardcore. There's a link on the Rules tab to share your clan's card with a friend who'd fit. [Set your clan's rules](/docs/clan-policy) has the details.
