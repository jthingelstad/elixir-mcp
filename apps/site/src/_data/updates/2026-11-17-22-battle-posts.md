# Post about a battle

Highlights are now posts. A great battle you want to show off is a post with the battle attached: tap **Post** on the battle's page, add your words or don't, and it goes out.

- **Same quick tap**: words are optional, and one post per player per battle.
- **Nothing lost**: every highlight you posted is a post now, with its words, cheers and comments. Its share link still opens.
- **Same reach**: your friends see it on their Timeline, your clanmates in the clan's Feed, and it still goes to your digest, notifications, RSS and Discord.
- **On a player's page**: their posts sit at the top of the Timeline tab. The Highlights tab is gone.
- **In your clan's Discord**: the leaders' setting is now called **Shared posts about battles**.

[Post about a battle](/docs/battles#post-about-a-battle) has the details.

MCP 13.11.0. JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
