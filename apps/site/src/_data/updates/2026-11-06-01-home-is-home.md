# Home is where you land

Signing in now lands on Home, and every page wears the same top bar and footer, with Home, Ladder, Clan and Search as tabs at the foot of a phone. The word Console is gone: what it held is Home for your day and Settings, in the account menu, for your account, and old links and bookmarks still arrive. A sign-in link that was taking you somewhere still takes you there. Overview is gone with it: its setup steps are on Home, a player's activity is on their record under Tracking, and the question to try with your agent is on Connections. Signing in is at `/signin`, and the service page is at `/data/service`.

MCP 11.16.1 and JSON API 3.3.0, unchanged.
