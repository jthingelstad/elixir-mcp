# New on Home, when you want it

Home's feed no longer jumps while you're reading it. When new battles or moments arrive, a small **1 new** (or **3 new**) button appears over the top of your feed and stays in view as you scroll. Tap it to see them at the top. Coming back to the tab or landing your own battles works the same way: the button tells you, and nothing moves until you tap. Home checks about once a minute while it's on screen, and not at all while the tab is hidden.

MCP 13.2.1 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
