# New on Home, when you want it

Home's feed no longer jumps while you're reading it. When new battles or moments arrive, a small **1 new** (or **3 new**) button appears over the top of your feed and stays in view as you scroll. Tap it to see them at the top. Coming back to the tab works the same way: the button tells you, and nothing moves until you tap. It counts a battle recorded late too, even one that belongs further down your feed. When you tap the freshness chip to check your own battles, the new ones show straight away. Home checks about once a minute while it's on screen, and not at all while the tab is hidden.

MCP 13.2.1 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
