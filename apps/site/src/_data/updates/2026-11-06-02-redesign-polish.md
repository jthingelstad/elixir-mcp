# The last of the redesign's small things

Timeline reads in order: a sitting shows and sorts at its last game, and under a day's heading a row shows only the time. The Timeline tab now carries a plain mark when something is new, and the New chip on the page keeps the count, so the two no longer disagree. A game opened in place says who it was against and the score: "Win 3-0 against a player, Trophy Road". Friends' moments call a person by the nickname on their card, in your view only, and Watching rows spell modes the way everything else does. On Home, step 3 stops mentioning your player when you have not added one.

Through your agent, a first player named as a friend now stays a friend, as Home's Follow always meant. Standing's page says plainly that its Path of Legends and donation weeks start ten hours before The week does, and what a daily reading really covers.

MCP 11.17.0, JSON API 3.3.0 unchanged.
