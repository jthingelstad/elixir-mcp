# Connect Discord, and sign in with it

Your profile has **Connect Discord**: Discord asks you to let Elixir
know who you are, and your Discord account is connected to your Elixir
account. After that, **Continue with Discord** on the sign-in page signs
you in, and your clan's Discord agent knows it is you without asking.
New here? **Continue with Discord** creates your account from the email
address Discord has verified. Elixir keeps only your Discord id and
username; **Disconnect** on your profile removes them.

For agents: `on_behalf_of: "discord:<user id>"` now resolves through a
person's connected Discord, and `meta.asker` says who an `on_behalf_of`
named and how.

MCP 11.14.0 and JSON API 3.3.0 (the `meta` schema lists `asker`).
