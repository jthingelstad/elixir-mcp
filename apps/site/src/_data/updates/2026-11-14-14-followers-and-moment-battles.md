# Every follower shown, and moments that open their battle

A player's **Followers** list now shows everyone. A follower who verified
their player has the gold check, as before. One who added a player but
hasn't verified it shows as that player, marked **Not verified**. An
account with no player yet shows as an Elixir account, and nothing more.

On a player's **Moments** and **Timeline** tabs, a moment Elixir saw happen
in a battle, like a promotion won on a 3-0, now has **Open the battle**.
A moment with no battle behind it has no link.

MCP 13.2.0: `players_follows` says which followers are verified. JSON API
5.1.0 unchanged.
