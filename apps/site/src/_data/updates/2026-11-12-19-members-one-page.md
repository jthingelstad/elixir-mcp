# Clan › Members is one page, with Follow on every row

Clan › **Members** no longer has a row of pages under it. It is the
roster, with **Find a member** over it, and each row has its own
**Follow**: the player page's, asked first with what it costs, against
the same player slots as Follow everyone. Your own players have none, and
someone you follow already says "Following". On a phone each member is
still one row: the name, then the trophies and Follow beside it.

What was on the row moved, and nobody sees less than before:

- **Standing** opens Clan › **Standings**, above the season's races, for
  every member.
- **Recruit**'s copy is in **Spread the word** at the foot of Members, for
  every member: tap **Write the recruiting copy**.
- **Scout** follows it at the foot, for elders, co-leaders and the leader.
- **Review** is in **Run**, beside Waiting and Decided, for the leader and
  co-leaders.

Old addresses and links in mail still open the right place.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
