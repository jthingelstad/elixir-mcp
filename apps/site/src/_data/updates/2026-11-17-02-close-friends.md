# Your circle this week

Star a few friends as close friends, and see how their week went at the top of your feed.

- **Your circle** shows each close friend's battles, days played and sessions since Monday, plus a thing or two the timeline told about them. Tap a face to open their newest session.
- It's in order of who played last. Nobody is ranked, and a friend who hasn't played just says "No battles this week".
- A **Close friends** chip on the feed shows only them, and stays picked for your next visit.
- Star or unstar a friend on their page, in Settings › Players & following, or right in your circle.

Only you see your stars. Your friend is never told.

MCP 13.4.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
