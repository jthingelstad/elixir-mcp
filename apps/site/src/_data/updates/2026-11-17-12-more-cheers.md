# Twenty cheers, glyphs alone

Cheers are glyphs now, with no words beside them, and there are twenty to pick from.

- **Tap the thumbs up** and the item is cheered with **Well played** at once. Tap it again to take your cheer back.
- **The smile beside it** opens six cheers in a row: Well played, On fire, Royal, Wow, Ha and Grind, the same six as before. Royal is a gem now.
- **More** at the end of the row shows all twenty in a grid, with new ones like Love it, Good game, Congrats, Clutch, High five, Champion, Big brain, Ice cold and Getting better.
- **Your last pick from More** waits just before More next time, when there's room.
- **Not sure what one means?** Hover over it, reach it with the keyboard, or hold your finger on it, and its name shows.

A busy item shows its top cheers with their numbers and adds up the rest, such as **+4**. Counts still say how many, never who. The full list is in [Cheers](/docs/timeline#cheers).
