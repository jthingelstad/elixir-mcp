# Home is ready before you open it

Your week, the bell and your clan's card are now kept ready even when you
have not opened Home for a while: the week and the bell are rebuilt every
two hours for a week after your last visit, and the bell is built for you
before its first read. Your clan's card shows the copy it kept, up to a
week old, and swaps in a fresh one a moment later instead of making you
wait. Two pages opened at once share one build. If People to follow cannot
be read, it now offers **Try again**.

Who may see the card, your role and the Actions waiting for you are still
read fresh every time, and a role change, a policy change, a hold or
leaving the clan still builds the card again at once.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
