# Explore folds into the player page and Find

Explore is gone. What it showed about a player is on their page, under a
new **Record** tab: the last 30 days as Elixir recorded them (battles, wins
and losses, the trophies they moved, the deck played most) and the last
profile read, with its trophies, best and lifetime counts. It is read only
when you open the tab.

Looking someone up is Find and the search sheet, as it already was. A war
week typed into search, such as "S135 W3", opens your clan's War tab on
that season. A clan you are not in has its tile in the clan directory. A
battle has its own page. The deck pages and the long battle lists are
retired; Stats keeps your decks.

Old Explore addresses, bookmarks and links in mail still open the closest
page.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
