# Home and Find, tidied

A polish pass on Home and Find. Nothing you can do changed; it reads
cleaner.

- **People you know** shows why on each row, and **Free** when following
  someone costs nothing. What following the ticked players costs is one
  line by **Follow these**: "8 players · uses 7 of your 50 follows".
- **Find's Suggested** and **From your battles** drop the per-row cost
  and keep **Free**. From your battles says when you last met the way
  Home does: "Last met today, 3:52 pm".
- On a phone, Home's head takes two rows instead of four, so the feed
  starts higher.
- The battle link on Home's feed says **Open the battle**, as it does
  everywhere else.

MCP and JSON API unchanged.
