# Find your people while your battles arrive

Add your player and start following familiar clanmates and friends while
Elixir reads your battles. Your other setup steps stay under **Finish
your setup**.

Choose who to follow and confirm once. Your remaining choices stay put,
and **Open Timeline** lets you start reading when you're ready.

An existing profile no longer holds up a missing first battle-log read.
Elixir can also ask its collectors for a newly followed clan's missing
roster, within the same rate budget.

MCP 13.11.1; JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
