# What the clan's model is never told, said exactly

Settings and the Clan docs now say exactly which notes the clan's model is sent. What you type in **What should it say?** when you draft is sent as you wrote it, beside what Elixir keeps about the clan in its leaders' own words, such as its recruiting words, its award names and, for the activity channel, the voice you describe (all of it for [drafts](/docs/clan-actions#the-words-to-send) and for [the activity channel](/docs/clan-policy#the-clans-activity-in-discord)). Notes saved on an Action, yours or another leader's, are never sent, and neither are tags, holds, a removal's reasons or inactivity numbers, or a member's share of the clan's fame. The awards page now says the model is told the winners and their numbers, while Elixir still writes the complete list of recipients itself. Nothing about what is sent has changed; only the wording. MCP and JSON API unchanged.
