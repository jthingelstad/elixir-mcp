# Your newest battles when you open Elixir

Open Elixir right after a battle and it now checks for it. The freshness
chip says "Checking for new battles…" while it looks, and your Home and
your player page show the new battle when it lands, usually within a
minute, with no reload.

Tap the chip to check again. Opening your own player page or a battle you
can highlight checks too. It looks at your own verified players only, at
most once every five minutes.

Every player's page has a Check for new battles button too. On someone
else's page, like an opponent you just played, it reads their latest
battles once, without following them.

The chip's "min ago" now keeps counting while the page is open.

MCP 13.1.1 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
