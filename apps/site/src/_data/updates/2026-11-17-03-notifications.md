# Notifications on your phone

Elixir can now ping your phone or computer when something about you
happens, even when you're not in the app.

- **Cheers on your play**, new followers of your players, Clan Actions waiting for you and answers to your feedback. How many, never who.
- Turn them on in Settings › Notifications, on each device. Elixir asks your browser's permission only when you tap.
- On iPhone and iPad, add Elixir to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen).
- A switch for each kind, and every device listed with a Remove. At most one every ten minutes.
- Tap a notification to open what it's about.

MCP 13.3.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
