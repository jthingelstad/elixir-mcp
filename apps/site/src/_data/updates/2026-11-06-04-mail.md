# Mail opens the right page

A name in mail now opens your door to it: your own player opens Ladder, someone in your clan opens their page in Clan, and anyone else opens their record. A clan you are in opens at Clan, Season. If you are signed out, the page says why it needs you to sign in and brings you back.

A clan with nothing to tell sends no report that week. A race counts as something, and a report with a gap in its reading still comes and says so. Gone quiet now shows the last battle's date, and only members of the clan read it. If you raced, the report has a line for you.

Clan actions waiting says why each action is waiting, and its subject leads with the action. A first on another of your players now waits for Tuesday's mail; your main player's still come as they happen. The footer is one line, with a short mail id, and the mail no longer says Console.

JSON API 3.4.0: a Clan mail line can carry a reason and an action number. MCP unchanged.
