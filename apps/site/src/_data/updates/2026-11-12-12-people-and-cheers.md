# People to follow, Find's sections, and cheers on more pages

Home has a new panel beside the feed, below it on a phone: **People to
follow**. It names a few players from your battles this week, your clan and
the players your friends follow, each with why ("You played them twice this
week", "In your clan") and whether following them is free or uses a slot.
Nobody is ranked by trophies or wins. Your clan's panel also says how this
week's river race stands.

**Find** now has sections: **Your clan**, with **Follow my clan**;
**Search**; **From your battles**, the players you met in the last 30 days
with how often and how it went; the **Clan directory**; and **Your link**.

Cheers now show on a player's page and on a battle's page too, for the items
in your own feed. Signed out, a battle's page shows no cheers.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
