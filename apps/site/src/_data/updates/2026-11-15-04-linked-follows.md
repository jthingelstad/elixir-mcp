# Follow a player, follow the person

A Verified player and its account are now one follow.

- Follow a Verified player, and you follow their account and their other Verified players too.
- Follow an account, and you follow all its Verified players, including ones it verifies later.
- Unfollow any of them, and the whole follow ends.

Claimed and Imported players stay just themselves. Follow tells you first what it uses of your player slots. If a player can't be added, your bell says why.

If you already followed a Verified player, you now follow their account too.

MCP 13.3.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
