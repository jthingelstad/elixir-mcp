# Welcome to Elixir finishes for good

Home's **Welcome to Elixir** steps now stay gone once all four are done.
Before, unfollowing everyone brought them back at 3 of 4; now Elixir notes
the day you finished, and nothing you change afterwards undoes it.
Following one player completes the follow step, and turning your digest
off counts as choosing it.

MCP and the JSON API unchanged.
