# Sessions in your digest

Your digest now tells your friends' sessions the way your feed does.

- Each friend's biggest sessions, up to three: the result ("Won 8 of 12 on Trophy Road, 5 in a row"), their record and trophies, three-crown wins, the widest win, a Ranked climb or a challenge run, and who they played with.
- The deck they played most, with its eight cards on one session each.
- A quick session of one or two battles is one line.
- Tap a session's result to open its own page.

Sessions open their own page from the bell, Cheers on your play, your RSS link and Discord posts too.

MCP 13.4.0 and JSON API 5.1.0 unchanged.
