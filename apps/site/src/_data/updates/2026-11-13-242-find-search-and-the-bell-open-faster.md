# Find, search and the bell open faster

Find, the search sheet and the bell now ask much less of Elixir's record
to open, so they show up sooner.

- Searching by name finds the players first and looks up their clan and
  trophies only for the ones you see.
- Find's Suggested and From your battles tabs read just your own battles.
- The bell skips a count it never shows.

What each page shows, and who can see it, is the same.
