# Your passport

Every Elixir account has a passport. It holds Stamps, small marks for
milestones in Elixir: verifying your player, picking your picture,
following your people, cheering them on. A Stamp never stands for a win,
trophies or a rank.

## Find it

- Tap your picture in the top bar, then **Passport**.
- Or open **You** and tap **Your passport**.
- **Settings > Profile** shows a preview at the top.

## What a Stamp shows

Each Stamp wears its name and the day you earned it. A Stamp from before
passports existed says **Before** over its day, and its detail says
"Earned before" that day. A Stamp you have not earned yet is a plain
outline that says **Not earned**, or **Optional** for the ones you can
skip.

Some Stamps have three levels, I, II and III. Every level wears the same
colour. Levels count totals, not streaks, and an earned level stays
earned.

Tap a Stamp to see its rule and its levels. On your own passport it also
shows how far along you are and a button to the place where you earn it.

## Other members' passports

When a verified player has a passport, their player page shows a row with
their latest Stamps and **Open passport**. Any signed-in member can open
it. They see what was earned and when, and the rules. They never see your
progress, your settings or where you are on a clan map: the On the Map
Stamp only says you are on one.

**Copy passport link** on your passport copies the address members can
open.

## The gold mark

The Player verified Stamp keeps the day your player was first verified.
The gold mark beside a name shows who is verified now, so it can go while
the Stamp stays. See [Verified players](/docs/verified-players).
