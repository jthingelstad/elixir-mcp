# Your clan's season record

**Season**, beside [The week](/docs/clan-week) in Elixir Clan, shows recorded
war decks, member period points and contributors. Every clan member can read
it, verified or not, with or without a clan policy. Each contributor counts
once in the season total even when they played in several races.

Choose a season to see its races in order. **So far** is the running season;
**Closed** means closure is recorded. **Closure unconfirmed** means the record
has not established closure. An upcoming race is different from an earlier
race with no capture. Unknown readings appear as a dash, not zero. A known
zero is the counter the record saw, never evidence of missing capture or
inactivity.

## What the totals include

The totals use one read covering up to eight weeks. They include known war
counters for the current members and former members represented in that
read, counted once per player and race. The page names how many current and
former members have readings. It shows the record's freshness, how many race
sections are recorded, missing readings and whether the season begins before
the read or roster recording.

These are recorded contributions for that represented population, not proof
of every contribution to the clan. A race in the record does not by itself
prove complete member capture. A season can be closed and still have a
partial record; missing sections or member readings remain unknown.

Points are members' **period points**, not the boat's fame. Decks are weekly
game counters: the page does not assign them to war days or divide points by
decks, since decks played after an early finish can earn no points. Current
races are marked **So far**, and completed races keep their own rows.

Donation and battle totals are not part of this view. Their weekly
fields cannot establish clan-attributable season totals after roster changes.
Seasons outside the bounded read are unavailable here, not missing activity.
Opening Season makes no policy decision, raises no action, grants no award
and sends no mail.
