# Set your clan's rules

Every clan runs differently, so Clan has no rules of its own. A clan's
**rules** say what members do each week, how Elder works, when a quiet
member gets kicked, and which [Actions](/docs/clan-actions) to raise.
Until a Leader saves them, nothing is judged: no standing, no Actions, no
awards.

## Who sets them

The Leader and Co-Leaders, under **Run › Rules**, once their player is
[verified](/docs/verify). Leaders can set and save rules at any clan size.
Actions, standing and awards wait for 10 members and start at 10. Saving
below ten raises no announcement Action. The rules are Elixir's agreement
for the clan, not a game setting.

Every member reads saved rules on the clan's **Rules** tab, and on
[Standing](/docs/standing) under **How it works here**.

## The Rules tab

Every member's **Rules** tab shows how the clan plays, the way you'd show
it to a friend:

- **How the clan plays**: a line on what the clan is about, and chips for
  Clan Wars, how serious it is, what it counts, whether Elder is earned,
  the kick day, the member count and the trophies to join.
- **Each week, do one of these** (or all of them, or over a few weeks):
  each goal, with **You** and your own number beside it.
- **Becoming Elder**: earned or picked by hand, and what makes a best
  player.
- **Staying in the clan**: how often to battle, the quiet, warning and
  kick days, and **I'll be away** to tell the clan you're away.
- **What we do together**.
- **Know someone who'd fit?**: a link to your clan's card in the
  [clan list](/docs/clan-directory), to copy and send.

Each part ends with a quiet line on who sees what about you under it:

- **What we do together**: nothing about you. Every member reads it, and
  so does the [clan list](/docs/clan-directory).
- **Each week**: every member sees your war decks, donations and battles
  each week, on [the week](/docs/clan-week).
- **Becoming Elder**: when Elder is earned and **Members see where
  everyone stands** is on, every member sees on Standing who's rising and
  who's slipping. Otherwise only you and the Leader and Co-Leaders see
  where you stand. Only the Leader and Co-Leaders see a promotion or
  demotion before it's done, and it never goes to the clan's Discord. It's
  shared with the clan once it's done.
- **Staying in the clan**: only you and the Leader and Co-Leaders see your
  days without a battle, whether you're at risk, and your away. A kick
  Action never goes to the clan's
  [Discord](/docs/clan-actions-delivery#actions-in-the-clans-discord), and
  a kick is shared with the clan once it's done.

The tab never shows another member's standing or clock, or a leaders-only exception. Elders read
the Elders' notes about a member, and the Leader and Co-Leaders read every
note. Closed Actions and past versions of the rules are for the Leader and
Co-Leaders.

## Quick starts

A clan with no rules yet starts from a quick start. Pick the closest one
and it fills every question, yours to change before you save:

| Quick start | Counts | How serious |
|---|---|---|
| A war clan | war decks and cards donated | Hardcore |
| A climbing clan | ranked battles and trophies | Steady |
| A donation clan | cards donated | Steady |
| A social clan | nothing; Elders picked by hand | Chill |
| A bit of everything | all four | Steady |

Every quick start turns on kicking quiet members, welcomes, goodbyes and
announcements, so read those questions before saving. Once rules are
saved, **Start over from a quick start** replaces the whole draft.

## The six questions

The editor asks [six questions](/docs/clan-policy-tabs), each on its own
card, numbered, and written for a phone. Each changed answer says what it
was. Settings most clans never touch sit under **More** inside their
question.

1. **What is your clan about?**
2. **What should members do each week?**
3. **Who becomes Elder?**
4. **When do quiet members get kicked?**
5. **Welcomes and goodbyes**
6. **What does the clan see?**

## What players see in the clan list

Beside the questions on a wide screen, and above them on a phone, the
editor shows your clan's card as the [clan list](/docs/clan-directory)
will show it, drawn from your draft as you change it. It uses only the
rules' public part: Clan Wars, how serious, what you count, playing
together and the kick day. The description is your clan's own, from the
game.

## The Rules check

Under the card, the **Rules check** points out what won't work:

- **Elder seats are full**: there is room for fewer Elders than you have,
  so nobody new can be promoted. Its button raises the seats to make room.
- **On but does nothing**: something you count has no goal and no share
  of Elder. Its button turns it off.
- **Question N needs a fix**: an answer is out of range. The question is
  marked, its problem said once, at the answer.

When nothing needs fixing, it says so.

## What these rules can do today

Once rules are saved, **Before you save** lists each judgment they ask
for, with one of:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| Live | Clan acts on it now |
| Needs more evidence | it will act once the record has what is named: 10 members, saved rules, or one finished review week |
| Off | the rules don't ask for it |

The judgments listed are promoting and demoting Elders, kicking quiet
members, asking quiet members if they're away, asking how a member left,
and welcoming new members.

## Preview before you save

**Preview the last reviews**, under **Before you save**, reads the clan's
record and shows what the last four reviews would have said under the
draft, beside the saved rules: the Elder band before and after, and each
member who would read differently. No rules, Action, award or decision is
saved or raised by a preview, and no model is called. The member-count
reading and normal recorded-read audit are updated; the preview itself is
not saved. Exceptions and decision cooldowns are not applied, so it is
not an exact forecast of pending Actions. Changing any answer clears it.

## Saving

A bar at the foot of the editor stays in reach as you scroll. It says
**You changed N things**; tap it for the list, each as before and after.
**Undo all** puts the saved rules back, and **Save rules** saves. Leaving
Run with unsaved changes asks first.

Saving makes a new version; the first is version 1. Add a line on why,
under **Before you save**, if you like; the versions list, for the Leader
and Co-Leaders only, keeps who saved each and when. An answer out of
range is refused, with its question marked. The clan is read afresh under
the new version, which can raise or withdraw pending Actions at the next
evaluation, including reviews of recorded weeks. Completed decisions and
saved award grants stay as recorded; awards have a separate setup. A save
from an old copy of the page is refused instead of overwriting newer
rules, and unsaved changes survive a refetch. No save changes game
settings, kicks a member or sends a message. Saved rules list the clan in
the [clan list](/docs/clan-directory#what-it-lists), with their public
part.

Saving does not restart review streaks or the activity clock. Recorded
review boundaries are replayed under the new values; the current Trophy
Road figure is used when trophies count. A pending recommendation that
still qualifies keeps the evidence and version saved when it was raised.
Current goals, grace and waiting periods apply at reevaluation without
rewriting earlier decisions.

When **Post when the rules change** is on, a save raises **Tell the clan
how it runs** for the leaders: chat copy with an explicit durable Inbox
alternative, saying how the clan runs the first time, and what changed
after.
