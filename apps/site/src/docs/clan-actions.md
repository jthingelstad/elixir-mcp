# Actions

Clan never acts in the game and never decides for anyone. When
the clan's [policy](/docs/clan-policy) says something should happen, it
raises an **action**: one numbered call for the person or role who can
make it, with the evidence, the rule that raised it, and the words to
send when there are any. Someone decides it, makes the change in the
game, and the action's log keeps what happened.

## Inspect a member's recorded activity

Roster names, current members in the award races, Standing and Season, and
member Actions link to the member's own page under Members, for
a current member of a clan you belong to (the counts are recorded game
data; the page lives inside Clan). It shows four weeks,
each from Monday's reset to the next, of clan-scoped recorded battle counts and recorded donation counters, the
recorded war weeks' deck usage and points, and paged battles grouped into
recorded sessions. Each battle opens its full detail page. Weekly
contributions and war weeks stay distinct; decks are never assigned to war days.
Battle modes use the same names as the battle detail page; an unrecognized
mode retains its recorded name. Roster search is kept when opening activity,
returning to the roster or refreshing the page.

Battle detail is clipped to the member's current recorded stint and only
battles whose participant names this clan. A returning player's earlier
stint and other-clan play do not enter these counts. Donation counters are
unknown for a week that starts before the current recorded stint. Earlier
war weeks are omitted; weekly totals crossing the stint or fixed read
boundary remain unknown rather than being split into days. Missing or failed
coverage reads remain unknown. Profile-counter intervals ending in the last
seven days can show missing battles, but do not prove that the entire
displayed window was seen. No recorded battle is not proof of no play.
Saved Action evidence retains its original timestamp and policy version;
the activity view is a new read. Removal eligibility is always checked
against current profile readings.

## The kinds

| Action | Goes to | Words to send |
|---|---|---|
| Promote to Elder | the leader and co-leaders | clan chat |
| Demote to Member | the leader and co-leaders | clan chat |
| Remove from the clan ([the inactivity clock](/docs/inactivity-clock#the-inactivity-clock)) | the leader and co-leaders | a line for clan chat |
| Departure: kicked, left, or ignore? | the leader and co-leaders | none |
| Welcome a newcomer | elders, co-leaders and the leader | a line for clan chat |
| Going to be away? | the quiet member alone | none |
| Share this week's award standings | the leader and co-leaders | clan chat, with a durable Inbox alternative |
| Announce the season's awards | the leader and co-leaders | clan chat, with a durable Inbox alternative |
| Tell the clan how it runs | the leader and co-leaders | clan chat, with a durable Inbox alternative |

Award announcement titles name the season and the awards covered by that
message. A manual award granted after an announcement was completed can raise
a separate Action for that later recipient; the completed announcement stays
unchanged. Notification mail uses the same award scope as the Action.

Every kind but one is off until the policy turns it on. Promotions and
demotions come with ranking Elder by participation
([Standing](/docs/standing)); removals, departures, welcomes, away
questions and the two announcements each have a switch of their own.
Award standings have no switch: they come with the clan's
[awards](/docs/clan-awards#how-they-reach-the-clan).

## When one appears

Clan reads the clan every morning from 11:00 UTC, and again when someone
opens Standing or chooses **Refresh suggestions** in Today. Today normally
opens the saved evaluation with its as-of time, keeping decisions quick; its
first reading or a changed policy also evaluates the record.
A member has at most one open action of each
kind. An action that stops being true is **withdrawn**, with the reason
in its log: the member left, played again, or rejoined; a welcome waited
a week past the join.

- A **welcome** is raised for someone who joined in the last three days.
  Its suggested words may use a recently recorded career fact or a proven
  return to the clan. Missing or old evidence gets a simple welcome; a career
  total is never described as a milestone just reached.
- A **departure** is raised when someone leaves and no removal action
  explains it, because a leave and a kick look the same in the record:
  say which, so the clan's history knows. A removal marked **Done**
  explains the leave and raises nothing. If the member's removal Action
  was still open when they went (a leader kicked them before marking it),
  the departure still asks, but it names that removal ("Elixir recommended
  removing them in Action #N") and offers **Kicked** first. Nothing is
  recorded until a leader presses it, and **Left** or **Ignore** stays
  open. The removal is withdrawn, and its withdrawal and History point to
  the departure.
- **Going to be away?** goes to a member who has reached at risk on the
  [inactivity clock](/docs/inactivity-clock#the-inactivity-clock), asking them before anyone
  else is.
- An **announcement** is raised when a season's awards are decided, or
  when the leaders save a new version of the policy.
- **Award standings** are raised after a war week closes, when the clan
  has at least one award set up; a leader can also ask for the current
  season's standings from **Awards**.

## Deciding one

Open an Action to read its reason and numbers, then its folds: **What this
comes from**, its message, and **History**, the saved log and comment form.
The buttons come last. These labels do not
change which decisions your role may make.

**Today** lists what is waiting for you. A member or elder also has a fold,
**Closed in the last 30 days**, under it, and a closed Action opened from there
links back to it. The leader and co-leaders have **Decided** beside Waiting
instead: every decision, newest first. Its **Who came and went** shows recent
recorded roster changes, capped at the newest twenty events, rather than the
entire history since roster recording began. Earlier changes are not listed in
that section.

| Action | The choices |
|---|---|
| Promote, demote, remove | **Done**, or **Decline** with a reason (not now, knows the member, evidence wrong, handled in the game, other) |
| Departure | **Kicked**, **Left** or **Ignore**, with an optional note |
| Welcome | **Done** or **Decline** |
| Announcement | **Done**, once the message is marked sent, or **Decline** |
| Going to be away? | **Mark me away**, or **I'm not away** |

Make the change in the game, then mark the action. For a promotion
that means promoting the member and sending the reviewed message;
they go together, as one action. Once decided, the decision stands; an
action someone else already decided says so.

After **Done**, Clan watches the record: if the role change or the
removal shows up within the clan's outcome window (48 hours to start),
the log says it was confirmed; if not, it is flagged. After a
**Decline**, the same kind for the same member waits before it can come
back: a week for a removal and two weeks for a promotion or demotion, to
start, or what the clan set.

Every action has a log: when it was raised and by which rule and policy
version, every comment, every draft, the decision and the outcome.
Anyone who can see it can comment, up to 1,000 characters. A **Drafted
by the clan's model** entry keeps what the model wrote, exactly as it
wrote it, before anyone edited it. Only the leader and
co-leaders see those words; an elder or a member who can see the
action's log sees that a draft was made, not what it said. An older
draft's entry has no words.

An award standings update has one numbered Action containing every message in
order, in compact game copy; as-of times and coverage stay in the app for
review. Edit and copy a part, send it in the game, then choose **Mark message N
sent**. Copying alone records no delivery. Each receipt keeps the actual words,
sender and time; retries preserve it. **Done** becomes available
after every part is marked sent. **Decline** closes the update, skipping the
remaining messages and keeping parts already sent. A failed Elixir recording can be retried by
the original sender without sending the game message again.

Each part records its chosen channel and exact reviewed words. An interrupted
reply asks you to check the saved receipt before retrying; it never resends
the game message. Mixed chat and Inbox deliveries keep their separate receipts.
**Review later · leave open** returns to the list without completing or
declining an Action. It does not schedule a send or change a clan decision.

Verified leaders and co-leaders can open a declined Action from **Decided**
and choose **Reopen action**. It returns the same numbered Action
to Waiting, keeping the original decline in its log and retaining any parts
already marked sent. Completed, withdrawn and fully delivered Actions cannot
be reopened. Reopening keeps the original evidence and does not send a message.

## The words to send

A Clan Leader Message is the game's leader message: only the leader and
co-leaders can send one, it has a title of up to 24 characters and a
body of up to 180, and appears in members' Inbox, separate from chat.
Routine messages default to **Clan chat**, where members normally talk.
For award standings, final award announcements and important rule changes,
choose **Leader Message** when you want members to keep it in their Inbox.
Welcomes remain chat. Edit welcomes and removal messages (up to 200
characters a line) before copying them into the game. Each field counts
characters and warns about known chat-filter problems. Copying a field waits
until its text fits; your edits stay in place to shorten. Nothing is ever
posted for the clan automatically.

Every chat field is a separate send, bounded to 200 characters; a long heading
and body can require two fields. A merged game chat bubble does not establish
a larger single-message limit. Channel choices keep separate edit buffers in
this browser tab, including across reloads and ordinary refetches. A changed
Action context starts a new draft; closing the Action or signing out clears
its local draft words. Copying records no delivery.

Leaders report a limit of **one Leader Message per day**. Elixir cannot see
sends outside these Actions or determine the reset rule, so it never predicts
remaining capacity or disables delivery from completion history. Choose which
important message to send first and leave the others open until the game allows
another. Approximate reports describe busy chat aging out in a day or two and
Inbox messages lasting longer; these are not retention guarantees.

Check the game text after sending. A delivered Inbox message can have its body
masked with asterisks, and may have consumed the daily slot. Known text warnings
cannot guarantee readable delivery or identify every filter trigger. Elixir
cannot verify game delivery or readability and never automatically resends.
Completed decisions and saved receipts do not change.

With the clan's own model set up under **Run › Model & voice**, an open
message, welcome or removal action offers leaders and co-leaders
**Draft in our voice**. It uses the
clan's Anthropic key to draft a promotion, demotion, awards announcement
or policy-change announcement, a welcome, or a chat line to use after a
removal decision. After a leader confirms **Kicked** or **Left**, the departure
Action can also draft a respectful departure message or farewell. A recorded
departure alone does not say which happened; an unconfirmed or ignored
departure has no voice draft.

The model is told what Elixir records, so the words can be about this
member and this clan rather than anyone's:

- **The clan**: its name, its recruiting words and declared goals, its
  member count and its last few finished war weeks (place and war
  trophies).
- **Elixir's own words** for the Action, the ones the card shows with no
  draft. A draft says at least that much, then more of the member.
- **What happened**: the Action's own evidence in plain words. For a
  promotion, what the rules counted (war decks, donations, time in the
  clan) against the policy's bar; for a demotion, only that the Elder
  moves back to Member for now, never why; for the season's awards,
  each award with its winner and number; for a policy change, what the
  policy now says; for a welcome, the frozen return or recorded career
  detail with when it was seen; for a confirmed departure, the
  leader's classification and the recorded tenure.
- **The member**, by name: trophies and best, arena, career wins,
  collection level, time in the clan and any earlier stint, their recent
  war weeks as decks used and points, their recent moments in the clan's
  timeline and the awards they hold here. For a demotion only their time
  in the clan and the awards they hold. For a removal or a departure only
  their time in the clan and the weeks they played: never their
  trophies, a week they missed or why they were removed.
- **More of their record**, when the draft is about one member: every
  war week they played in the clan over the last year as decks and
  points, with their best week and their longest run of weeks in a row;
  their donations here week by week over the last 12 weeks, and in their
  career; from their game profile, years playing, favourite card, cards
  at the top Mastery level, legendary badges, their best Path of Legends
  league by name in a season Elixir recorded (Ultimate Champion, the top
  league, also from the profile's own best when that season is older
  than the record) and their Clan War Wins badge; their decks over the last 28 days, each
  mode on its own, named by archetype with games, wins and losses; their
  milestones over the last 28 days; and their role changes here. Elixir
  then picks up to four story angles from it, strongest first, and every
  number in an angle is in that record. Reaching Ultimate Champion leads
  a welcome's or a promotion's angles.
  - A **demotion** is told only what they gave before the policy's
    recent weeks, so the message can thank them for it: never those
    weeks' numbers, a zero, a missed week or how long it has been.
  - A **removal** or **departure** is told only the war weeks they
    played.
  - A promotion or a welcome is told all of it.
- **The clan's voice**: **How our messages sound**, as its leaders
  wrote it (below).
- **What you ask for** in **What should it say?**, as you wrote it.

Never sent: a tag, a note saved on the Action (yours or another
leader's), a hold, a removal's
rationale or inactivity numbers, a member's share of the clan's fame, or
anything the record does not hold. A fact the record lacks is left out.
A draft says at least what Elixir's own words say, then two to four
facts that belong to this member, each said for what it means, one to a
sentence. It never judges a member (no score, rank or
standing) and gives no advice on how to play. A demotion thanks the
member for what they did before and never says or hints why.

In clan chat a draft is lines, each its own send of up to 200
characters: up to three for a promotion, two for a welcome, a demotion
or a farewell, and one for the words after a removal. The card shows a
field for each line, with its own copy button; completing a welcome
records every line as you edited it. An awards or policy announcement
keeps its heading and message. In the Inbox a draft is a title and a
message; it aims for 150 characters of the message, short of the 180
leaders report the game takes, because that limit is reported, not
published.

A draft is marked for you to check before sending when it carries a
number or a name the model was not given, says less of the record than
Elixir's own words, hints at why in a demotion, or has a line the chat
filter may mask or that runs past the limit. It is still your draft to
edit. The model never decides whether to promote, demote or
remove anyone. Drafts are bound to the Action's context: a changed
confirmation or frozen detail clears older words and rejects an
in-flight result. A careful draft can take longer than one request; the
page keeps asking until it lands, for up to about 100 seconds.

The clan's saved words in **Recruit** say what it is about, and **How
our messages sound** in [Clan
settings](/docs/clan-settings#clan-settings) says how it sounds: the
leaders' own words, given to the model as written. They set
the tone and the wording, never the facts: everything above still holds,
and a voice never lifts a rule or adds to what is sent. The clan's
Discord activity editor reads the same voice. For a welcome or
a farewell, choose a tone and add a short note in **What should it
say?** on what to stress; the words after a removal take only a tone;
a promotion, a demotion or an announcement takes the note. Review and
edit the answer. **Put back what I had** restores your previous words.
Elders can edit and copy welcomes without using the clan's model. Drafting completes no action and sends no game message.
While a draft is loading, copying and action decisions wait for it to finish.
Review the returned words before sending and marking the action complete.

When a call's outcome is unknown, that attempt counts toward the daily
limit. Check the use log under **Run › Model & voice** before requesting another draft;
Elixir does not automatically repeat it. The daily use limit is not a dollar cap.
The use record counts attempts; an unknown result is not confirmation of
a provider charge. Review your provider's usage record for billing.
The worker checks for an existing reply before starting a request. A missing
reply or claim is confirmed separately from denied access; a denied read of
an existing object still fails without starting another model call.

## Who sees what

**Today** lists what is waiting for you and, for a member or elder, what
closed in the last 30 days. You see the actions that are yours, by your name or by your
role, and no others: another's action number answers that there is no
such action. **Nobody below co-leader ever sees a removal action or who
is on the clock.** The leader and co-leaders also have **Decided**,
every action the clan has had with its outcome, and the holds.

Everyone opens a clan on **Today**
([Finding your way](/docs/bring-your-clan#finding-your-way)).

You need a verified player to decide or comment; until then you can
read your own actions.
