# The rules' six questions

## The questions

| Question | What it decides | Under More |
|---|---|---|
| 1. What is your clan about? | Clan Wars (we war every week, we don't war, or not saying), how serious (Chill, Steady or Hardcore), and whether you like to play together | |
| 2. What should members do each week? | which of war decks, ranked battles, cards donated and trophies count, a goal for each, whether a member does their part with any one or all of them, and the weeks Elixir checks | |
| 3. Who becomes Elder? | picked by hand or your best players, what makes a best player (each count's share), Elder seats (the most of the clan who can be Elder), and how long new members wait | the band's floor, how high a best player ranks, how many reviews a promotion or demotion needs, how long a declined one waits, and each count's window |
| 4. When do quiet members get kicked? | **Suggest kicking quiet members**, the quiet, warning and kick days ([the inactivity clock](#the-inactivity-clock)), whether Elders can be kicked too, and how long members can say they're away | whether to ask quiet members if they're away, grace for meeting the goals, the roster cap, how long a declined kick waits, and the window for checking a done Action |
| 5. Welcomes and goodbyes | **Help us welcome new members** and **Ask if someone left or was kicked** | |
| 6. What does the clan see? | **Members see where everyone stands**, **Post each season's awards** and **Post when the rules change** | |

Turning a count on asks for its goal; turning it off hides it. **How
serious** sets where goals and days start in a quick start; changing it
alone does not retune answers already in the draft. **Playing together**
can be said but is never measured: the game does not say who plays with
whom. Every answer carries a line saying what it does.
[Standing](/docs/standing) explains what the counts, goals and Elder
seats mean.

**Clan Wars** records the leaders' intent: we war every week, we don't
war, or not saying, which is where new rules start. It is separate from
counting war decks, which decides goals and Elder; quick starts and
counting never set or change it. It is part of the rules' public part: the
clan's card in the [clan list](/docs/clan-directory#agents) shows it, and
`clans_directory` reads it as `war_intent`. Not saying never means not
warring.

### The inactivity clock

A member's clock counts the days since their last battle in Elixir's
record, or since they joined if they have not battled since. A rise in
the battle count on their profile counts as play too. **Quiet after**,
**Warning after** and **Kick after** are each a number of days on that
one clock: with warning at 7 and kick at 10, a member is at risk after 7
days without a battle and the Leaders get a kick Action on day 10, not
day 17. The kick day is never before the warning day. Grace for meeting
the goals adds days to the kick day. A hold, or a member's own away,
keeps the Action from being raised, and Elders are Protected unless
**Elders can be kicked too** is on.

A member who reaches the warning day is asked whether they're away, when
the rules ask quiet members. If Elixir could not read a member's
activity, or the player has left, nothing is raised for them until the
next run can read it. Elixir never kicks anyone: a kick stays a Leader's
call in the game.
