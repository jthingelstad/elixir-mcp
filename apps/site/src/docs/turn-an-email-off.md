# Turn an email off

Elixir sends four kinds of email, and every one is on for a new
account. Most people get only one of them: [the digest](/docs/email#your-digest). Each kind is one switch, and turning one off is immediate and
yours to undo. [Email](/docs/email) has what each one holds and when it
lands.

## The switches

**Settings › Emails**
([/settings/emails](/settings/emails)),
reached from the account menu, lists the scheduled kinds under **Scheduled**, each
with the day and time it arrives in your time zone: Your digest and Collector
activity. Your digest is three choices instead of a switch: **Weekly**
(Mondays, right after the weekly reset), **Daily** (every day, at the same time) or **Off**
([Your digest](/docs/email#your-digest)). Tapping Weekly or Daily turns it
on at that pace, and Off turns it off. Under it, **Clan news** is a
switch for the digest's clan part: your clans' races, joins, departures
and role changes. Turn it off and the digest keeps the people you follow
and your own week. **Every email** leaves Clan news as you set it. The two that come when something happens,
Clan actions waiting and Answers to your feedback, sit below under
**When something happens**. Each has a
line on what it is and a switch; turn one off and it stops. A kind
already sent to you links the last one, and the page lists the last
few emails sent to you, with **All sent** for the rest.

**Every email**, at the top of the page, turns all four off at once,
or all four back on. While some are on and some are off it says how
many are on.

Two of the switches only matter to some accounts, and are dimmed for
the rest: **Collector activity** needs a collector you run, and **Clan
actions waiting** needs a [verified](/docs/verify) player in a clan.

## From the email itself

Every issue carries a link to turn that kind off, and **Manage
emails**, which opens Settings › Emails. The turn-off link opens a page that asks first: nothing changes
until you press **Turn it off**. Every issue also carries the standard
one-click unsubscribe, so a mail app that shows its own unsubscribe
button turns the kind off in one step, with no page.

Either way it can be turned back on from Settings › Emails. Every change
is kept in your account's log (**Settings › Sign-ins & devices**), saying
whether it came from the page or from a link.

## One player or one clan

The switches are per kind, never per player or per clan. To hear less
about one subject:

- **Your digest**: switch off the player's **Notifications** on its
  page under Settings › Players & following, and it leaves the digest and your timeline while
  recording carries on ([Follow a friend](/docs/follow-a-friend#the-notifications-switch)).
- **Your clans in your digest**: switch **Clan news** off under the
  digest and no clan's part is in it.
- **A clan in your digest**: stopping following a clan takes its news
  out of your digest, and stops its recording too unless something else
  keeps it recorded.

To stop them all, turn **Every email** off; turn it back on to have all
four again.

## Mail that has no switch

Sign-in codes and links, and the welcome email when your account opens,
are service mail. They come whatever your switches say, and sign-in
codes are not in the list of emails sent to you.

## The newsletter

The product newsletter is not one of the four. It goes out through a
separate service, Buttondown, and you leave it with the unsubscribe link
in any newsletter issue.

For a new account, **Send me Elixir product news** on the sign-in form is
checked by default. Uncheck it before requesting your email to avoid newsletter
enrollment. Your email proof preserves the choice; subsequent sign-ins leave
existing choices and unsubscribes alone. The **Every email** switch controls
Elixir's reports and notices, not Buttondown or sign-in/welcome mail.
