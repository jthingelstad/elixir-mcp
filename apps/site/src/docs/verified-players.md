# Verified, Claimed and Imported players

Every player on Elixir is one of three things:

- **Verified**: an account added this player and proved it is theirs.
  Nobody else can claim a Verified player.
- **Claimed**: an account says it is this player but has not proved it
  yet. More than one account can claim the same player.
- **Imported**: someone follows this player, so Elixir records their
  battles. No account has claimed them.

## The rings

A Verified player wears a gold ring around their avatar and a gold check
beside their name. A Claimed player wears a grey ring. An Imported player
has no ring. The ring stays when a player uses initials, has not chosen a
profile image, or the image cannot load. Choosing a card image does not
verify a player.

The rings follow the player through Home, Find, their player page, Stats,
clan lists and shared battles. They come from the current claims; a
follow, nickname or clan role cannot grant one.

## Who claims a player

A player's page says which state it is in and which account holds it:

- Verified: "Verified by" the account that proved it.
- Claimed: "Claimed by" each account that says it is this player.
- Imported: no account, just a note that someone follows them.

Tap an account to open its account page.

## Account pages

An account page shows a person on Elixir:

- The player they say they are, and their alts, each Verified or Claimed.
- Their badges, their place if they set one, and their Stamps, with
  **Open passport**.
- Who they follow and how many follow them.
- One timeline: their players' battles and news, plus the account's own
  news, like a Stamp earned, a place set, or a player verified.

Tap **Follow** to follow an account. You also follow every player it has
verified, now and later, and its news (Stamps, places, verified players)
shows on your Home. Elixir tells you first what the follow uses of your
player slots. Open your own account page from **You**, then **Your account
page**.

## Linked follows

A Verified player and its account are one follow:

- Follow a Verified player, and you follow the account that verified it,
  plus its other Verified players.
- Follow an account, and you follow all its Verified players. When it
  verifies another player later, you follow that one too.
- Unfollow the account, or any of its Verified players, and the whole
  follow ends.
- Claimed and Imported players stay just themselves. Anyone can claim a
  player, so a claim never pulls in an account.

If Elixir can't take one of the players (no free player slot, or you
don't have a player of your own yet), it skips that one and your bell
says so. You still follow the rest.

A verification is big news: once an account verifies a player, nobody
else can claim them. It shows on the account's timeline and on Home for
anyone who follows the account or the player.

[Verify your player](/docs/verify) to prove it is yours.
[Your profile image](/docs/your-account#your-profile-image) is a separate
choice. Linking Discord has its own badge on the player page.

The site and Docs show your player after a recent app visit. Otherwise,
their header uses a generic account icon. Open Elixir to see your player’s
current state.
