# Verified players

A verified player wears a gold ring around their avatar and a gold check
beside their name. The ring stays when they use initials, have not chosen
a profile image, or the image cannot load. Choosing a card image does not
verify a player.

The state follows the player through Home, Find, their player page, Stats,
clan lists and shared battles. It comes from the current verified claim;
a follow, nickname or clan role cannot grant it.

[Verify your player](/docs/verify) to prove it is yours.
[Your profile image](/docs/your-account#your-profile-image) is a separate
choice. Linking Discord has its own badge on the player page.
