# War decks

Deck-set recommendations and upgrade recommendations have retired. Elixir records what you played and what you hold: use `battles_query` for your war games and duel rounds, `battles_decks` for your recorded decks, `players_collection` for observed inventory, and `war_current` or `war_history` for participation.
