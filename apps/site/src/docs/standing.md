# Standing and the Elder band

A clan on Elixir Clan writes down what it counts, in its
[policy](/docs/clan-policy), and **Standing** reads every member against
it. It is participation, not skill: wins, win rates and trophies won are
not weighed, and inactivity is measured from battles played, never from
time online. Standing appears once the clan has a policy and at least
10 members.

## What a clan can count

| Category | Read as | Over |
|---|---|---|
| Clan Wars | war decks played out of the decks asked (four a war day up to the clan's finish, all four days in a Colosseum week) | the last finished war weeks, 1 to 8 |
| Ranked play | Path of Legends battles played; the league reached does not count | the last whole weeks, 1 to 8 |
| Donations | cards donated, as a weekly average | the last weeks, 1 to 8 |
| Trophy road | trophies today | no window: the record keeps no trophy history |

Each category is off until the clan switches it on, and each clan picks
its own windows. Eight weeks is the most, because Clan reads eight weeks
of the clan's record. Where the record cannot say, the answer is
unknown, never a miss.

## Minimums

A clan can set a minimum in any category it counts (war decks, ranked
battles, weekly donations, trophies), over a window of its own, and say
whether a member must meet any one of them or all of them. Minimums are
what a member needs to be promoted, what an Elder needs to keep the
role, and, when the clan allows it, what earns extra time before the
inactivity clock runs.

## How Elder is earned

The policy says how Elders are chosen: **by the leaders, by hand**,
which is where every clan starts, or **by participation in what the
clan counts**. By hand, Clan raises no promotions or demotions and
draws no band.

By participation, each category the clan counts gets a weight, and the
weights are relative (60, 20 and 20 are three fifths, one fifth and one
fifth). Each member who took part is placed against the others in each
category, and the weighted places make one score. Leaders and
co-leaders are never ranked. Time in the clan is a gate and a
tiebreak, never a way to earn Elder on its own.

## The Elder band

The band is how many Elders the clan wants, as a share of the roster: a
lower and an upper share, with the target between them. It is a limit
on drift, not a quota: nobody is promoted to fill it. A member can be
promoted when they meet the minimums, have been in the clan long enough,
score at or above the clan's floor, and rank inside the target.

An Elder who ranks below the band's upper line is outranked. A member
above them who scores higher by at least the clan's margin can take
their place; on a closer call, the one longer in the clan does. An
Elder can be demoted for two reasons: missing the minimums, or being
outranked.

## A change has to hold

Clan judges at each review: the end of each war week when Clan Wars
carries weight in Elder, the end of each calendar week otherwise. A promotion
is raised only after several qualifying reviews (one missed review is
forgiven; two in a row start the count again), and a demotion only
after consecutive reviews. After a leader declines one, the same kind
waits before it comes back. The counts and waits are the clan's own.
When the evidence is unknown, nothing is raised.

Clan never changes a role. A promotion or demotion is an
[action](/docs/clan-actions) for the leaders, who make the change in
the game.

## What everyone sees

Once the clan has a policy, every member's **Standing** page shows:

- **How it works here**: what the clan counts and the weights, the
  minimums, how Elder works and how big the band is, the inactivity
  rules, and what newcomers can expect.
- **Their own line**: where they stand, the evidence in a player's
  terms ("100% war decks over 4 war weeks, 12 ranked battles, ~213
  donations a week"), their war decks race by race when the clan counts
  war, what would move them, the days since their last battle, and any
  hold a leader has set.

When the clan ranks Elder and the policy lets members see where everyone
stands (on unless the leaders turn it off), the page also lists every
member and Elder in one of five groups, each with their own evidence
and, when the clan counts war, their war decks in each race of the
clan's war window, out of the decks asked:

| Group | Who |
|---|---|
| Holding Elder | an Elder with no demotion building |
| Slipping | an Elder whose demotion is building |
| Rising | a member whose promotion is building |
| Participating | a member meeting the minimums |
| Quiet | a member missing them |

Leaders and co-leaders always see the groups when Elder is ranked.
Nobody sees a score, a percentile or a rank; the leaders' board shows
the band's size and how full it is.

**Nobody below co-leader ever sees a removal action or who is on the
inactivity clock.** A member sees only their own days since a battle.

## When it is read

Clan reads every clan with a policy each morning from 11:00 UTC, and
again when someone opens Standing, Actions, Trophies or a leaders'
page, at most every five minutes. A reading can raise or withdraw
[actions](/docs/clan-actions). A new version of the policy is read
afresh.
