# Exceptions

An exception tells the rules to skip one thing for one member. The
Leader and Co-Leaders set them under **Run › Exceptions**, or from a
member's **Notes and exceptions**.

## The kinds

- **Keep in clan.** No kick. For someone who's away for a while, like a
  trip or exams. The days without a battle keep counting; the rules just
  won't suggest removing them while it lasts.
- **Keep as Elder.** No demotion. For an Elder who helps the clan in ways
  the numbers miss. A kept Elder doesn't take one of the earned Elder
  seats, doesn't block anyone's promotion and isn't ranked for demotion.
  Elders only.
- **Not for Elder.** No promotion. For a second account, or someone who
  said no thanks. They aren't ranked for Elder, so they push nobody out of
  line. Members only.
- **Away.** No kick. A member marks themselves away under **Away**, up to
  the number of days the clan's rules allow. A leader can also mark a
  member away, up to a year. Away is always one the whole clan can see.
  An Away a leader set is the leader's to change.

A member can have more than one exception, but only one of each kind at
a time. Setting a kind that is already running changes it.

## How long

Every exception has an end date: 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months, or
a date you pick, up to a year away. When it ends, the rules treat the
member like everyone else again. To keep it going, change it and pick a
new end. A leader can end one early.

## Who can see it

Each exception is **Leaders only** or **The whole clan**. A leaders-only
exception is seen by the Leader and Co-Leaders alone, not even by the
member it is about. One the whole clan can see shows beside the member's
name on **Members**, and to the member on their own page. Members see the
kind and the end date, never the why or who set it.

No exception reaches the clan's public listing, the directory or any MCP
tool.

## What the rules leave alone by themselves

Some members need no exception. **Run › Exceptions** lists them under
**Automatic**:

- **The Leader and Co-Leaders.** No kick, no demotion, not ranked for
  Elder. Leaders are never judged by the rules, so no exception is set on
  them.
- **Elders**, unless the clan's rules include Elders in kicks
  ([the inactivity clock](/docs/inactivity-clock)).
- **Members who marked themselves away**, until they're back.

## When an exception counts

The rules look back over several weekly reviews before they suggest a
change ([Standing](/docs/standing#a-change-has-to-hold)). An exception
counts only at the reviews it was running at, so one set today doesn't
rewrite last month.

## History

Every exception is kept after it ends. Each one records who set it and
when, its end date, the why and who could see it, and every change:
added, changed, ended early, or ran out. **Run › Decided** lists the
latest, one line each, like "Keep as Elder added by Ada".
