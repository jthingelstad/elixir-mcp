# Cards

## Cards

**Cards**, at `/home/you/stats/cards`, answers one question: the cards
you played, and the cards you faced, in one mode this season. It has the
same chips as every tab, and each list names its mode and how many battles
it read ("35 Trophy Road battles"), so no list reads as all your games.
Other reads Challenge and Tournament apart, each with its own lists.

An evolution or a hero is a different card from its base form, so each
keeps its own row, as the tools keep it. Each row is the card's art, its
name, the battles and the share you won, and your record on the right.
Tap a row to open the card's public page, which holds its catalog facts:
rarity, elixir cost and forms. A tower troop has no page.

- **Cards you played**: from
  [battles_cards](/docs/tools/battles#battles_cards) with
  `perspective: mine`: for each card and form, the battles where your
  deck held it and your record in them. A card in more than one deck
  counts all their battles, so the rows overlap and never add up.
- **Cards you faced**: the same tool with `perspective: opponent`: each
  card your opponents played, how many battles you faced it in, and your
  record in those battles.

Both lists read most battles first, the tools' own order, and show twelve
rows until you tap **Show all**. The tool leaves out cards in fewer than
three battles, and the page says so.
